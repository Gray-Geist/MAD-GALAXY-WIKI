# Jargon & Slang

*A living glossary of the language of the Antevic Sector — from the bridge to the gutter.*

---

## Directions & Navigation

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Coreward** | Moving toward the core of the Milky Way galaxy |
| **Edgeward** | Moving toward the Edge of the Sector |
| **Spinward** | Traveling with the galaxy's clockwise spin (left-ish on the map) |
| **Trailward** | Opposite of spinward — counter-clockwise |
| **North/Up** | Toward the top of the map (+Z coordinate) |
| **South/Down** | Toward the bottom of the map (-Z coordinate) |
| **Topside** | Pulling up or down in a system to get a better overview |
