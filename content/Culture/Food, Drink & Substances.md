# Food, Drink & Substances

*What people eat, drink, and consume tells you who they are.*

---

## Common Foods

**Kibble:** Cheaply produced food resembling dry dog food — rock-bottom nutrition for the very poor.

**SCOP:** "Single Cell Organic Protein." Makes up 20% of all food items. Also used as a derogatory term for something low quality or a person of low regard ("scopsucking", "scophead").

**Phase Crunches:** Crunchy SCOP fry-sticks. Flavors: Void, Solar, Blackhole (increasing spice intensity). Almost never expire.

**Pep O+:** Sweetened carbonated drink — roughly 2.5x the caf content of a normal cup.

---

## Cultural Cuisine

**Sinasian Cuisine:** Curries, rices, and various meats. Known for **Ul'bada** — fermented milk and honey macerated with dates, mint leaves, and seasonal berries.

**Ul'Rekar Cuisine:** Strikingly similar profile to Sinasian cuisine — curries, rices, and various meats, also centered around **Ul'bada**. Whether this represents cultural exchange or common origin is debated.

## Tobacco & Smoking

**Synthacco / Synthcigs™:** The most commonly used tobacco product in the Sector (70% market share). First "nano-dipped" cigs — self-lighting via ignition-nanos. Ranges from "Lows" to "Mids" to "Highs." Does not age. Also available in chewable form. *Produced by [[BioMega]].*

**Orbital Red:** Second most common. Genetically modified space station plants. Potency improves with age; fresh batches are harsh.

**Asteroid Baccy:** Cultivated on asteroid mining colonies. Robust, earthy, metallic undertones.

**Eclipse Frost:** Very strong menthol blend, popular among augmented humans.

**Nexus Spice:** Synthetic blend for advanced vaping devices. Exotic spices and aromatics.

**N Leaf:** High-tech smoking substitute. Vapor infused with stimulating nanobots. Mostly used by the wealthy and cybernetically enhanced.

**Mousaan:** Light psychoactive. Effects include relaxation, euphoria, altered time perception, increased heart rate, dry mouth, red eyes, and impaired coordination and memory.

**Solari Gold:** Premium blend from rare interstellar flora. Prized among spacefarers and traders.

**Space Shag ("Pirate's Shag"):** Harvested from the depths of space-faring vessels. Very potent.

**Under Tobac:** Grown below a planet's surface. Intense focus after use. **Extremely addictive.**

## Liquor

**Groja:** Fermented from algae and sea plants. Cheap and often homemade. **Gr'Aja** (from [[Olodumare System]]) is the largest groja brand in the galaxy.

**Saccaharum:** Distilled from sugar grown on Orisha Oko's tropical land. Unaged = clear. Cerasus pine barrels = bright red and sweet; green erable barrels = bitter yellowish spirit.

**Helios Gin:** From glowing flora of Helios Prime. Considered a transcendent experience.

**Luneshine:** Potent moonshine brewed in clandestine labs. Popular: Luneshine Lyte and Luneshine Cyber.

**Nebulactar:** Distilled from nebula gaseous clouds. Ethereal flavors.

**Rypian Brandy:** Ultra-elite. Found in cantinas across the Sector.

**Synthspirits™:** Molecularly engineered compounds. Customizable from smooth to fiery. *Produced by [[BioMega]].*

## Illicit Substances

**Amphekain *(“Uncle Cain”)*:** A synthetic stimulant whose formula is now generic and widely available, though that does not mean it's safe. Clears psychological stress entirely and pushes physical and cognitive performance above baseline. The drug compels action; it cannot be sat on. Cardiovascular crash on wear-off. See [[Medical & Trauma Care]].

**'Dorph *(Synthetic Endorphin)*:** Powerful painkiller and combat drug. Reduces pain, stun, and fatigue. Risk: permanent nervous system damage — loss of coordination, reflexes, and dexterity. See [[Medical & Trauma Care]].

**Redline *("Lace")*:** Military-grade variant of 'Dorph. Euphoria and adrenaline rush. Prolonged use: lost sense of self as human; addicts begin viewing others as weak and inferior. See [[Medical & Trauma Care]].

**Synthcoke:** Highly addictive stimulant. Euphoric high, heightened senses, clarity. A favorite among corpos.

**H8 ("Hate"):** Fine red powder. Produces violent rages. Extremely cheap, extremely effective, extremely addictive. Found in slums and flophouses.

**Marder Resin:** Chewed sweet substance from the Marder tree. Causes euphoria then deliriousness and loss of feeling (a formidable painkiller in refined doses). Extremely addictive. Addicts ("sweet eaters") identifiable by their extremely white teeth — a side effect.

**Black Lotus:** Made from narcotic leaves. The smell is always uniquely attractive to the user — sweet, citrus, or fruity.

**Complidon:** Known as "compliance in a syringe" — a banned interrogation drug used to break down resistance and destroy short-term memory. See [[Medical & Trauma Care]].

**Gilamine:** A powerful narcotic derived from the resin of the gilam plant. Smoked, dissolved, or pressed into tabs. Produces a dissociative euphoria — users describe the sensation as existing slightly outside their own body, watching events with warm detachment. The come-down is punishing: hours of grey exhaustion and emotional flatness that drives most users straight back to the next dose. Widely distributed on the black market; cheap enough that it has eaten entire districts in frontier settlements.

**Ganjir:** A mild narcotic derived from native plant species found across multiple colonized systems. The fruiting body is ground into paste, smoked, or brewed as tea depending on local tradition. Reduces stress and produces a state of relaxed detachment. Easy to cultivate — hydroponics setups on even modest colony ships can sustain a supply. Illegal across most of the Sector, common everywhere regardless. One of the oldest substances in continuous circulation.

**Illegal Opioids:** A catch-all designation for the homebrewed synthetic opioid compounds that circulate through the Sector's grey and black markets. Cancels panic responses and dramatically blunts psychological stress — cheap, effective, and utterly unreliable in composition. The batch you buy today is not the batch you bought last week. Dependency forms fast. The only consistent fact about Illegal Opioids is that you never really know what you're buying.
