# Death Rites

*How the civilizations of the Antevic Sector honor the dead reveals much about how they believe the living should conduct themselves.*

---

## Republic *(Pentarchy-Influenced)*

### Space Rites
**Celestial Lanterns:** The body is cremated and ashes placed in transparent capsules with ionized gases. These "lanterns" are released into space, glowing in colors chosen by the deceased's loved ones or compatriots. Believed to find and converge with God — aligning with the First Promised Truth of the Pentarchy.

**Vigilant Star Rites:** The body is atomized; each atom is encoded with prayers before being scattered into space as "Vigilant Stars." Each particle serves as an eternal prayer, with the hope that God will hear them across the universe — linked to the Third Promised Truth: *"God is in the sound."*

### Ground Rites
**The Star's Rest:** Ashes are mixed into a nutrient medium and used to cultivate a flowering plant in a memorial garden. Families visit and care for the deceased's plant, representing God's promise of eternal life in His love.

**Truth Stones:** Ashes are molded into small ceramic stones engraved with a Pentarchal prayer and placed along walkways in the Republic's gardens of remembrance. Relatives visit to lay fresh flowers or candles beside the stones.

## Coalition

### Space Rites
**Lightstone Sacrifice *(Pentarchy-Influenced)*:** Ashes pressed into small luminescent stones, dispersed in space in clusters. The cold of space causes them to react and form a faint constellation — the soul's journey to God's light, per the Pentarchy's Second Truth.

**Gostic Will:** Bodies reduced to particulate dust, bound within a strong transparent casing marked with Helgoth sigils and launched toward the nearest asteroid or celestial body. These resting places act as eternal markers of Gostic resilience — waypoints for future Gostic travelers and symbols of their "iron" will.

**Void Echo Rites:** A ceremonial dissolution of the body into atoms, each particle encoded with a unique Helgothic frequency. Released slowly into space — the "Void Echo" is believed to amplify the willpower and soul of the Helgost, merging it with the energy of the universe to strengthen future generations.

### Ground Rites
**Helgoth Ironstone Burial:** Ashes mixed with powdered iron and set into carved stones bearing the sigils of the deceased's lineage and deeds. Placed in Helgoth's mountainous memorial fields, enduring the harsh climate as a testament to Helgost culture — believed to guide younger generations.

**Banner of Ancestry:** Ashes woven into synthetic fibers that become part of a family's "Ancestral Banner" — a fabric woven from the remains of family members across generations. Displayed only in family homes or honored shrines. A cultural artifact reminding each generation of their bloodline's resilience.

**Arkadian Remembrance Vial:** Ashes placed into small glass vials carried by the next of kin. Worn for one year to honor the departed, then returned to the soil in a private ceremony.

## Ul'Rekar Hierocracy

### Space Rites
**Chariot of Stars:** The deceased is wrapped in an elaborate shroud woven with cosmic symbols. A ceremonial chant calls upon the Netjer to receive the spirit. The body is cast into space with a cluster of bioluminescent-infused reflective stones that glow faintly as they drift — forming a "chariot" formation that carries the spirit through the stars, honoring the Ogdoad's eternal cycle.

**Fire of the Netjer:** The body is cremated in a sacred containment pod, capturing ashes condensed into a small dark orb — an "Ember of the Netjer." After ritual chants, the orb is released into space. A chemical layer reacts to the vacuum, causing a brief radiant flash. This brilliant, short-lived fire symbolizes the spirit's reunion with the Netjer.

### Ground Rites
**Tomb of the Veiled Mirror:** The deceased is interred within a burial chamber with walls plated in mirrored metal reflecting the face of the Netjer — symbolizing the infinite realms within the Strands. Located in consecrated caves. Each tomb is sealed with a "Strand Stone" — a dark polished crystal believed to resonate with the Strand's energy.

**Ritual of the Nine Eyes:** The body is mummified and adorned with nine ritualistic eye-symbol amulets, each representing the Netjer's gaze upon the soul through a different realm of existence. The body is buried with ceremonial weapons and a carved statuette of the Netjer. *This rite is exclusive to Ul'Rekar leaders.*

## Merovinist Triumvirate

### Space Rites
**Light's Passage *(Ul'Rekar Influence)*:** The body is flash-frozen; metal filigree is etched on exposed skin with Ul'Rekar symbols and released into the void — honoring the Netjer's "embrace" even beyond Merovinist borders.

**Pentarchal Incandescence *(Pentarchy Influence)*:** Using the Pentarchy's Fifth Truth, bodies are converted to energy in a reactor core, fueling the ship. Each life becomes energy — symbolizing humanity's endurance and enlightenment.

### Ground Rites
**Mantle of the Enduring Line:** Ashes pressed into crystal shards embedded in the family's "Mantle of Lineage" — a ceremonial cloak passed down through generations. Each shard is inscribed with the deceased's name and accomplishments. Worn only by the family elder at significant gatherings.

**Circle of Continuance:** Ashes mixed into a binding agent and crafted into a small clay tablet marked with the deceased's role or achievements. Placed in a "Circle of Continuance" within communal halls — each tablet a visible link in an unbroken chain of Merovinist heritage.
