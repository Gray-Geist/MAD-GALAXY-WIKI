# Culture

What people eat, how they bury their dead, the slang they use in dark cantinas and on military comms — these are the textures of a civilization. The Antevic Sector spans star nations and centuries, but certain threads run through all of human culture: the need to make sense of death, the comfort of shared food and drink, and the living language that marks who belongs and who doesn't.
