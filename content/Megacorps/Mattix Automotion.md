# Mattix Automotion

**Industry:** On & Off-World Vehicle Manufacturing · After-Market Parts  
**CEO:** Jace Tycen-Mattix  
**Headquarters:** Mattix Center, Lux Aureum (Arkadien system)  
**Presence:** Primarily Coalition space

![[mattix_art.png]]
---

## Overview

Once an industry leader in space travel and vehicle transportation, **Mattix Automotion** has experienced a significant fall from its former heights. Now a shadow of its former self, the company has found surprising new life through aftermarket parts and the enduring reputation of its most reliable vehicle.

---

## History

Headquartered in **Mattix Center** — an industrial district on [[Arkadien System#Lux Aureum|Lux Aureum]] founded by the once-rich and powerful Mattix Family — the corporation has been hollowed out by competition over generations. Multiple acquisition approaches from [[Neus Kapitel Group (NKG)]] and others have been firmly rebuffed by the Tycen-Mattix family, who have maintained control of their company.

---

## Recent Resurgence

Under **Jace Tycen-Mattix** — a distant grandson of the original founder — the company has taken a methodical approach to revival:

- **Aftermarket parts**: Mattix has found success producing cheap, reliable aftermarket parts for a wide range of vehicles beyond their own brand. Smugglers, traders, and settlement specialists swear by Mattix for **reliability, durability, and repairability**
- **The P1-J9 Corvette (the "Pigeon")**: Their best-selling vehicle remains the Prototype 1-J9 Class — beloved primarily because it rarely breaks down. A large order for the Pigeon has recently been placed
- **Factory reopening**: One of their largest manufacturing plants has recently been brought back online, providing a rare glimmer of hope for Lux Aureum's struggling population

---

## Leadership Philosophy

Jace Tycen-Mattix has deliberately avoided the corporate warfare and political drama that consumed his predecessors. His philosophy: build reliable vehicles, be a trustworthy company, and let rapid expansion happen only when the foundation is stable.

He leads by example — often found on the factory floor alongside his employees — and is gaining a reputation as one of the better business leaders in Coalition space.
