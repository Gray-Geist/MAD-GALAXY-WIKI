# E-Con

**Industry:** Chained Eidolon Reaction Drives · Hover Technology  
**CEO:** Hectir Attioch  
**Headquarters:** Eidolon City, Alpha Terra, Primus system  
**Presence:** Core, Mid-core, and Edge worlds

![[e-con_art.png]]
---

## Overview

**E-Con** is the oldest surviving megacorporation in the known galaxy — a remarkable testament to its resilience and adaptability. It has absorbed punches that would destroy lesser corporations, getting back up each time and often acquiring the very competition that knocked it down.

---

## History

Headquartered in **Eidolon City** on **Alpha Terra** in the Primus system, E-Con's leaders recognized the writing on the wall long before Alpha Terra began its economic decline — and shifted their E-drive and Hover Technology production to other systems in the Republic decades before the collapse.

This foresight is characteristic of an organization that has demonstrated an extraordinary ability to survive across centuries of civilizational upheaval.

---

## Products

E-Con specializes in:
- **[[Eidolon Technology|Chained Eidolon Reaction Drives]]** — the skip-drives that power interstellar travel
- **Hover Technology** — on-world and off-world vehicle propulsion systems
