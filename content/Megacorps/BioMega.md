# BioMega

**Industry:** Genetic Engineering · Microbiological & Biochemical Research · Pharmaceuticals · Psychoactive Substances  
**CEO:** Sarida Edgars  
**Presence:** Core, Mid-core, and Edge worlds

![[biomega_art.png]]

---

## Overview

**BioMega** is a privately held, Coalition-originated corporation within the **Edgars family** — claiming to trace its founding to the original colony ships from Old Earth, potentially making it the **oldest megacorporation in existence**.

---

## History & Signature Technologies

During the Exploration era, BioMega introduced the first-ever **Womb Genesis nanotechnology**, later perfected, which contributed directly to a massive population boom and eventually the era of overpopulation known as **"the Squeeze."** The Womb Genesis technology remains BioMega's most closely guarded patent — reportedly held on physical holo-paper only, each part stored in different high-security locations.

Today, corporate espionage estimates place **pharmaceuticals at roughly 40% of revenue**. BioMega holds a virtual chokehold on anti-addiction and therapy medications.

### Consumer Products
- **Synthcigs™** — the Sector's most commonly used tobacco product, made from patented **Synthacco**
- **Synthspirits™** — a synthetic concoction from molecularly engineered compounds offering a customizable, affordable drinking experience

---

## Weapons & Black Projects

BioMega holds a significant chemical and biological weapons program with contracts across several governments.

Under CEO Sarida Edgars, the company has moved into morally questionable and outright dark territory:
- A plan to **suppress poly-psykers** via engineered virus, alongside development of a second virus to *reveal* them
- Funding of multiple "black projects"
- A **Life Extension research program** that extends the life of patients — always at a significant price

Although not associated with paramilitary activity, due to the worldwide importance of its developments, BioMega has a fair amount of power in the political community, and it can usually obtain whatever military equipment it needs — although some items may take more time to procure than others.

---

## Rivals

BioMega's dominant pharmaceutical position has drawn it into direct conflict with [[GaiaTech]], which has massively invested in vaccines and future medicine, threatening BioMega's market share.
