# Nutrionix

**Industry:** Food Processing · Agricultural · Livestock  
**CEO:** Sabine Tronstrad  
**Headquarters:** Solarus Station, Olodumare system  
**Presence:** Core, Mid-core, and Edge worlds
![[Nutrionix_art.png]]
---

## Overview

**Nutrionix** is an agricultural and food processing company with proprietary ownership of a new-age food processor that removes the most harmful parts of reconstituted meat while mixing in spices. Their wildly successful marketing campaign — **Process It!** — demonstrated the technology's uses and cemented their brand across the Sector.

They control **large swathes of Antevic agricultural land** and hold a controlling stake in the most fertile land on **Erinle** — the breadbasket planet.
