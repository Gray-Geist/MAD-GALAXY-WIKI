# Arienai Corporation

**Industry:** Banking (Corporate, Personal >100Bcr) · Colonial Activities · Manufacturing (Weapons, Cyberware, General) · Security (PMC, Police, CSEC)
**CEO:** Nakano Hiro
**Presence:** Core, Mid-core, and Edge worlds

![[arienai_art.png]]

---

## Overview

One of the most influential megacorporations in the Sector, **Arienai** maintains the **largest and most powerful armed forces of any corporation** in the Antevic Sector. Its weapons and military vehicles are among the most sought-after by police and security firms. Most firms will look to licensing from Arienai corporate security, couriers, or PMCs to protect their assets. However, Arienai assets take priority if they are at risk and the security forces themselves are loyal to death to the Japanese-originated conglomerate.

Arienai has a dark reputation — a corporation to be feared, one that covers its tracks through assassins, an army of lawyers, and the leverage of loyal PMCs.

---

## History & Background

A Japanese-originated conglomerate, Arienai has made a habit of buying up other companies, bleeding out competition, and using legally gray but cutthroat tactics to get footholds in difficult markets. They also give a heavy amount of support to Arienai-originated colonies or communities all over the Sector, despite their business practices and overall history being considered unethical.

The corporation has many enemies, who range from:
- Corporations that compete directly with the company or have clashed with it (such as the proxy-wars fought against **[[Atheon Heavy Industries]]**, their primary corporate rival)
- Powerful individuals who suspect that their blackmailers have some relation to Arienai
- Law enforcement officers from across the Sector who suspect the extent of the illegal operations of Arienai

---

## Corporate Rivalry

Arienai and [[Atheon Heavy Industries]] are the two largest paramilitary corporations in the Sector and have been rivals for many years. Through mutual sabotage and clandestine black operations, tensions have escalated into an **open corporate proxy war**.

---

## Name Origin

*Arienai* — Translated as "impossible" from old Japanese to standard. It brings a feeling of "I don't believe this can happen." The dying father of the founder of the Arienai Corporation uttered this single word to his son, who took it as a challenge.
