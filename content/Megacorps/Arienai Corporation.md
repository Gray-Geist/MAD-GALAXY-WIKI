# Arienai Corporation

**Industry:** Banking · Colonial Activities · Manufacturing (Weapons, Cyberware) · Security (PMC, Police, CSEC)  
**CEO:** Nakano Hiro  
**Presence:** Core, Mid-core, and Edge worlds

![[arienai_art.png]]

---

## Overview

One of the most influential megacorporations in the Sector, **Arienai** maintains the **largest and most powerful armed forces of any corporation** in the Antevic Sector. Its weapons and military vehicles are among the most sought-after by police and security firms. Most firms look to Arienai for licensed corporate security, couriers, or PMC protection.

Arienai has a dark reputation — a corporation to be feared, one that covers its tracks through assassins, an army of lawyers, and the leverage of loyal PMCs.

---

## History & Background

A Japanese-originated conglomerate, Arienai has built a habit of acquiring other companies, bleeding out competition, and using legally gray but cutthroat tactics to gain footholds in difficult markets. It also provides heavy support to Arienai-originated colonies and communities across the Sector, despite its overall history of unethical business practices.

The corporation has many enemies:
- Corporations that have directly competed with or clashed with Arienai (including those caught in the proxy wars against **[[Atheon Heavy Industries]]**, their primary corporate rival)
- Powerful individuals who suspect Arienai connections to their blackmailers
- Law enforcement officers from across the Sector who suspect the extent of Arienai's illegal operations

---

## Corporate Rivalry

Arienai and [[Atheon Heavy Industries]] are the two largest paramilitary corporations in the Sector and have been rivals for many years. Through mutual sabotage and clandestine black operations, tensions have escalated into an **open corporate proxy war**.
