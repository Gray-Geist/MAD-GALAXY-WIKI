## Overview

**Umbra Shipyards** are traditional builders of many types of star vessels and craft — one of the oldest and most established names in ship manufacturing across the Antevic Sector. Where [[Fabrique Orbital]] pushes design boundaries, Umbra is known for reliability, heritage, and proven hull configurations that have carried humanity through wars and exploration alike.
