# Atheon Heavy Industries

**Industry:** Artificial Intelligence · Manufacturing · Non-Human Engineering · Robotics  
**CEO:** Jethro Zachta  
**Headquarters:** Aquila, Praetarus system (Republic city-planet)  
**Presence:** Core, Mid-core, and Edge worlds

![[atheonheavy_art.jpg]]
---

## Overview

**Atheon Heavy Industries** is the Sector's premier AI, robotics, cybernetics, and non-human engineering conglomerate. It operates under heavy Republic patronage — the Republic invests massively into Atheon, effectively granting it **carte blanche** to operate where other businesses would receive sanctions.

Controlling and monitoring Aquila's enormous population is virtually impossible without Atheon's technical support. On Aquila, a citizen is more likely to be fined or arrested by a **weaponized bot** than an actual human officer.

---

## Legal Protections & Illegal Operations

Law enforcement agencies attempting to scrutinize Atheon are systematically contained — not only through eliminating their agents, but through Atheon's remarkable skill at concealing conflicts of interest and illegal activities. The corporation is headquartered on the same world it effectively controls.

---

## Corporate Rivalry

Atheon and [[Arienai Corporation]] are the two largest paramilitary corporations in the Sector. Their rivalry has escalated through years of mutual sabotage and clandestine black operations into an **open corporate proxy war**, with each seizing every opportunity to whittle down the other's resources.
