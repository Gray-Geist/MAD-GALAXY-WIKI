# Neus Kapitel Group (NKG)

**Industry:** Banking · Colonial Activities · Manufacturing (Military Cyberware, Military Vehicles, Ships, Weapons) · Security (PMC, Police, CSEC)  
**CEO:** Jorhan Stur  
**Headquarters:** Nagindr, Helgoth, Baadensenar system  
**Presence:** Core, Mid-core, and Edge worlds

---

## Overview

**Neus Kapitel Group** is a megacorporation forged in the crucible of the Savage Wars and one of the Sector's largest manufacturers of weapons and military vehicles. Its name — *Neus Kapitel* — is derived from an old Earth tongue meaning **"New Chapter"**: the corporation was built to bring a new chapter to those who fled during and after the Disaster at Sot Prime.

---

## Products & Services

NKG provides equipment to numerous governments, private organizations, and governmental bodies — including the **Helgost Army**, police forces, and **Falkcast Navy**. Products include:
- Personal firearms
- Drones
- Tanks
- Aircraft
- Ships
- Assisted Combat Personal Armor (**ACPA**)

An alternate corporate branch provides **private military forces** nominally for defense, peacekeeping, and nation-building — but these forces have a documented history of being used for more unsavory purposes.

---

## Reputation

NKG is frequently accused — *"by its enemies with reason to lie"* — of involvement in:
- Ethically shady or outright illegal black operations
- Supporting revolutions and military coups
- Assassinations
- Terrorist attacks
- Ethnic cleansings

The Republic of Man's government has been repeatedly irritated by NKG's supply relationship with the Coalition. Intense lobbying by NKG has successfully stalled Republic legislation against them.

---

## Rivalry with Arienai

Though both operate in weapons manufacturing and PMC space, NKG and [[Arienai Corporation]] have distinct cultural and geographic bases. Multiple approaches to acquire [[Mattix Automotion]] have been resisted by the Tycen-Mattix family.
