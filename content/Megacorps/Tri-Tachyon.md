# Tri-Tachyon

**Industry:** Electronics · Communications · Manufacturing (Semiconductors, Eidolon Computers, Strandlock Encoders)  
**CEO:** Unknown  
**Presence:** Core, Mid-core

---

## Overview

Once the most powerful megacorporation in the Sector — before and partly during the Savage Wars — **Tri-Tachyon** specialized in cutting-edge AI development and exotic high-power devices: starship engines, skip drives, power generators, energy converters, force fields. Several notable cases of violating the Astral Alliance ban on autonomous Alpha+ level AI were tied directly to the corporation.

As the superpowers formed post-war, they made an example of Tri-Tachyon — breaking up and severely limiting the corporation to send a clear message to the myriad megacorps jostling for power. Since then, Tri-Tachyon has vastly diversified its operations and insinuated itself throughout the known galaxy. Even hostile factions paranoid of hardwired backdoors find themselves forced to employ standard Tri-Tachyon devices — beginning with the ubiquitous **Tri-Tachyon personal smart devices**: the hand terminal, board terminal, and battleboard.

---

## Corporate Culture

Tri-Tachyon profiles as arrogant scientist/corporate types. They see themselves as **keepers of knowledge** — heirs to the technological marvels created since defeating the Decline. Obsessed with efficiency and the acquisition of remaining Sector technology, they condemn those who shun them.

- **At their best:** Competent, effective, and cooperative with one another
- **At their worst:** Detached, elitist, and cruel

Their central belief: the occult Savage technology was merely used in the wrong way. If they could unlock it, they could restore humanity's lost time during the Decline and bring it technological wealth. **Secret tech-mining operations** run by the Corporation can be found in the farthest reaches of the Sector.

---

## The AI Question

From a certain perspective, Tri-Tachyon's corporate charter contains peculiar **algorithmic self-modifying features** that might be considered an abstract form of AI — one that uses human corporate officers as integral components. This philosophical argument was never satisfactorily resolved in a way that led to additional convictions for violating the AI ban.
