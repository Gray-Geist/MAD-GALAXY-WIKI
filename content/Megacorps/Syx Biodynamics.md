# Syx Biodynamics

**Industry:** Fashionware · Bioware · Biosculpting · Genetic Engineering  
**CEO:** Adric Carthen  
**Presence:** Core, Mid-core

---

## Overview

> *"Humanity is driven by five genetic needs: survival, love and belonging, power, freedom, and fun. We provide it all."* — Adric Carthen, CEO

**Syx Biodynamics** operates at the intersection of identity, vanity, and biology — offering body modification, genetic tailoring, and biosculpting services to those who can afford to reshape themselves at the cellular level. In a Sector where cyberware is commonplace, Syx competes by going deeper: not metal under skin, but rewritten biology itself.

Their product philosophy is simple and ruthless: the body is a starting point, not a destination. Where cybernetics bolt hardware onto a person, Syx rewrites the person. Grafted musculature grown in their vat labs is indistinguishable from what the body grew naturally. Woven skin resists damage without a visible seam. The customer walks out looking — and feeling — entirely human, only better. That invisible quality premium is what Syx charges for, and what keeps their clinics booked years in advance on every core world they operate on.

---

## Market Position

Syx is the dominant civilian bioware provider in the Antevic Sector. Their licensed clinics — ranging from gleaming core-world medical centers to cramped but certified mid-core facilities — represent the primary legal channel through which most citizens access enhancement bioware. Black market Syx product exists at the edges, usually sourced from unlicensed practitioners running gray-market vat equipment, quality varying sharply.

The company's civilian dominance does not mean military irrelevance. Several star nation procurement offices hold standing contracts with Syx for enhancement bioware in specialist roles — units where invisibility of augmentation matters as much as the augmentation itself. Syx bioware does not announce itself on a scanner. That alone makes it valuable to forces that operate where being obviously augmented is a liability.

Their market position generates constant friction with [[BioMega]], whose pharmaceutical dominance and black-project biological programs have repeatedly brought the two companies into conflict over IP, talent, and territory. BioMega views Syx's civilian goodwill as a market threat; Syx views BioMega's ethics as a business risk that occasionally becomes a regulatory crisis. The rivalry is cold and continuous.

---

## Products & Services

Syx's commercial catalog covers the full range of civilian bioware enhancement — vat-grown muscle grafts, woven dermal reinforcement, and biosculpting services from the cosmetic to the dramatic. Exotics — individuals who have undergone extensive biosculpting — are disproportionately Syx clients; the company pioneered many of the techniques that make large-scale biological reshaping survivable and predictable.

Their flagship enhancement lines are sold under proprietary brand names and backed by post-procedure support contracts that are, depending on who you ask, either excellent value or a necessary subscription to keep your own biology from rejecting its upgrades.
