# Cadre Galactic

**Industry:** Space Exploration · Space Survey · Archaeological & Historical Analysis  
**CEO:** TBD  
**Presence:** Core, Mid-core

---

## Overview

An eclectic mix of highly accomplished adventurer historians, galactic romantics, ex-administrators, admirals, and board members of some of the Sector's larger salvaging companies — all unified under a single corporation that is, outwardly at least, **dedicated to the preservation of galactic knowledge and galactic culture**.

Using a mix of low-tech and midline ships backed by an impressive collection of techmined blueprints, **Cadre Galactic** provides modest support to the Coalition while retaining the bulk of their forces for independent exploration into the Sector — discovering ruins and protecting them from the grasp of independent salvagers, rival corporations, and anyone else who might plunder what they consider their domain.

Whether their preservation mandate is genuine altruism or elaborate monopolization of galactic history is a question the Sector has never satisfactorily answered.
