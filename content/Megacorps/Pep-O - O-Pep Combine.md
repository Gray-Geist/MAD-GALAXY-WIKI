# Pep-O / O-Pep Combine

**Industry:** Food Processing · Agricultural · Livestock  
**CEO:** Koric Tragion  
**Presence:** Core, Mid-core, and Edge worlds
![[Pep-O_O-Pep_Combine_art.png]]
---

## Overview

The **Pep-O / O-Pep Combine** was formed by the merger of two bitter food empire rivals — resulting in the **longest-running legal battle in history** over the resulting company name, currently entering its **8th decade**.

---

## The Name War

Two years ago, a momentous legal breakthrough was reached: legal teams from both sides mutually agreed to stop the practice of **drone-enforced lawsuits against private citizens** using the preferred terminology of the opposing brand — so long as those citizens immediately follow that terminology with the opposing brand identity. As a result, the corporation's towering headquarters now displays an **ever-rotating hologram** cycling between both names.

---

## Products & Reputation

Despite its legal absurdities, the combine is a genuine industry leader in:
- Innovative healthy superfood and drink production
- Gene-engineered crops and animals raised in expensive **space installations**
- Products marketed to the ultra-wealthy at exorbitant prices for supposed health benefits of **space-grown food**

It is rumored they also offer other **"experimental" delicacies** to the uber rich — details unconfirmed.
