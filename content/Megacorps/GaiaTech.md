# GaiaTech

**Industry:** Genetic Engineering · Microbiological & Biochemical Research · Pharmaceutical  
**CEO:** Cosma Gadro  
**Presence:** Core, Mid-core, and Edge worlds

![[gaiatech_art.jpg]]
---

## Overview

**GaiaTech** has earned a more positive public reputation than most megacorporations by focusing on environmental restoration, food security, and medicine. Their labs have enabled much of the Republic to develop new bio-engineered "replacement species," and they are active in restoring both animals and habitat throughout many blighted environments.

---

## Environmental & Colonial Work

GaiaTech's most visible work includes:
- **Ecosystem restoration** — rebuilding forests and bio-engineering previously extinct animal species across many planets
- **Hucklewheat modification** — engineered to resist sub-zero temperatures, sold to the Republic's most remote and barren colonies
- **Protein farms** — massive tent-like structures on colony outskirts producing synthetic protein derivatives at scale, making life on affiliated colonies significantly more livable

---

## Pharmaceutical Research

GaiaTech has invested heavily in vaccines and future medicine products to address the effects of various climates on human biology. This research has brought them **directly into conflict with [[BioMega]]**, the Sector's largest pharmaceutical company.

---

## Reputation

Unlike most megacorps, GaiaTech's focus on healing, feeding, and restoring has generated genuine goodwill. However, operating in the same corporate ecosystem means GaiaTech is not without rivals or conflicts of interest.
