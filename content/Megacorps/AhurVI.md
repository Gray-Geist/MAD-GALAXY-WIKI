# AhurVI

**Industry:** Virtual Intelligence · Manufacturing (Semiconductors, Eidolon Computers, Strandlock Encoders) · Software (especially Navigation)  
**CEO:** Ra-neb Anhur  
**Headquarters:** Rostam City, Mazandaran, Ahura system  
**Presence:** Core, Mid-core, and Edge worlds

![[ahurai_art.png]]
---

## Overview

**AhurVI** is a large technology manufacturer and the Sector's leading supplier of VI software, [[Eidolon Technology|Eidolon Computers]], and [[Eidolon Technology|Strandlock Encoders]] — the critical systems that facilitate interstellar navigation. Their software underpins navigation operations across much of the Sector.

---

## History

Once named **AhurAI**, this megacorp rebranded itself to **AhurVI** during the Savage Wars — a necessary pivot given the sweeping legal prohibitions against artificial intelligence that emerged during and after that conflict.
