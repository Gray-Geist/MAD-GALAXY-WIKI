# Kiloforce Systems

**Industry:** Autonomous Combat Platforms · Android Engineering · Bot Manufacturing · Integrated Weapons Systems  
**Headquarters:** Undisclosed (suspected Milicron-aligned space)  
**Presence:** Core and Mid-core worlds  
**Partner:** [[Milicron Heavy Industries]]

---

## Overview

**Kiloforce Systems** is a specialist manufacturer of androids, autonomous combat bots, and integrated weapons platforms. Unlike generalist robotics firms, Kiloforce focuses exclusively on military and paramilitary applications — their products are built to fight, and nothing else.

Kiloforce maintains a close commercial and development partnership with [[Milicron Heavy Industries]]. Kiloforce platforms ship with Milicron-stamped weapons as standard loadout, and the two companies co-develop integrated solutions: Kiloforce handles chassis architecture, locomotion, and onboard AI; Milicron provides the ordnance.

---

## Platforms

### AR-4 "Huntsman"
Kiloforce's flagship heavy combat platform. Heavily armored bipedal frame with tracked lower legs. Standard loadout: KFA Heavy ALIM Cannon (two-arm cradled carry) and a shoulder-mounted rocket pod. The Huntsman is a walking emplacement — slow but extraordinarily difficult to put down. Operated by Milicron-aligned forces and occasionally seen in the service of Republic security contractors.

### SR-1 "Specter"
Lightweight long-limbed precision platform. Carries the KFA ALIM Precision Rifle — a flat open-lattice rail-sniper optimized for extreme range. Deployed for overwatch, interdiction, and high-value target elimination. Designation: **SR-1**.

### KRB-X "Karbon Exo"
Light tactical bot. Carries the E/MLR-C or E/MLR-P Tele-Eye laser systems. Humanoid frame, tactical vest aesthetic, sensor cluster head. Power cell integrated into chassis torso — routes superconducting feed internally to the weapon. "Karbon Exo" designation visible on chassis.

### KBS-3 "Packwolf"
Mid-weight platform configured to carry and operate the RAC-27 ALIM Rotary Autocannon. Bipedal upper body, tracked lower section. Integrated ammo drum on left chassis side. Deployed for area suppression and fortification assault.
