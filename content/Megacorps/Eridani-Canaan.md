# Eridani-Canaan

**Industry:** Space Survey · Terraforming · Planetary Stabilization  
**CEO:** Dr. Soren Valt *(ascended amid boardroom purges; leverages longevity treatments to maintain iron-fisted control)*  
**Presence:** Scattered across Core and Mid-core worlds; frontier outposts in the Lyra Veil and Orion Reach; operations span multiple superpowers' borders via deniable subsidiaries

---

## Overview

A monolithic megacorporation from the pre-Savage Wars golden age, **Eridani-Canaan** embodies humanity's relentless drive to conquer and commodify the stars. They spearheaded ambitious colonization schemes, viewing planets as mere resources to be reshaped for profit — often at the expense of workers, ecosystems, and ethical boundaries.

Their cultural footprint is immense: Eridani-Canaan logos adorn everything from orbital habitats to frontier outposts, symbolizing progress to some and ruthless exploitation to others. Whispers abound of covert experiments in bioengineering and occult tech scavenging, where **"accidents" bury inconvenient truths** under layers of corporate deniability.

---

## Key Technologies

**Stellar Mirrors & Shades** — Orbital arrays that manipulate stellar output to temper hostile climates, enabling settlement on worlds that would otherwise scorch or freeze inhabitants.

**Autonomous Mantle Bore** — A rogue-capable deep-core extractor for tapping planetary mantles. Self-sustaining but unpredictable, with built-in failsafes barring use on inhabited worlds. Lost override protocols fuel black-market hunts.

**Soil Nanites** — Nanomachines accelerating soil regeneration far beyond natural rates. Heavily regulated under *Sector Nanite Protocol 4324, subsection 81* to avoid reactions with transplutonic ores or volatiles — though enforcement is lax on the fringes.

---

## History

The Savage Wars ravaged Eridani-Canaan's fleets, stranding survey teams and halting grand terraforming initiatives as supply lines collapsed under Savage assaults. Post-war, the corporation rebuilt with **predatory efficiency** — absorbing rival assets through hostile takeovers and proxy deals.

Today they pursue megascale projects with unyielding ambition: colonizing at any cost, embedding corporate spies in rival factions, and exploiting workforce contracts that border on **indentured servitude**. Stranded remnants on edge worlds sometimes break away, forming rogue colonies that haunt the corporation's ledgers.
