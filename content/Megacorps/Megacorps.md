# Megacorps

In the Antevic Sector, power is not held by governments alone. The great megacorporations stretch across star nations, buy governments, field armies, and shape the lives of billions — often more effectively than the states they nominally operate within. Some were born in the Savage Wars and baptized in blood. Others trace their lineage to the colony ships that left a dying Earth. A few are older than the Republic itself.

They are not monolithic villains. They are employers, innovators, suppliers of medicine and food and ships and weapons. They are also ruthless, unaccountable, and occasionally monstrous.
