# Cosmic Fold

**Status:** Religion  
**Adherents:** Hundreds of millions (known as **Seekers**)


![[cosmic_fold_art2.png]]


---

## Overview

The **Cosmic Fold** views the universe as an indifferent force governed by patterns and entropy. Seekers strive for **Resonance** — a state of harmony with the cosmos — through paths such as art, science, or meditation. Resonance is deeply personal and not inherently pacifist; balance may demand destruction as much as creation.

Those who fail to achieve Resonance are seen as **"Dissonant"** — their lives wasted as noise in the universe's greater symphony.

---

## Organization

The Cosmic Fold is **decentralized** — it has no formal hierarchy. Respected figures called **"Resonants"** guide local communities but are not leaders in any authoritative sense. Schisms sometimes arise over differing interpretations of Resonance.

Most adherents are found in **Coalition territories**, but pockets of followers exist throughout the Sector.

---

## Places of Worship

**Celestial sanctuaries** serve as sacred spaces — often featuring observatories and resonance chambers. Urban sanctuaries rely on artificial projections, frequently paired with remote sanctuaries for unpolluted cosmic views. Designs range from minimalist to intricate labyrinths.

---

## Core Beliefs

- The universe is neither benevolent nor cruel — it is a force to be understood and respected
- **Patterns are sacred**: they embody the universe's interconnected nature, whether in nature, thought, or action
- **Resonance** is the height of existence — achievable only through self-discovery and alignment with the cosmos
- **Entropy is not to be feared** but embraced as part of the universe's eternal cycle
- Balance may require both compassion and violence

---

## History

The Fold's rejection of god-worship emerged after Old Earth's abandonment, spreading among those disillusioned by state faiths like the [[Pentarchy]]. It grew from a position of philosophical opposition to organized religion and institutional faith.

---

## Militant Factions

Some factions — called **"Dissonant Hunters"** — interpret Resonance as a justification to eliminate perceived threats to cosmic harmony. This adds considerable tension to the religion's otherwise meditative roots.
