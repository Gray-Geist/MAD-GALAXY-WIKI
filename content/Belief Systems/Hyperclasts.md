# Hyperclasts

**Status:** Political Group / Movement  
**Membership:** Corporate elites, business magnates, private-sector technocrats

![[hyperclasts_art2.png]]

---

## Overview

The **Hyperclasts** are an ideologically extreme political movement that operates within the legal frameworks of various star nations — yet most view them as a dangerous extremist organization. Their appeal lies in a promise of decisive, unyielding leadership and efficiency, seductive to those disillusioned with bureaucratic decay.

Rivals view them as dangerously radical and elitist — *"a movement of corporate tyrants dreaming of an energy-dominated future."* Mainstream society regards them with a mixture of fear, fascination, contempt, and — perhaps most dangerously — **hope**.

---

## Organization

Hyperclasts follow a **hyper-efficient boardroom hierarchy**:
- A single **hyper-executive** at the apex
- Chosen by an inner council of influential financiers and industry captains
- Regional cells mimic corporate divisions — autonomous but bound by strict internal protocols and secret loyalty rites
- Internal power struggles and ruthless ambition are common — mirroring the cutthroat world of high finance

They operate legally, primarily within **democratic and open societies**.

---

## Core Beliefs

The central tenet: humanity's future depends on **The Crossroads** — consolidating power under a single, autocratic regime capable of harnessing and controlling all energy resources in the Sector and beyond.

- Humanity has **stagnated**
- Existing state institutions are **fragmented and weak**
- Only a **centralized hyper-executive authority** can drive humanity to the next evolutionary step on the **Kardashev Scale** (though this vision is centuries or millennia away)
- Current governments and religious institutions are **distractions** that have been proven to inhibit true progress

---

## Rumors

- Some rumors once linked the Hyperclasts to **the Autarch**, though Hyperclast contacts dismiss this, claiming the Autarch remains too entangled with existing power structures
- It is rumored they have secured their own colonies on the Edge
- Their true support base is difficult to measure — whispers suggest significant backing in select industrial hubs
