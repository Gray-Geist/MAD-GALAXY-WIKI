# Ul'Rekar

**Status:** Religion / Mono-culture / Theocratic State  
**Adherents:** Billions of **disciples**

![[ulrekar_art_main.png]]

---

## Overview

The **Ul'Rekar** are both a religion and a theocratic state — the sole sanctioned source of trained [[Polymath & Poly-psykers|Navigators]] in the Antevic Sector. Founded by **Agamen Ul'Rekar**, they worship a deity called the **Netjer** and control the [[Polymath & Poly-psykers#The White Fleet & Being "Taken"|White Fleet]] that Takes poly-psykers from across the Sector.

Without the Ul'Rekar, humanity would be unable to crack or maintain [[Cosmic Strands|Strands]], and interstellar civilization would collapse.

## Locations

The Ul'Rekar Hierocracy controls many systems of known space. The location of **Ul'Haupt** — the nation's home world — is unknown even to their own agents. The jump coordinates are among the most closely guarded secrets in existence.

All Ul'Rekar worlds house **hwern-natir** (tabernacles). It is extremely rare for any Ul'Rekar settlement to lack one.

---

## Core Beliefs

- Worship of the **Netjer** (net-JAIR) — a deity first communed with by Agamen Ul'Rekar
- The Ul'Rekar assert that brief **glimpses of the Netjer** occur during [[Skip Drives & FTL Travel|skip jumps]]
- The energies found in poly-psykers are the direct result of sentience from the wider galaxy — a gift from the Netjer
- The Netjer gifted Mankind the ability to travel the Strands long ago, then entered a **hibernation lasting a millennium** to recover its strength
- Now that the Netjer is finally awakening, it will soon reclaim its creation in an event known as the **"Ogdoad"**

---

## Founding

In **57 AE**, Agamen Ul'Rekar — a mathematician working on the covert Project Perseus — claimed communion with the Netjer, used polymath to crack the first Cosmic Strand (**Alpha Omega**), and became the first poly-psyker and first Navigator. He then trained many more, enabling humanity's escape to the Antevic Sector.

The Ul'Rekar have provided the Sector with all trained Navigators ever since — Taking raw poly-psykers from across known space, forcibly if necessary.

---

## The White Fleet & Hunter Finder Teams

The **White Fleet** Takes poly-psykers, who never return. The process of transforming them into Navigators is one of the most closely guarded secrets in human history. Most Navigators do not remember what was done to them.

**Hunter Finder Teams** actively pursue and acquire poly-psykers using every available method, including their own loyal poly-psykers as lures.

See: [[Polymath & Poly-psykers#The White Fleet & Being "Taken"|The White Fleet]]
