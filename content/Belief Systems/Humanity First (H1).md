# Humanity First (H1)

**Status:** Cult / Movement  
**Members:** Known as **Cygnets**

![[H1_art.jpg]]

---

## Overview

**Humanity First** (commonly abbreviated **H1**) is a militant organization with many cells — some that could be considered hedonistic cults composed of social-anarchists, luddites, fringe individuals like drug addicts and the derelict, and others that are run like well-oiled machines of organized labor made up of workers displaced by automation. What unites them all: violent opposition to technology, automation, and the industries that produce them.

Total membership is unknown, but the organization appears to be steadily growing and stereotypically attracts societal outcasts, disgruntled workers displaced by automation, and social-anarchist radicals.

---

## Leadership

Presently unknown. Followers refer to themselves as **cygnets**. Unconfirmed rumors describe a **diarchy**:
- **"Hetaira"** — unknown matriarch
- **"Hetairoi"** — unknown patriarch

---

## Locations

H1 exists throughout the Sector but concentrates in **large urban centers and metroplexes**, especially those with immense industrial centers.

Members meet in clandestine gatherings rumored to be called **"the backroom."**

---

## Beliefs & Activities

Cygnets congregate in clandestine meetings and execute plans that cause social upheaval by destroying technology, AI, robotics, and mechs, and harming people who use these devices. H1 also targets the industries that create or use these technologies, aiming their focus in ways that disrupt the means of production.

### The Two Faces of H1

**Hedonistic Cells** — Humano-centric rituals allegedly held in "back rooms" where individuals take part in drug-fueled orgies that allure, entrap, and coerce their membership.

**Organized Cells** — Run like well-oiled machines of organized labor, made up of workers displaced by automation. Execute plans of social disruption with discipline and efficiency — organizing protests, riots, and terrorist activities.

---

## Escalation of Violence

In their early days, H1 cultists engaged in lower-level crimes: vandalism and larceny. Within recent years the organization has become increasingly violent through:
- Acts of mass terrorism
- Suicide bombings
- Guerilla attacks on high-population urban areas and industrial centers
