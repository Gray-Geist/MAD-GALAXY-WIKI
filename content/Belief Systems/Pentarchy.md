# The Pentarchy

**Status:** Religion (State Sanctioned — see Republic of Man)  
**Adherents:** The galaxy's largest practiced organized religion (known as **Pentarchists**)

![[pentarchy_art2.jpg]]

---

## Overview

The **Pentarchy** is the most widely practiced organized religion in the Antevic Sector, with parishes found on every major settlement and every human-occupied planet. Born on a dying Old Earth, its core belief is that **God exists somewhere in the universe and is guiding humanity to meet Him**.

---

## Leadership Structure

| Title | Role |
|---|---|
| **High Presbyter** | Inter-Sector leader of The Pentarchy |
| **Rector Superior** | Leader for each major settlement/planet; reports to the High Presbyter |
| **Deacons** | Leader for each major settlement/planet; reports to Rector Superior |
| **Vicar Populi** | Local parish leader; reports to the planetary Deacon |

## Places of Worship

- **The Elysian Cathedral** on Pacem — the holiest building associated with the Pentarchy
- Local churches are officially known as **Quinaries**

---

## Origins & History

Pentarchy was nearly extinguished in the final days of Old Earth, as civilization collapsed after the elite exodus. But in those same desperate final days, the [[Skip Drives & FTL Travel|Chained Eidolon Reaction Drive]] was discovered — and with it, humanity's escape into space.

Pentarchists assert that humanity's invention of the skip drive is direct evidence of **divine guidance** and God's desire to be found. This became the **First Promise Fulfilled**.

As the venerable religions of Old Earth let go of their terrestrial tenets — bound to a dead planet — Pentarchy saw explosive growth in those final days and carried that momentum into the stars, absorbing many ancient traditions into its inclusive platform.

---

## The Five Promised Truths

1. God is in the universe; HE will grant us the ability to travel the galaxy to see HIM.
2. God is in the light; HE will show us how to enlighten humanity in HIS love.
3. God is in the sound; HE will teach humanity to listen and accept HIS message.
4. God is in the time; HE will breach time, space, and show us new dimensions.
5. God is in life; HE will let us live forever in HIS name as we spread HIS message.
