# Belief Systems

The Antevic Sector is home to billions of people and dozens of faiths — from the galaxy's largest organized religion to violent cults, ascetic philosophies, and theocratic states. In a universe where faster-than-light travel has been observed to produce brief glimpses of something vast and incomprehensible, the question of what lies beyond the stars is not merely spiritual. It is political, military, and existential.

---

## Religions & Philosophies

| Entry | Summary |
|---|---|
| [[Cosmic Fold]] | A philosophy of harmony with the universe through Resonance — attuning oneself to the natural energies flowing through all living things |
| [[Path of the Hoshi-Petal]] | A Sinasian-rooted philosophy of personal enlightenment and self-reflection, guided by Star Stewards and the Ten Thousand Petals |
| [[Pentarchy]] | The Sector's largest organized religion, born on dying Old Earth — its followers believe God is out there, and humanity's skip drive is the first proof |
| [[Ul'Rekar]] | A theocratic monoculture and the sole sanctioned source of trained Navigators — without them, interstellar civilization would collapse |

## Movements & Cults

| Entry | Summary |
