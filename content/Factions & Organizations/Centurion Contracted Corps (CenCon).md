## Headquarters & Safehouses

**Primary HQ:** FLS Voltaire — a Lothranium refueling station (*"gas station"*) on The Archipelago in the **Ilion Beta system**. Gas stations are of immense importance, especially on the Edge, and house more than just fuel for vessels. Though they vary in size, they are all-in-one businesses that have lodging, recreation, dining, shopping, and of course, refueling. Due to their pivotal role, they are often seen as a "Sector crossroads" where foot traffic is often high. Though CenCon doesn't control the entire station, their influence on it is wide — using it as a front was a wise move in order to diversify and legitimize their revenue streams.

**Confirmed Safehouses (per RIA intelligence):**
1. Sage's Row Orbital near Alexandria *(Republic governmental super-station; only the truly elite may enter Alexandria itself)*
2. Vulcain Arche, District 9 on Aquila *(Republic homeland, Praetarus system)*
3. Prodigy Terminal, Primus system *(Coalition space; first major human settlement at Alpha Terra)*
4. Talonfall on Lux Aureum *(Arkadien system, Coalition space)*

## The Quartet

CenCon was first established nearly thirty years ago, but have experienced the majority of their growth within the past 8 years. This is largely due to the rise of the **Quartet**, four agents nearly a decade ago who earned reputations as being an effective and cunning team. Para-military circles, mercenary groups, and smugglers lionize the Quartet for their accomplishments and their stories are told across the Sector typically in dark smoke-filled cantinas, clubs and bars.

## History

Former Coalition commander and war-hero **Adran Wiart** started the Centurion Contractor Corps more than thirty years ago after a twenty-year career in the military. Wiart, tired of the bureaucratic red-tape, politics, and lack of progress, left Coalition space and moved to the Republic where he started CenCon.

For nearly fifteen years, Chief Commander Wiart struggled to get the organization off the ground and could only secure small contracts, despite his overall reputation as a respected military officer. At the time, Wiart was not only the face of CenCon, but one of its most active agents. Because more established PMCs often took the most lucrative contracts, Wiart had to secure smaller contracts. In many cases he was often viewed as an older and severely maimed former soldier that was past his prime, though he doggedly proved the last point untrue. Wiart adapted to his handicaps and with every successful mission, he invested most of the credits back into CenCon, but also invested in himself, via cyberware. After many years he became a force to be reckoned with and earned the nickname "Iron Wiart", completing enough contracts to attract skillful agents. Through contracts Wiart had proven himself to be a diligent tactician but his genius was truly in his strategic ability to invest in human capital and build cohesive units, which solidified his rise as Chief Commander over the coming years. Even in his older age, Chief Commander Wiart has an active role in CenCon's management, but has focused a lot of his attention in recent years to mentoring his star students First Warden Sinon Haya & IO Cyra Noves.

Since the Quartet, CenCon's popularity as an Omni-PMC has risen, but recruitment efforts to expand have been minimal in order to preserve the integrity of its ranks. Becoming an agent requires an intense referral process, testing and training regimen, unlike many larger rival groups. The Centurions on the other hand are starting to create an even larger reputation as a jack of all trades PMC that effectively gets the job done.

For the past fifteen years, CenCon command has taken place on the FLS Voltaire, a center of Lothranium refueling aptly nicknamed a "gas station".

## Name References

- *Adran Wiart* — An homage to the "Indestructible Soldier" Adrian Carton de Wiart, an English soldier during WW1 known for his savagery, grit, and ability to push through adversity.
- *Sinon Haya* — A reference to Simo Häyä, the legendary Finnish sniper that has one of the, if not the largest body counts as a military sharpshooter.
- *FLS Voltaire* — FLS (Fortified Lothranium Station); Voltaire is a reference to the Enlightenment philosopher known for his beliefs on freedom of speech.
- *The Archipelago* — A series of small, medium and some large space stations all linked together using an intra-gravitational link (IGL) that binds select stations/ships together.
