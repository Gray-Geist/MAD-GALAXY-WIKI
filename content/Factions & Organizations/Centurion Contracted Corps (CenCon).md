## Headquarters & Safehouses

**Primary HQ:** FLS Voltaire — a Lothranium refueling station (*"gas station"*) on The Archipelago in the **Ilion Beta system**. Gas stations are pivotal Sector crossroads: all-in-one facilities with lodging, recreation, dining, shopping, and refueling. CenCon doesn't control the entire station, but their influence on it is wide — using it as a front to legitimize and diversify their revenue.

**Confirmed Safehouses (per RIA intelligence):**
- Sage's Row Orbital near Alexandria *(Republic governmental super-station; only the truly elite may enter Alexandria itself)*
- Vulcain Arche, District 9 on Aquila *(Republic homeland, Praetarus system)*
- Prodigy Terminal, Primus system *(Coalition space; first major human settlement at Alpha Terra)*
- Talonfall on Lux Aureum *(Arkadien system, Coalition space)*

## History

**Adran Wiart** — former Coalition commander and war hero — founded CenCon **over thirty years ago** after a twenty-year military career, driven out by bureaucracy, politics, and a lack of progress. He moved to the Republic and built the organization from scratch.

For nearly fifteen years CenCon struggled, securing only small contracts. As an older, heavily injured (and then heavily cybered) former soldier, Wiart was underestimated constantly. He proved them wrong — doggedly taking every available contract and reinvesting credits back into the organization and himself. Each successful mission expanded his reputation.

His true genius lay in **strategic investment in human capital** and building cohesive units. After years of proving himself, he began attracting skillful agents. The Quartet's rise accelerated CenCon's reputation dramatically.

Since the Quartet, CenCon has deliberately kept **recruitment minimal** to preserve unit integrity. Becoming an agent requires an intense referral process, testing, and training regimen unlike most larger rivals. CenCon is building a reputation as a jack-of-all-trades PMC that **actually gets the job done**.
