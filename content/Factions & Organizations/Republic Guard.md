# Republic Guard (RG)

**Status:** Republic Military Branch
**Size:** Millions of soldiers across the Sector; often composed of members drawn from local planetary defense forces
**Headquarters:** Republic Center for Military Operations, Alexandria

![[republic_guard_art1.jpg]]

---

## Overview

Republic Guard (i.e. soldiers, "basics"), are the standard infantry deployed by the Republic of Man throughout the Sector. They are separate from the elite [[RTAC (Paladins)|RTAC Paladins]] and the Marines of the Republic Navy. Often formed from young, eager conscripts, they serve as expeditionary units tasked to hold territory as a garrison and operate the heavy equipment that isn't always as feasible for rapid planetary assaults.

---

## Equipment

They wear a set of armor which covers the torso, shoulders, and upper arm. Polarized goggles and respirator masks could be issued to filter out toxic substances in hostile environments. The set also featured optional helmets, shin guards, and colored bicep bands that denoted the trooper's position, with red indicating a soldier and white indicating a medic. As front-line combatants, Guard have access to an extensive arsenal deployed by the Republic.

![[republic_guard_art4.png]]

---

## Urban Garrison Role

Urban garrisons are fielded with non-lethal riot gear such as ballistic shields and batons to suppress citizen uprisings and protests and are specially trained to form multi-layered walls with their shields.
