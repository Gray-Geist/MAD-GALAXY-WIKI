# Republic Guard (RG)

**Status:** Republic Military Branch  
**Size:** Millions of soldiers across the Sector  
**Headquarters:** Republic Center for Military Operations, Alexandria

![[republic_guard_art1.jpg]]

---

## Overview

The **Republic Guard** are the standard infantry of the Republic of Man — the everyday boots on the ground deployed throughout the Sector. Separate from the elite [[RTAC (Paladins)]] and the Marines of the Republic Navy, Guard forces are often formed from young, eager conscripts.

Their role: **expeditionary garrison and heavy equipment operations** — holding territory and operating heavy assets that aren't always feasible for rapid planetary assaults.

---

## Equipment

Standard issue armor covers:
- Torso, shoulders, and upper arm
- Polarized goggles and respirator mask (for toxic environments)
- Optional helmets and shin guards
- **Colored bicep bands** indicating role: red = soldier, white = medic
![[republic_guard_art4.png]]
Guard have access to an extensive arsenal deployed by the Republic.

### Urban Garrison Role
For civilian control, Guard are fielded with **non-lethal riot gear**:
- Ballistic shields
- Batons
- Training in **multi-layered shield walls** to suppress uprisings and protests
