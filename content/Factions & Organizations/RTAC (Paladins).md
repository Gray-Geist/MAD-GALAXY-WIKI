# Republic Tactical Assault Corps (RTAC)

**Status:** Republic Military Branch (Semi-Autonomous)  
**Personnel:** [REDACTED] — "1% of the 1%"  
**Common Name:** **Paladins**

![[rtac_symbol.jpg]]

---

## Overview

**RTAC** is the premier Republic military branch — an elite force whose members are universally called **Paladins**, regardless of the organization's modern name. They are the Republic's ultimate weapon: the 1% of the 1%, forged through a training program so brutal that most candidates never survive it.

Central Command is a **dispersed network of node locations** designed to survive even the destruction of individual installations. For political reasons, much of High Command is based in **Alexandria**.

---

## Origins: The VTAP

RTAC traces its roots to the **Verovingia star system** and its capital planet of **Lothar**. After the fall of Sot Prime during the Savage Wars, humanity's last desperate hope was a new kind of fighting force: the **Vulkus Tactical Assault Paladins (VTAP)**.

**Tyrus Martell** — *the Butcher of Andalore*, first Rex General — built VTAP through the merciless **Crucible** training program, transforming recruits beyond their physical and mental limits to survive unforgiving environments and enemies. Only the 1% of the 1% earned the right to become Paladins. This standard holds to this day.

VTAP operated under virtually **no political restrictions** — no limits on weaponry, experimental tech, or tactical approach. It was Tyrus Martell who authorized the CNTO strike on Sot Prime (see [[History of Humanity#The Disaster at Sot Prime|The Requite Dilemma]]).

---

## Post-War Transition

After the Savage Wars, VTAP held "the stick" to the Astral Alliance's "carrot" in political negotiations — and leveraged this position to become a **semi-autonomous military branch** of the newly formed Republic of Man under their new name: the Republic Tactical Assault Corps.

Their members would forever be called **Paladins**.
