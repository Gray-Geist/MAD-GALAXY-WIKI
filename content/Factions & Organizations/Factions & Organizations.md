# Factions & Organizations

Beyond the great star nations and megacorporations, the Antevic Sector is shaped by the soldiers, operatives, and contractors who do the work that governments won't claim. Some serve the Republic openly. Others operate in the shadows between powers — taking the contracts that never appear on any official ledger.
