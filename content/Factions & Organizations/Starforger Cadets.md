# Starforger Cadets

**Status:** Active — Civilian Youth Organization
**Ages:** 8–18
**Region:** Primarily Core and Mid-Core Worlds; small chapters at the Edge
**Class:** Upper middle class to elite only, typically

---

## Overview

The **Starforger Cadets** are a structured youth organization focused on practical engineering, spacecraft repair, and survival skills. Popular among the children of mid-core engineers, shipyard workers, and aspirational families, the Cadets are well-regarded for producing capable, technically literate young people prepared for careers in starship operations, colonial settlement, and military service.

---

## Activities & Training

Starforger Cadets focus on:
- Survival skills and resource management
- Spacecraft maintenance and mechanical repair
- Basic self-defense
- Minor FTL Jump-navigation courses

Drills are hands-on and demanding. Cadets are expected to perform under pressure — the organization's philosophy holds that competence is built through repetition and consequence, not classroom theory.

---

## Ranks & Badges

Cadets advance through a tiered rank structure:

| Rank | Notes |
|------|-------|
| **Gearlings** | Entry level |
| **Forgeborn** | Intermediate |
| **Starwrights** | Senior cadets |

Merit badges are earned for demonstrated skills, including: *Stellar Engineering*, *FTL Jump Preparedness*, *Metalworking*, and *Substellar Navigation*.
