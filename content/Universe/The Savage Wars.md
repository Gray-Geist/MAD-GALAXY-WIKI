# The Savage Wars

*"At the cost of possibly much of their own humanity, the Astral Alliance was victorious."*

---

## Overview

The Savage Wars were a six-century-long conflict between humanity and the **Savages** — post-human entities who had left Old Earth aboard massive Lighthugger ships (megacity-sized "hulks") incapable of FTL. They lived in the void between worlds, emerging as raiders that eventually became an existential threat to all of human civilization.

The wars defined the modern Sector: destroying the Coalition's dominance, forging the Republic, and leaving scars — political, environmental, and genetic — that still shape every faction today.

---

## The Savages: Who Were They?

![[Savage Dynasties - Extinct.png]]
![[Savage Dynasties - Extinct2.png]]
![[The Uplifted Order (this is when the Savage Dynasties united).webp]]

The Savages were **not colonizers**. They remained in the cold black void between stars — a place safe, empty, and incapable of giving anything back. The void gave nothing, so the Savages **needed resources, materials, people, and flesh**. They became raiders.

For three decades they hit, pillaged, and ran — never engaging in prolonged battle with military forces. They preferred the shadows. Their Lighthugger hulks dwarfed human starships but were not originally designed for combat. The Savages were **not known for colonization** — instead, they were advanced **occult technological abhumans** who stuck to their massive ships.

Over time, they jury-rigged their hulks with **occult technology** that could not be fathomed or reverse-engineered. Their internal alliances were tenuous. Their dynasties had evolved along entirely separate paths from humanity for generations, developing a **dogmatic belief** that they had shed all that stained mankind — that they were something new and better.

## The Fall of Sot Prime

![[savage_wars_sot_prime1.png]]
![[savage_wars_sot_prime2.png]]
![[savage_wars_sot_prime3.jpg]]
![[savage_wars_sot_prime4.png]]

All of that changed with the **Fall of Sot Prime** — the Coalition's crown jewel planet.

The Coalition, thrust into war, planned revenge. A Coalition-led force called the **Combined Forces** was formed. For two months they swept aside Savage forces that seemed to melt away in flight. Victory appeared at hand.

**62 days after it went dark**, the Combined Forces launched an all-out assault to retake Sot Prime. Meticulously made plans fell to ruin in moments. The Savages **did not retreat into the gloom of space**. They stood, vented their hate through occult technology, and crushed the Combined Forces in what became known as the **Disaster at Sot Prime**.

For the first time, the Savages had not just raided — they had **stood their ground on a planet and won**. Shockwaves of fear swept through all of mankind.

---

## The Alatian Accords

Faced with annihilation, the Coalition **kowtowed to the Savages** and signed the **Alatian Accords** — a non-aggression ultimatum:

- The Coalition would fall back to other sectors of space
- The Coalition would maintain **forced unwavering neutrality** in all future conflicts
- **Sot Prime** — the capital of the [[Alatyr System]] — would become Savage territory

Billions escaped. Billions more were left behind.

The Combined Forces political entity ceased to exist. In its place, the **Astral Alliance** formed — **without the Coalition**.

---

## The Refugee Crisis and Founding of Helgoth

![[helgoth_founding1.png]]
![[helgoth_founding2.png]]

Billions of refugees fled Sot Prime. The Coalition was **starving for resources**, and a newly surveyed planet was deemed viable for emergency colonization. The refugees named it **Helgoth** — after a terrifying underworld of torture and death — because that is what it was.

The **[[Neus Kapitel Group (NKG)]]** took up the reins of this desperate operation and siphoned the refugees to Helgoth, where they became colonists. Life was brutal and many died from exposure, illness, and starvation. It took decades of struggle to even develop settlements.

After approximately **three generations**, humans on Helgoth began to genetically mutate. They became the **Helgosts** — a people with pale skin, often hairless, with strengthened connective tissues, more oxygenated blood, increased lung function, and higher radiation resistance. They are faster, jump higher, and outpace baseline humans. But **muscle tone significantly decreased**, requiring pharmaceutical intervention with its own side effects: aggression, insomnia, and infertility.

See: [[Baadensenar System]] and [[Baadensenar System#The Helgost Mutation]]

## The Century of Darkness and the Astral Alliance

For **nearly a century**, Savage territories continued to spread. Alatyr was drained of its resources and subjected to untold horrors. Then **whispers spread** of a newly formed intra-Sector force called the **Astral Alliance**.

The Astral Alliance reached Alatyr and fought the Savage Armada head-on in a prolonged campaign — but could not secure an advantage. They could not liberate Sot Prime.

---

## The Requite Dilemma (Req-D)

Commander **Tyrus Martell** of the **Vulkus Tactical Assault Paladins (VTAP)** knew the war could not be won while the Savages held Sot Prime. The Alliance authorized what became known as the **Requite Dilemma** ("Req-D").

Martell unleashed an experimental **Chained Nuclear Trigger Ordinance (CNTO)** — pronounced "Sin-toe"; also called a "trigger nuke."

> A CNTO works by nuclear-igniting every hydrogen atom on a planet, causing a chain reaction that completely burns off the atmosphere and **glasses everything** capable of ever supporting life. After such use, **no terraforming is possible**.

Sot Prime was glassed. **Billions died**. The planet became a perpetually poisoned wasteland. Top-secret records indicate that a special forces element attempted a humanitarian rescue before the CNTO was deployed — but failed.

The Requite Dilemma ended Savage occupation of Sot Prime. It is considered the turning point of the war — and one of the most morally contested acts in human history.

---

## Victory and the Founding of the Republic

For **six centuries** the Savage Wars raged. At the cost of possibly much of their own humanity, the Astral Alliance was victorious.

The Alliance disbanded and **reformed as the Republic of Man**.

Several nations refused to abdicate control to the Republic and went to war. The Republic **swallowed most of them** — but lost their campaign against the Merovins in a humiliating defeat and was forced to recognize the **[[Cannard Noz System|Merovinist Triumvirate's sovereignty]]** via the Ysian Accords.

---

## The Orders of Man: The Coalition's Humiliation

![[coalition_history_banner.png]]

The Coalition had initially welcomed the Republic as liberators. But the Republic **labeled them as conspirators** for the Alatian Accords — their non-aggression pact with the Savages.

Under the **Orders of Man**, the Coalition was forced to pay reparations. The Republic took **[[Primus System|Primus]]** — the first settled human system, and the Pentarchy's holy land — as a form of reparation. The Coalition has **never forgotten this humiliation**.

---

## Legacy

The Savage Wars left:
- **Sot Prime** — glassed and uninhabitable, the wound at the heart of the Alatyr System
- **The Helgosts** — a genetically distinct people born from refugee suffering, now led by the Autarch
- **The Republic** — ascendant, but built on force, betrayal, and impossible moral choices
- **The Coalition** — humiliated, diminished, and seething
- **The Merovinist Triumvirate** — independent, sovereign, trading with all
- **The Ul'Rekar** — guardians of the Navigators without whom none of this was possible
