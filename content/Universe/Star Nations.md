# Star Nations

*A strategic intelligence overview of the Antevic Sector's political landscape — compiled by Astrospatial Intelligence (ASTINT).*

> *"The current starmap is a snapshot of the extent of human exploration and settlement across interstellar space. It denotes just a small segment of the Perseus Arm spiral. Its name derives from the Antevic Nexus, a dense cluster of habitable worlds at the heart of humanity's earliest expansion."*

![[star_nations_index.jpg]]

The Sector contains far more systems than can be reasonably settled or peopled with the current population. Political maps often project national borders over empty, unpeopled, or unadministered space — this document does not. Independent systems exist, and their independence is real.

---

## A Note on the Cold War Era

The modern political landscape was shaped by two conflicts that followed the [[The Savage Wars|Savage Wars]]:

**The Wars of Independence (1440–1453 AE)** erupted 29 years after the Savage Wars ended. The Coalition's decentralized confederacy structure left it fatally vulnerable to internal division and external manipulation. Rebellion spread from Coalition space into Republic and Novoset territories alike. By 1444, the **United Citizen Federation** was born from the navies of breakaway colonies. The Wars stripped the Coalition of superpower status permanently.

**The Secession Wars (1488–1509 AE)** followed 35 years later. Disaffected systems in the Republic and Novoset Continuum launched coordinated insurgencies that neither superpower could fully suppress. Both emerged weakened and more inward-looking. The UCF emerged strengthened, cementing its rise as the third superpower.

## Classification Methodology

ASTINT classifies star nations across three tiers:

**Superpowers** — Super-extensive astropolitical influence; dominant military with interstellar reach; robust and diversified economy; advanced technological prowess; compellingly strong Sector-wide diplomatic and cultural influence.

**Major Powers** — Extensive astropolitical influence; adequate military with interstellar reach; prospering or diversified economy; above-average technological prowess; strong extra-regional diplomatic and cultural influence.

**Minor Powers** — Limited astropolitical influence; minimal interstellar military reach; narrow economy; common technological prowess; restricted diplomatic and cultural influence. *Note: a minor power may individually satisfy one or two superpower or major power criteria while still ranking as minor overall.*

## Superpowers

### Republic of Man

![[Republic of Man Battle Flag.jpg]]
![[Republic of Man Peace Flag.jpg]]
![[Republic of Man Crest Emblem - large.png]]
![[Republic of Man Crest Emblem - small.jpg]]

*Founded 777 AE · Oldest superpower · Oligarchic Republic*

The Republic is generally credited with pulling humanity out of the Decline, though its history and culture date to around 600 AE. It is the oldest continuous state in the Sector — and the most internally fractured.

The Republic is not a democracy. Centuries of political monetization and nepotism have concentrated power in the hands of the few. All citizens have a vote, but less affluent systems are deliberately less influential, and the poorest citizens are systematically disenfranchised. It is best described as a **Constitutional Oligarchy** — power concentrated in the few, but with strong safeguards against concentration in any one individual.

The Republic's battle flag comprises the **Rays of Humanity**, the twelve stars of the founding core systems, and the **Lady of Peace** — one hand at her side in peace, the other wielding the **"Sword of Lex"** (the law) to protect the citizens. The black field symbolises space, noting the Republic's mission in bringing the Rays of Humanity to all. The battle flag bears a widened light ray compared to the peacetime flag.

For the full Republic government structure diagram, see [[Alexandria]].

**Government Structure**

*The House of Reason* — Nearly two thousand members split into two congresses. The Congress of the People elects representatives by population. The Congress of Citizens divides 200 seats by economic class: 20 to ancient Patrician families, 80 to wealthy Partisans, 80 to middle-class Risers, and 20 to the Commoners — the vast majority of the population.

*The Senate* — The primary legislative organ and final arbiter of all legislation. Senators must be Patrician or Partisan class and serve for life. The Senate elects the two co-heads of the Republic, appoints Governors-Prime, and appoints military flag officers. Each Senator is awarded a personal guard of 50 palatine vestals.

*The Synarchs* — Two co-Heads of the Republic elected from among the Senate. Each holds a veto that cannot be overridden. In times of emergency, the Senate may pass the "final enabling act," granting the Synarchs a six-month temporary dictatorship with no checks on their power. This has no precedent.

*The Ordinatorium* — Six Ordinators, each heading a different ministry, execute the Senate's directives. The position of First Ordinator rotates yearly. Unlike the Synarchs, Ordinators need not be Senators and are elected directly by the Congress of Citizens.

This structure, along with its other trappings such as the integration of the state religion and the power of the military, have created a government that quashes dissent from the mainstream political opinion and favors the interests of the wealthy. In this era of growing threats, this system will be tested — and with populism on the rise among the Commoners and Risers, there has been more and more alignment with Megacorps to keep things orderly.

**Society**

The Republic is a deeply stratified society where wealth dictates opportunity, but one that offers genuine autonomy in personal life. Censorship is minimal. Arts, media, and philosophy flourish in core world urban centers. The state religion reinforces unity and duty, participation is strongly encouraged, and its values permeate civic culture.

For those with the ambition and cunning to navigate political alliances, business monopolies, and patronage systems, the rewards are unparalleled.

**Military**

- *Republic Guard (RG)* — Primary planetary defense and expeditionary force. Combined-arms doctrine with an unmatched logistics network. Excels at peacekeeping and large-scale planetary invasions.
- *Republic Armada* — The largest and most technologically advanced navy in the Sector. Carrier strike groups, ballistic missile platforms, deep-space dominance, and orbital bombardment capability.
- *Republic Tactical Assault Corps (RTAC / Paladins)* — Elite semi-autonomous branch answering directly to the Senate and Synarchs. Small, agile strike groups for deep-strike assaults, counterinsurgency, black ops, and high-value target elimination. See [[RTAC (Paladins)]].

*Article 22* — A constitutional statute allowing RTAC to assume control of the Republic government for up to six months in an emergency. The Senate can be dissolved, delegates tried in court, and individual worlds elect new senators in their place.

**Relations**
- *Novoset Continuum:* Viewed as an existential threat to be contained. Hostile — cyber warfare and propaganda campaigns run constantly.
- *United Citizen Federation:* Considered a rogue offshoot that betrayed its heritage. Diplomatic relations strained; covert operations and political jockeying over neutral systems.

### Novoset Continuum

![[Novoset Continuum.jpg]]

*Founded 1330 AE · Born from the Sovolian Dominion · Authoritarian Technocracy*

The Novoset Continuum is a grim monument to efficiency, sacrifice, and control. Some argue the Sovolian Dominion was not destroyed by the Savages but was murdered by the Continuists themselves. Whatever the truth, the Continuum emerged from near-annihilation and forged itself into a state driven by collective survival above all else.

Its motto: **"Order Above All."**

The Continuum's flag displays a white circle against a black field, symbolizing infinite unity, industry, and the technological mastery necessary to thrive in a dark, hostile universe. The blue represents the strip of flesh torn from the Sovolian Dominion in the Good Revolution, the white the purity of the same Revolution, and the crimson symbolizes the blood of the workers and low class that was shed.

Citizens live under constant surveillance, their lives planned and optimized by the **Novosyan Secretariat Sovnarate (NSS)** — a technocratic council that views society as a machine requiring constant refinement. Individual ambition is stripped in favor of collective advancement. Dissent is met with swift suppression.

**Government Structure**

All power flows through the NSS, housed in the capital city of **Sovol**. Commissars and technocrats are selected through exhaustive ideological vetting — not elected. The **Director-General**, elected from within the Sovnarate, serves as face and commander, wielding near-absolute power in crisis. Regional Councils govern each system and submit directly to the NSS.

Despite its efficient surface, the Continuum suffers from rampant internal corruption. The NSS enforces harsh punishments for it while remaining deeply embedded in it.

**Society**

Three classes: *Labor Units* (majority — regimented, functional education, assigned roles); *Citizen-Technocrats* (intellectual core, many cybernetically enhanced); *Sovnarate Elite* (political and military leaders living in isolated luxury under constant peer scrutiny — failure means disappearance).

Class mobility is technically possible through the **Merit Assessment Bureau (MAB)** — but in practice, rare and politically motivated.

*The Cyberapparat* — A vast surveillance network monitoring all citizens. Personal communications, work productivity, health metrics — all collected and analyzed. **Data-Scribes** interface directly with it; **Neural Enforcers** suppress dissent; **The Shadow Bureau** eliminates internal threats.

**Ideological Principles**
- *Unity Through Sacrifice:* The individual is secondary to the state.
- *Technological Mastery:* Heavy investment in cybernetics, VI (within strict parameters), and automation. Neural interfaces mandatory for military and many bureaucrats.
- *Survival Above All:* Resource efficiency above ecological or humanitarian consequences.

**Military**

- *Infinite Host* — Vast mechanized ground force. Mass-mobilization with cybernetically enhanced infantry, armor columns, and drone swarms connected via neural uplinks. Specializes in siege warfare, planetary occupation, and urban pacification.
- *Red Fleet* — Attrition doctrine. Overwhelming saturation attacks at extreme range using wave after wave of multi-role missiles. Advanced ECM suites cripple enemy fleets. Capital ships launch Perun Heavy Torpedoes and Stormpiercer Barrage Missiles.
- *Continuum Corps of Engineers* — Paramilitary organization deploying terraforming teams, industrial drones, and mobile construction platforms to convert conquered worlds into fortified resource hubs. Responsible for orbital defense arrays, subterranean bunkers, and planetary shielding.

**Relations**
- *Republic of Man:* Viewed as decadent, inefficient, and destined to collapse. Cold — frequent border skirmishes and espionage.
- *United Citizen Federation:* The Federation's meritocratic ideals are a direct challenge to Continuum collectivism. Tense diplomatic standoffs and proxy wars in contested systems.

### United Citizen Federation (UCF)

![[The Federation.jpg]]

*Founded 1444 AE · Youngest superpower · Federal Parliamentary Republic*

The UCF rose from the chaos of the Wars of Independence, uniting collapsing colonial administrations under military veterans and civic leaders who formed a junta — then, in an act of contrition, enacted **The Covenant of Service**: a doctrine of shared sacrifice, personal responsibility, and martial order.

Motto: *"For the Federation, We Serve."*

Unlike the Republic, which concentrates power among wealthy elites, the UCF grants citizenship only to those who complete a term of **Federal Service**. Citizenship is earned, not inherited.

**Government Structure**

*Federal Armed Services (FAS)* — The executive arm. The **Sky Marshal**, elected by the Federal Council, commands all military operations. Every Sky Marshal must have served in both the Mobile Infantry and the Fleet before eligibility.

*Federal Council* — The legislative body. Representatives selected from national governors within each system. One vote per member. During conflict, the Sky Marshal provides weekly military situation reports.

*Federal Court* — The judicial branch. Swift penalties for failures of duty reinforce a culture of accountability.

Leadership at every level must be earned — Council members and judges are elected from veterans of distinguished Federal Service.

**Citizenship & Federal Service**

Society is divided into *Civilians* (most legal rights, no vote, no public office, no subsidized tuition) and *Citizens* (earned through Federal Service). Service lasts a minimum two years — military, hazardous research, frontier construction, or other critical roles. Quitting is made easy, but forfeits the right to ever re-enroll.

The Citizens' Path includes service roles such as:
- *Human Experimentation Programs* — Testing survival equipment or serving as subjects for advanced medical research
- *Terraforming & Frontier Construction* — Hazardous projects developing new colonies or maintaining planetary infrastructure
- *Civil Administration* — Critical non-combat roles in logistics, governance, and education

Upon completing their term, Citizens are granted full voting rights and priority access to public resources. Those who serve 20 years may join the Federation's elite cadre, earning reserved positions in high-ranking civil or military roles.

**Ideological Principles**
- *Service before Privilege:* Only those who have sacrificed can understand what it means to lead.
- *Strength through Unity:* Individual needs subordinate to collective purpose.
- *Merit Above All:* Authority awarded on merit, not birthright or wealth.

**Focused Thought** — The UCF's ideological doctrine, recognizing that education and civic duty shape generations. Enforced through:
- *FedNet* — Primary telecommunications, media, and news network. Monitored to remove "distract-information" and "malinformation."
- *FedWatch* — Cyber security institution enforcing Focused Thought; pursues unlicensed VIs, neutralizes hacks, apprehends troublesome netrunners.
- *Federal Transportation Authority (FTA)* — Transit administration across all Federal systems.
- *Starside R&D* — Coveted research and development positions for high-achieving minds.

**Military**

- *Mobile Infantry (MI)* — Elite shock force in advanced power armor. Blitzkrieg-style attacks deep behind enemy lines. Every soldier is warrior, medic, and engineer. Self-sufficiency is a core principle.
- *Federal Fleet* — A fleet of hunters. Wolfpack tactics combined with boarding actions. **Specter-class** boarding ships use phased disruption fields and magnetic grapples to close fast and board. **Riftbreach Torches** carve open bulkheads in seconds for **Breacher Squads**. Halcyon-class frigates and Wraith-class corvettes provide support and interference.
- *K9 Recon Corps* — Cybernetically enhanced neodogs bonded to handlers. Reconnaissance, tracking, and infiltration far ahead of the main force.
- *Military Intelligence* — Counterintelligence, psychological warfare, and battlefield analysis.

**Relations**
- *Republic of Man:* Seen as a corrupt plutocracy — the antithesis of true meritocracy. Tense, with covert operations and border disputes.
- *Coalition:* Deep historical ties — several founding UCF systems seceded from the Coalition. Cautious diplomacy with unresolved tensions.
- *Novoset Continuum:* Authoritarianism viewed as a betrayal of human freedom. Wary relations, frequent ideological and strategic clashes.

## Major Powers

### Coalition of Systems

![[Coalition of Systems.jpg]]

*Confederacy · Declining former superpower*

The Coalition was one of the original three superpowers following the Savage Wars, but the Wars of Independence stripped it of that status permanently. It remains a confederacy — the **Coalition Chamber** is composed of High Regents from each sector, with its capital rotating to a different settlement every five years. This decentralized structure grants local freedom but leaves it chronically vulnerable to division and slow decision-making.

Today the Coalition faces an existential internal threat: the **Gostic National Realm** (GNR) and its charismatic leader, **the Autarch**, are pulling Confederate systems toward centralized nationalist governance against the resistance of traditional Coalition leadership.

### Merovinist Triumvirate

![[Merovinist Triumvirate.jpg]]
![[Merovinist Triumvirate Icon.jpg]]

*Corporatocracy · Three ruling families*

A corporatocracy led by three related families — the **Carols**, **Merovins**, and **Bosonids** — each governing their own system independently. The Triumvirate has a corporate libertarian philosophy: attract businesses to an environment away from watchful governments, as long as they pay leasing dues to the ruling families. Commerce of all kinds has flourished here, including what is deemed illegal everywhere else.

**VinCorp**, the largest Merovin subsidiary, handles the majority of bureaucratic administration and financial accounting. The [[Cannard Noz System]] is also extraordinarily rich in **lothranium** — the Sector's essential fuel source. Despite the Triumvirate's small size, it has the power to crush empires simply by cutting access to that commodity.

### Principality of Nagan

![[Principality of Nagan.png]]
![[Principality of Nagan Emblem.png]]

*Constitutional Monarchy · Commerce-oriented neutral power*

One of the oldest and smallest major powers in the Sector. Most known for the exportation of raw materials — logging and mining. Since inception under Prince Valla I, Nagan's settlement initiative has focused on targeted colonization of mineral and plant-rich planets, with a regenerative approach to cultivation: one planet devoted to settlement, its neighbor to production, preventing pollution and environmental collapse.

The **Nagan Settlement & Defense Force (NSDF)** is small but highly specialized — dedicated to planet colonization support, trade defense, and protection of human settlements. Nagan is a neutral actor in Sector affairs and trades raw materials to all buyers.

Currently led by **Prince Tarna Vos IV** — elderly, wise, and popular. His two hot-headed sons appear increasingly desperate for power, and the question of succession strains his reign.

### Sinasian Cooperative Axis

![[Sinasian Cooperative Axis.jpg]]

*Single-party state · Centralized technocracy*

The **Vanguard of Perpetual Prosperity (VPP)** exerts absolute control over the **Unity Congress (UC)** — the singular governing body enshrined as the highest organ of state power. All branches of government answer solely to the UC. Elections at every level are orchestrated under VPP grip, with two-thirds of Congress seats allocated to its members. The remaining seats are nominally independent — an illusion of plurality.

The VPP embeds its influence through corporate-style committees and advanced VIs monitoring efficiency and loyalty. The system is efficient but fragile: dissent is stifled, innovation stymied, and corruption festers in the shadows.

### Seven Suns Diktat

![[Seven Suns Diktat.jpg]]

*Formed in the Lyran Veil Crisis · Proud nationalist polity*

The Seven Suns Diktat was forged in crisis, producing a proud people with a clarity of purpose and the will to overcome the division and parliamentary chaos that failed them when tested. It takes its name from the seven suns of its founding systems.

The capital, **Volus**, hosts multiple ore refining complexes, an orbiting star fortress, and one of the Sector's most prestigious lothranium fuel production facilities. **Olus** (orbiting the gas giant Salus) is the sole producer of the exotic **Olusian Lobster**. The moon **Cruor** is a mining outpost rich in natural resources — and in Diktat labor camps.

*Friendly with the Kazeron League.*

### Astralov Crown Systems

![[Astralov Crown Systems.png]]

*Monarchical offshoot of the Sovolian Dominion*

A monarchy rooted in tradition and court intrigue. At its helm stands the **Kosmodar** — a ruler embodying the divine right of the cosmos, descended from royal exiles who fled the collapse of the Sovolian Dominion before it became the Novoset Continuum. Barons are granted significant autonomy in exchange for unwavering loyalty — but their ambitions fuel relentless court intrigue. The monarchy projects strength and stability, but beneath that facade lies fragility: a deranged Kosmodar could lead to outsized societal decay.

### Auric Compact

![[Auric Compact.jpg]]

*Economic alliance of city-state systems · Founded 1924 AE via the Golden Concord*

A formidable alliance bound by trade, mutual defense, and economic ambition. While not a superpower, the Compact's strategic control of trade routes and exclusive access to **Solvite** — a rare luminescent alloy vital for advanced circuitry and Gulf-drive stabilization — gives it influence far beyond its modest territorial holdings.

The Compact's private fleet, the **Aurentillians**, is renowned for heavily automated drone swarms and sleek, deadly hulls. Governed by the **Golden Assembly** — an oligarchic council on Midas Prime where votes are proportional to economic output. Trade power translates directly into political power.

### Gostic National Realm (GNR)

![[Gostic National Realm.png]]

*Nationalist movement within Coalition space · The Autarch's power base*

The GNR is a proud nationalist renaissance championing Gostic blood, heritage, and shared destiny. Forged from centuries of stellar navigation tradition and iron resolve, the GNR envisions a renewed "National Realm" where every Gostic stands tall — resilient, uncorrupted, bound by common purpose.

Though formally nested within the Coalition, the GNR holds sway over vital Gostic heartlands: **Nagrindr** (industrial powerhouse and spiritual seat), **Maldrecoy** (cradle of strand-fleets and heavy manufactories), and **Arcea** (ancestral garden-world with academies preserving cultural lineage).

*Slogans: "One Blood. One Realm. One Eternal Star." · "Gostica Awakens."*

**Leadership:** At the apex stands the **Autarch** — charismatic steward of Gostic renewal, revered as the living embodiment of national will. Beneath him, the semi-legal **Stellaroberkommando (StOK)** serves as high council and overseer: regional commissioners, cultural archivists, and anti-corruption investigators.

The StOK commands:
- **Gostic Stelwehr** ("Star-Defense") — unified national defense encompassing the Voidsturmkorps (VSK, elite void assault), Sternenfeldtruppen (SFT, regular ground), Kosflotte (strand-capable fleet), and Astralstreitkräfte (aerospace wings)
- **Sicheramt (SA)** — discreet internal security rooting out graft, foreign influence, and decadence

### Manichaean Republic of Athran's Cradle (MRAC)

![[Manichaean Republic of Athran's Cradle.jpg]]

*Theocratic revolutionary state*

The MRAC emerged from a fiery religious revolution following Unity Day, when the Eight Founding Cities of Athran's Cradle — long exploited as an industrial breadbasket — rose against Republic administration and its megacorporate allies as manifestations of cosmic darkness.

The trigger was the **Phanex Massacre**: delegates convening secretly on orbital station Phanex to ratify a declaration of faith-guided sovereignty were killed in a catastrophic explosion. Surviving clerics and citizens believed it a deliberate strike. The Eight Cities immediately proclaimed the Manichaean Republic and expelled Republic forces.

Led by the **Barzor Prime** — paramount interpreter of the Dualist Path — whose edicts are tempered by the **Diwan-e Barzor**, a council of senior theologians and veteran commanders. Strategic industries fall under state-religious trusts. Service in the **Manichaean Defense Forces** or the elite **Zul'narq Corps** is mandatory for the able-bodied.

The **Seven Moons system** is the unassailable heart of the Republic — a fortified industrial and spiritual chokepoint where the **Sanctum of Rha'zul** rises as capital and beacon.

### States of Juno

![[States of Juno.png]]

*Secular pluralist federation · Frontier-focused*

The States of Juno emerged as a defiant oasis of secular pluralism in the aftermath of Athran's Cradle's theocratic revolution. While the cluster's core worlds embraced Manichaean fervor, the outer systems — populated by refugees, prospectors, and freethinkers — chose a radically different course: individual liberty, multicultural coexistence, and relentless outward expansion.

Governed by a unicameral **Assembly** (proportional representation, coalition governments, a powerful Prime Minister, and a largely ceremonial President). A robust bill of rights enshrines secular freedoms; an independent judiciary protects minority voices.

Every citizen must complete **mandatory Frontier Service** — two years on an unsettled frontier world, exploration vessel, or deep-void outpost. The result is a society of hardened innovators. Economy surges on diversified trade, cutting-edge R&D, and innovative agro-domes. The **Juno Defense Forces** are renowned for improvisation and asymmetric void warfare.

Though the Manichaean Republic dominates the industrial core, the States of Juno command the outer volumes and vital trade lanes.

*Drone-dominated technocracy · Lament Star system*

The Technate of Odnu is shrouded in the flickering brilliance of the **Lament Star** — a celestial anomaly whose unstable luminosity and erratic radiation affect both the environment and the psyche of those who dwell beneath it. Electronics must be shielded against its electromagnetic surges. The culture embraces unpredictability as cosmic truth.

Few dare challenge the Technate — not for fear of its sparse human population, but for the unsettling swarm of drones and bots that vastly outnumber them. **Lament Sentinels** (humanoid security drones with seamless synthetic skin and lifeless eyes) and **Harvest Scribes** (arachnid bots carving farming patterns with hyper-efficient precision) embody a discomforting uncanny valley.

Governed by the **Temporal Architects** — veiled in secrecy, issuing cryptic decrees through the **Temporal Pact** based on complex simulations of potential futures. Their rule is authoritarian yet quietly revered; their perceived prophetic authority goes largely unchallenged.

### United Systems (USC)

![[The Unified Systems of Cressa.jpg]]
![[Cressa.jpg]]
![[Gamarii.jpg]]

*Democratic alliance · Isolationist*

The **Unified Systems of Cressa** is a union of democratic states organized for mutual economic and security benefits. **Cressa** is the founding member and administrative capital; the union also includes the **Callwood United Peoples**, the **Lanyan Republic**, and **Gamarii**.

The USC pursues broadly isolationist policies — military efforts focus on colonization within immediate systems and mutual defense of member territories. The standard of living within the USC is among the highest in the Sector. This success has attracted the hostile attention of larger powers: the Republic of Man in particular conducts ongoing subterfuge, sabotage, and espionage operations against it.

Currently led by **Chancellor Vero Wynn** (from Callwood) in his first of potentially three 10-year terms, alongside **President Trista Yeung** of Cressa (in her fourth 5-year term as Cressa's head of state).

*Member: **Gamarii** — A small participatory democracy of 500,000 that overthrew its dictatorship a century ago. Governance by weekly citizen assembly; decisions require a highly educated voting bloc. Deep investment in eugenics and genetic engineering to sustain this model. No military — defense guaranteed by the USC in exchange for technological advancements and open borders.*

### Vrexian Union

![[Vxrexian Union.jpg]]

*Reclamation state · Rapidly growing*

The VU is the relatively new scion of the fallen **Vrexian Empire** — a long-dead polity tracing its origins to the first ships that reached the Sector, believed to have been wiped out by the Savages long before the Republic's rise. The Union slowly reclaims the husk of that former empire, rapidly growing into a Sector power and restoring its heritage.

Their liberal use of barely-legal AI and "unsanctioned" privateering has created several enemies — but their openness to trade and collaboration has earned them sizeable backing from other polities.

## Independent Systems

Independent, or "unpeopled," spaces exist throughout the Sector. The Antevic Sector has more systems than can be reasonably settled with the current population. These systems are usually not officially controlled by any nation and have only a small or semi-permanent population. Some are more governed than others. Leadership varies.
