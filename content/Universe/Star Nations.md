## Classification Methodology

### Superpowers

The methodology for classifying nations as superpowers, as outlined in this document, combines established criteria with the ASTINT analysis, incorporating both historical and contemporary qualifications. These include:

- **Super extensive** astropolitical influence and desire to assert such influence
- **Dominant** military capabilities with interstellar operational reach
- A **prospering/robust** and **diversified** economy
- **Advanced** technological prowess
- **Compellingly Strong Sector-wide** diplomatic and cultural influence also considered
- Number of undisputed territorial claims on star systems also considered

### Major Powers

- **Extensive** astropolitical influence and desire to assert such influence
- **Adequate** military capabilities with interstellar operational reach
- A **prospering/robust** or **diversified** economy
- **Above Average** technological prowess
- **Strong extra-regional** diplomatic and cultural influence also considered
- Number of undisputed territorial claims on star systems also considered

### Minor Powers

- **Limited** astropolitical influence and desire to assert such influence
- **Minimal** military capabilities with interstellar operational reach
- A **narrow** economy
- **Common** technological prowess
- **Restricted** diplomatic and cultural influence also considered
- Number of undisputed territorial claims on star systems also considered

*Note* — Minor powers may have some criteria that a super power or major power has, but it still falls into a minor power categorization.

## Superpowers

### Novoset Continuum

![[Novoset Continuum.jpg]]

> **Government Type:** Novosyan Secretariat Sovnarate (NSS)
> **Founding Documents:** The Accords of Sovnarate
> **Capital:** Sovol, Novoset System

#### Overview

The Novoset Continuum stands as a grim monument to efficiency, sacrifice, and control. Born in 1330AE, from the cadaver of the Sovolian Dominion, which some argue was murdered during the Savage Wars from the Continuists themselves and not the Savages, the Continuum faced looming annihilation and forged itself into a state driven by collective survival above all else. Its citizens live under constant surveillance, their lives meticulously planned and optimized by the Novosyan Secretariat Sovnarate (NSS) — a technocratic council that views human society as a machine needing constant refinement.

What began as an experiment in centralized governance and resource management soon evolved into a rigid, authoritarian state with the motto: "*Order Above All*". Citizens are stripped of individual ambition in favor of collective advancement. The Continuum prides itself on its unrivaled logistical prowess and mobilization capacity, but this success comes at a steep cost — dissent, imagined or real, is met with swift, often brutal suppression, and innovation is stifled by a suffocating bureaucracy.

The Continuum's flag displays a white circle against a black field, symbolizing infinite unity, industry and the technological mastery necessary to thrive in a dark hostile universe. The blue represents the strip of flesh torn from the Sovolian Dominion in the Good Revolution, the white the purity of the same Revolution, and the crimson symbolizes the blood of the workers and low class that was shed. Beneath the flag's stark aesthetics lies a society of contradictions: stability and stagnation, order and oppression, collective strength and personal sacrifice.

#### Leadership & Organization

All power in the Novoset Continuum flows through the **NSS**, a supreme directive council housed in the capital city of **Sovol**. The NSS controls every aspect of governance, from military operations and resource allocation to cultural directives.

- **The Sovnarate:** The NSS is composed of **commissars and technocrats**, selected through an exhaustive process of ideological vetting and expertise in fields critical to the Continuum's survival — logistics, resource management, military strategy, and interstellar navigation. These leaders are not elected but chosen for their loyalty and adherence to Continuum principles.

- **Regional Councils:** Each star system within the Continuum is governed by a regional council that submits directly to the NSS. These councils oversee local resources and population management, ensuring seamless execution of state mandates.

Despite its efficient surface, the Continuum's leadership suffers from **rampant internal corruption**. Regional leaders often manipulate bureaucratic systems for personal gain, and while the NSS enforces harsh punishments for corruption, it remains deeply embedded in the state's structure.

The **Director-General**, elected from within the Sovnarate, serves as the face of the Continuum. This figure is both a political leader and a military commander, wielding near-absolute power during times of crisis.

#### Ideological Principles

The Continuum is built on the core belief that **individual sacrifice ensures collective survival**. Its philosophy is rooted in pragmatism, viewing society as a complex machine that must be constantly maintained, optimized, and — when necessary — purged of inefficiencies.

- **Unity Through Sacrifice:** The individual is secondary to the state. Every citizen has a role, and fulfilling that role without deviation is the highest virtue.
- **Technological Mastery:** The Continuum invests heavily in cybernetics, virtual intelligence (within strict parameters), and automation to maintain its edge. Advanced neural interfaces are mandatory for military personnel and many high-ranking bureaucrats, connecting them directly to the state's vast information network, to enhance and control.
- **Survival Above All:** The Continuum prioritizes resource efficiency and strategic advantage, often exploiting entire star systems for their raw materials, regardless of ecological consequences.

#### The Continuum Society

The population of the Continuum is divided into **three primary classes**: **Labor Units, Citizen-Technocrats,** and the **Sovnarate Elite**.

1. **Labor Units** make up the majority and live regimented, tightly controlled lives. Their education is functional, designed to prepare them for their assigned roles in industry, agriculture, or military service.
2. **Citizen-Technocrats** enjoy greater privileges and autonomy, managing complex systems and acting as the intellectual core of the state. Many undergo **cybernetic enhancements** to increase efficiency.
3. **The Sovnarate Elite** are the political and military leaders, living in isolated luxury but under constant scrutiny from their peers. Their lives are highly controlled, and failure often leads to sudden, unexplained disappearances.

Class mobility in the Novoset Continuum is technically possible but heavily regulated by the **Merit Assessment Bureau (MAB)**, which evaluates citizens through rigorous exams, productivity metrics, and loyalty audits. Exceptional performance or innovation can elevate a Labor Unit to Citizen-Technocrat status, while failure or dissent can see even Sovnarate Elite demoted to menial roles — or worse. Though the state promotes the idea of mobility as a reward for loyalty, in practice, it's rare and often politically motivated.

#### The Cyberapparat

The Novoset Continuum employs a **vast surveillance network** known as **Cyberapparat** to monitor its citizens. Every piece of data — personal communications, work productivity, even health metrics — is collected and analyzed by advanced algorithms. The **NSS Central Archives** act as the beating heart of this system, predicting and controlling societal behavior through predictive models.

- **Data-Scribes:** These cybernetic bureaucrats oversee the Cyberapparat, interfacing directly with the network to interpret and execute state policy.
- **Neural Enforcers:** Elite military units equipped with neural uplinks and enhanced reflexes, tasked with suppressing dissent and ensuring loyalty.
- **The Shadow Bureau:** A secretive branch of the NSS that investigates internal threats and eliminates them with ruthless efficiency.

#### Military & Strategic Doctrine

The Novoset Continuum's military is a fearsome and relentless machine, blending overwhelming force with technological precision. Its doctrine revolves around **total control, mass mobilization, and asymmetrical warfare**, ensuring that the Continuum can respond to any threat with brutal efficiency. Heavily reliant on **cybernetic integration and predictive warfare**, the Continuum fields one of the galaxy's most disciplined and adaptive fighting forces.

- **Infinite Host:** The Infinite Host is the Continuum's ground force — a vast, mechanized army built on **quantity and adaptability**. Using mass-mobilization tactics, the Host fields **cybernetically enhanced infantry**, mechanized armor columns, and drone swarms. Soldiers are connected via neural uplinks, receiving real-time tactical data from battlefield overseers, making them devastatingly coordinated in battle. The Host specializes in **siege warfare, planetary occupation, and urban pacification**, deploying overwhelming force to entrench its control and convert occupied worlds into resource hubs or if the population is unwilling, graveyards.

- **Red Fleet:** The backbone of the Continuum's power projection, its doctrine emphasizes **attrition via overwhelming saturation attacks** at extreme range, using wave after wave of multi-role missiles to smother enemy defenses and **disruption via electronic warfare** using advanced ECM suites to cripple enemy fleets and disrupt communications. It maintains **massive battlecruisers and stealth corvettes** that play into these roles. Capital ships launch salvos of **Perun Heavy Torpedoes** and **Stormpiercer Barrage Missiles**, creating chaotic kill zones that force enemy fleets to close the distance or be torn apart from afar. Unlike the Republic's precision-focused tactics, the Red Fleet thrives on attrition warfare, grinding down opponents in a brutal contest of endurance and firepower.

- **Continuum Corps of Engineers:** The Continuum Corps of Engineers is a paramilitary organization specializing in **planetary transformation and fortification**. Embodying a culture so focused on infrastructure and industrial strength, the Corps deploys **terraforming teams, industrial drones, and mobile construction platforms** to rapidly convert or rebuild newly acquired worlds into fortified strongholds or resource hubs. The Corps is also responsible for building **orbital defense arrays, subterranean bunkers, and planetary shielding systems**, ensuring that once a planet falls under the Continuum's control, it becomes a nearly impregnable fortress.

The Novoset Continuum's strategy blends **deep-battle concepts** with **total war philosophy**, aiming to **break enemy morale and secure long-term dominance**. It relies on rapid, multi-layered offensives, with the Infinite Host overwhelming planetary defenses while the Red Fleet secures space control, all **irregardless of casualties**. Once victory is assured, the Continuum Corps of Engineers transforms the battlefield into an asset, ensuring that no victory is temporary. **War is a tool of reshaping reality**, and in the Continuum's eyes, every conquered world is another piece in their grand design for eternal survival.

#### Relations with Other Superpowers

- **Republic of Man:** The Continuum sees the Republic as decadent and dangerously inefficient. It views the Republic's oligarchic structure as inherently unstable, a relic of a bygone era destined to collapse under the weight of its corruption. Relations are cold, marked by frequent border skirmishes and espionage.
- **United Citizen Federation:** The Federation's meritocratic ideals are a direct challenge to the Continuum's collectivist philosophy. While both powers respect discipline and order, they are fundamentally opposed in how society should be structured — leading to tense diplomatic standoffs and proxy wars in contested systems.

### Republic of Man

![[Republic of Man Battle Flag.jpg]]
![[Republic of Man Peace Flag.jpg]]
![[Republic of Man Crest Emblem - large.png]]
![[Republic of Man Crest Emblem - small.jpg]]

> **Government Type:** Oligarchic Republic
> **Founding Documents:** Constitution of the Republic
> **Capital:** Victoria Centrum, Alexandria

#### Overview

The Republic of Man was formed in 777AE, and is generally credited with pulling humanity out of the Decline, but its history, systems and culture date back to around 600AE. It is the oldest of the three supereminents or superpowers in the Sector. Though that is not to say that it is without regional galactic competition or a monolith without internal faction strife.

The Republic of Man's battle flag has a widened light ray compared to the peacetime flag. The flag comprises the Rays of Humanity, the twelve stars of the founding core systems, and the Lady of Peace, one at her side in peace while the other wields the "Sword of Lex", the law, to protect the citizens. Under the Republic banner, countless worlds prosper, and ultimate peace is kept for those in the core worlds. The black field symbolises space, noting the Republic's mission in bringing the Rays of Humanity to all.

#### Leadership & Organization

The Republic of Man is just that - a republic - it is by no means a democracy. Centuries of political monetization, nepotism, and revolving-door uprisings have forged a state based entirely on the concept of concentrating power in the hands of the few. While all citizens have a vote, the less affluent systems are less influential than their wealthier counterparts by design - and of course, the poorest citizens on these systems are themselves deliberately disenfranchised as well. In summation, the Republic can best be described as an Oligarchic Republic or a Constitutional Oligarchy - for while power is concentrated in the hands of the few, there are a number of powerful safeguards that prevent power from being concentrated in the hands of the one.

#### Popular Assembly

The "lowest" tier in the political ladder is that of the House of Reason. A body of almost two thousand members, the House is meant to be a public forum for the airing of local grievances and the sole voice of the poorest citizens. It is split into two "Congresses," a Congress of the People and a Congress of the Citizens. The Congress of the People is the closest the Republic gets to true democracy, where representatives are elected from constituencies purely based on population rather than wealth or status.

The Congress of Citizens, in contrast, is a group of 200 representatives divided among the four economic classes recognized as citizens in the Republic - the ancient Patrician families receive 20 seats, the fabulously wealthy Partisans receive 80 seats, the middle-class Risers are awarded 80 as well, and the Commoners - the majority of the population - are given 20. This has resulted in a body that concentrates representation in the hands of the wealthy and powerful - and it is no accident that this body is responsible for electing the members of the Senate.

#### The Senate

While both houses of the House of Reason are capable of proposing and voting on legislation, the Senate - the primary legislative organ of the Republic - is the final arbiter of all of it. Should the Senate vote against a draft of legislation passed by either of the Congresses, that is the end of it. It is also charged with electing the two Synarchs of the Republic, appointing Governors-Prime (sovereigns of the Republic's vast territories), and appointing military flag officers known as high commanders. Senators must be members of the Patrician or Partisan classes, and serve for life. They are sacrosanct under the laws of the Republic's state religion, and each Senator is awarded a personal guard of 50 palatine vestals for protection.

#### The Executive

The Senate elects the Synarchs from among their number, the two co-Heads of the Republic of Man. To be a Synarch is the greatest honor a Senator can be awarded, and after their two-year term is up, they occupy positions of great respect among their peers, and can be given positions as Governors-Prime. The Synarchs, as the chairpeople of the Senate, can not only convene, set the agenda for, and dismiss the Senate, they can also propose their own legislation for debate as well, and each one has a veto that cannot be overridden (although it is not used as often as one would think, as the other co-Synarch has equal powers of veto and veto-based brinkmanship leads to a waste of both of their terms). The Senate can, in times of emergency, pass what is known as the "final enabling act," where the Synarchs are - for a term of six months - awarded a temporary dictatorship. They are empowered to do whatever is necessary to defend the Republic, with no true checks on their power. However, this is considered very dangerous and has no precedent.

The Synarchs share power with the Ordinatorium, the Republic's collegiate head of government. Six Ordinators, each one in charge of a different "ministry," head up the bureaucracy and execute the directives of the Senate. The position of First Ordinator, a first among equals position, is rotated on a yearly basis; during a Ordinator's six-year term, each Ordinator will serve for one year. The Ordinatorium is elected directly by the Congress of Citizens, and unlike the Synarchs, they do not have to be Senators.

This structure, along with its other trappings such as the integration of the state religion and the power of the military, have created a government that quashes dissent from the mainstream political opinion and favors the interests of the wealthy. In this era of growing threats, this system will be tested - and with populism on the rise among the Commoners and Risers, there has been more and more alignment with Megacorps to keep things orderly.

#### The Oldest Society and Mobility

The Republic of Man, as the oldest continuous state in the galaxy, prides itself on its legacy of stability, culture, and personal freedom — at least for those within its core worlds. Unlike the rigid control of the Novoset Continuum or the meritocratic militarism of the United Citizen Federation, the Republic offers its citizens a surprising degree of autonomy in their personal lives. Censorship is minimal, with diverse media outlets, arts, and philosophies flourishing in the urban centers of the Republic's core systems.

However, this veneer of freedom masks a deeply stratified society where wealth and influence dictate opportunity. The ancient Patrician families hold immense power, tracing their lineage back to the Republic's earliest days, while the fabulously wealthy Partisans dominate commerce and industry. Middle-class Risers enjoy modest success, but the vast majority of Commoners find themselves locked out of the Republic's upper echelons, despite official rhetoric promoting social mobility.

The Republic's state religion plays a central role in daily life, reinforcing a sense of unity and purpose. While not mandatory, participation in religious ceremonies is strongly encouraged, and many of the Republic's core values — duty, honor, and prosperity through order — are rooted in its spiritual teachings. Citizens often celebrate public holidays with grand festivals, while monuments to the Republic's heroes stand in every city, reminding all of the sacrifices made to protect the Republic's prosperity.

Despite its flaws, the Republic remains a beacon of opportunity where the rewards are unparalleled for those with the ambition and cunning to navigate the complex web of political alliances, business monopolies, and patronage systems.

#### Military & Strategic Doctrine

The Republic Military is the oldest in the galaxy, and with that it comes with advantages and disadvantages. It is made up of:

- **The Republic Guard (RG):** The Republic Guard serves as the primary planetary defense and expeditionary force, modeled on a doctrine of **rapid deployment and overwhelming ground presence**. It emphasizes **combined-arms operations** with an unmatched logistics network to sustain long-term campaigns across multiple worlds. The mainline RG excels at **peacekeeping** and **large-scale planetary invasions**, supported by elite airborne divisions and mechanized infantry brigades. While its focus is planetary operations, the Guard remains highly adaptable, with specialized divisions trained for urban combat, jungle warfare, and desert campaigns.

- **Republic Armada:** The Republic Armada is the largest and most technologically advanced navy in the Sector, prioritizing power projection and space control. It maintains **carrier strike groups** and **ballistic missile platforms**, enabling deep-space dominance and precision strikes and orbital bombardment. The Armada's doctrine revolves around **strategic deterrence and force projection**, securing trade routes and responding to threats with overwhelming naval firepower. Its fleet is highly flexible, capable of engaging in high-intensity conflicts while maintaining a constant presence across contested sectors.

- **Republic Tactical Assault Corps (RTAC):** An elite, semi-autonomous military branch originally designed to combat the Savage threat. RTAC units are highly specialized, operating independently in small, agile strike groups. They are trained for rapid, high-risk operations, including **deep-strike assaults, counterinsurgency, black ops missions, and high-value target elimination**. RTAC's authority allows them to **bypass traditional military command structures**, answering directly to the Senate and the Synarchs. This autonomy makes RTAC both feared and respected across the Republic.

The Republic's military strategy blends deterrence with **securing borders** and **crushing rebellion with decisive, overwhelming force**. The Armada **holds space superiority**, enforcing containment and blockade tactics, while the Guard **consolidates gains through fortification and control via garrisoning population centers**. This layered strategy ensures the Republic can both expand and defend its holdings, relying on RTAC for swift punitive strikes against any emerging threats.

#### Article 22

Article 22 is a statute in the Republic Constitution that allows [[RTAC (Paladins)|RTAC]] to assume control of the government for up to six months in the event of an emergency. Executive Tower and House of Reason delegates can be tried in court and the Senate can be dissolved while individual worlds elect new senators in their place.

#### Relations with Other Superpowers

- **Novoset Continuum:** The Republic views the Continuum as an existential threat, a dystopian experiment that must be contained. Relations are hostile, with both sides engaging in cyber warfare and propaganda campaigns to destabilize the other.
- **United Citizen Federation:** The Republic considers the Federation a rogue offshoot that betrayed its heritage. While open conflict is rare, diplomatic relations are strained, and both powers jockey for influence over neutral systems.

### United Citizen Federation

![[The Federation.jpg]]

> **Government Type:** Federal Parliamentary Republic
> **Founding Documents:** The Covenant of Service
> **Capital:** Solis Prime, Hespera System

#### Overview

The United Citizen Federation (UCF) is the youngest of the three superpowers, which rose by uniting a collapsing and rebelling group of colonial administrations 33 years after the end of the Savage Wars during the Wars of Independence in 1444AE. As widespread chaos threatened the systems, a federation of military veterans and civic leaders formed a military junta and seized control, very violently in some cases. But, in an act of contrition for those lost, they enacted The Covenant of Service — a unifying doctrine built on the principles of shared sacrifice, personal responsibility, and martial order. The Federation's motto, "*For the Federation, We Serve,*" reflects its emphasis on duty and unity. Some of its founding systems are breakaway colonies from the then newly formed Republic of Man and fragmented Coalition, while others were independent systems, all seeking a new path founded on egalitarian principles and military-led governance.

Unlike the Republic of Man, which consolidates power among wealthy elites and ancient families, the UCF grants citizenship only to those who complete a term of Federal Service. Citizenship is not an inherited right but something earned through sacrifice and service, ensuring that only those with a proven commitment to the Federation's ideals hold positions of power.

The Federation is a Federal Parliamentary Republic as it is ruled by the authority of the Federal Council in all matters except those directly related to the military.

The red at the bottom of the flag of the Federation symbolizes the foundational blood made of the defenders of the nation, while the white line is the barrier between the past and the present. The twenty stars represent the twenty systems and the eight sided flake represents the eight regions made up of these systems (such as Pacifa and Carcardion).

#### Leadership & Organization

The United Citizen Federation is structured around three core branches:

1. **Federal Armed Services (FAS)** — The executive arm, responsible for defense and enforcement of federal policies. It consists of multiple branches, including the Mobile Infantry, Fleet, and Military Intelligence. The **Sky Marshal**, elected by the Federal Council, serves as the Commander-in-Chief of the Federal Armed Services, wielding true decision making power over military operations and soft-influence over civil operations.
2. **Federal Council** — The legislative body, composed of representatives selected from the pool of national governors within each star system under the jurisdiction of the Federation. Each Council member is able to cast a single vote pertaining to all matters directly affecting the Federation. The Council debates and enacts policies affecting all aspects of the Federation. During conflict the Sky Marshal provides the weekly reports on the military situation.
3. **Federal Court** — The judicial branch, ensuring that laws are upheld and treason against the Federation is dealt with swiftly. High-ranking officers and civilian officials can face harsh penalties for failures in duty, reinforcing the culture of accountability.

Each branch operates under the principle that leadership must be earned, not inherited. For instance, Council members and Judges are elected from the ranks of veterans who have completed distinguished Federal Service. And more extremely, every Sky Marshal must rise through the ranks of both the Mobile Infantry and the Fleet before being eligible for election. This ensures that all leaders have firsthand experience in defending the Federation and understanding its challenges.

#### Citizenship & Federal Service

In the UCF, society is divided into two groups: **Civilians** and **Citizens**.

- **Civilians** possess most of the same legal rights as Citizens, except for the right to vote, hold public office and government-subsidized tuition fees.
- **Citizens** earn their status by completing a voluntary term of Federal Service, which typically lasts two years but may be extended during times of conflict.

Federal Service is rigorous by design. It can involve military duty, hazardous scientific research, frontier construction, or other critical roles. Every applicant is thoroughly assessed to ensure they understand the oath they take. Even those with severe physical disabilities can serve in specially designed roles, ensuring that almost no one is excluded from the chance to earn citizenship only if a psychiatrist decides one cannot understand the oath.

Quitting a term of service before completion (even during war time) is made to be as simple as possible, but once one quits, they are never allowed to enroll again. This ensures dedication while also discouraging wash outs.

#### Ideological Principles

The UCF promotes a collectivist ideology centered around shared responsibility, civic virtue, and personal accountability. Its core belief is that society is best governed by those who have proven their commitment to the greater good.

- **Service before Privilege:** Only those who have served and sacrificed can understand what it means to lead.
- **Strength through Unity:** The Federation thrives on collective action and shared purpose, with individual needs subordinate to the state's goals.
- **Merit Above All:** Positions of authority are awarded based on merit, not birthright or wealth.

#### Military & Strategy

The Federal Armed Services (FAS) are the backbone of the UCF's strength, driven by a military ethos that prizes discipline, adaptability, and decisive action. Though the youngest and smallest of the superpowers, the UCF's military doctrine **emphasizes quality over quantity**, relying on elite, highly trained forces **capable of achieving strategic victories against larger foes**. Every soldier is a combatant first, and **self-sufficiency is a core principle** — small units are expected to operate independently for extended periods without support. Unlike the overwhelming force tactics of the Novoset Continuum or the precision-driven strategy of the Republic of Man, the UCF prefers swift, surgical strikes and sustained asymmetric warfare.

- **Mobile Infantry (MI):** The spearhead of the UCF's military, the Mobile Infantry is an elite shock force specializing in rapid deployment and high-intensity combat. Clad in advanced power armor, MI soldiers are highly mobile, able to speed across battlefields and withstand extreme environments. Known for their resilience and small-unit autonomy, they excel in blitzkrieg-style attacks, often striking deep behind enemy lines to disrupt logistics and command structures. The MI embodies the UCF's core philosophy: **every soldier is a warrior, medic, and engineer in one**, capable of surviving and fighting even when cut off from reinforcements.

- **Federal Fleet:** The Federal Fleet is a fleet of hunters — small, fast, and deadly. Lacking the capital ship dominance of the Republic or the long-range saturation power of the Red Fleet, the UCF instead employs **wolfpack tactics** combined with **boarding actions** to devastating effect. Specialized **Specter-class boarding ships**, equipped with **phased disruption fields** and **magnetic grapples**, form the vanguard of the Fleet's engagements, turning space battles into brutal infantry clashes inside enemy hulls.

  The **phased disruption field** is a cutting-edge stealth technology that distorts local electromagnetic signatures, making Specter-class ships nearly invisible to conventional sensors for short periods by flooding their sensors with false readings and temporary blind spots and confusing enemy targeting systems — buying precious minutes to close the gap. Once within range, **magnetic grapples** — advanced rail-guided projectiles — fire at enemy hulls, locking onto and retracting the Specter ships at incredible speeds, drastically reducing the vulnerable approach time.

  Upon boarding, **Riftbreach Torches** carve open bulkheads in seconds, allowing **Breacher Squads** to flood enemy corridors. These elite Mobile Infantry units move with surgical precision, sowing chaos and confusion. The psychological terror of the UCF boarding tactics is as much a weapon as their technology. Survivors often speak of the "whispering hum" of Specters attaching to their hulls, a prelude to death or capture, paralyzing enemy morale.

  Meanwhile, supporting the Specters, **Halcyon-class frigates** provide heavy suppression fire, while **Wraith-class corvettes** run interference and launch **counter-sensor bursts**, ensuring the wolfpack remains a shadow until it's too late.

- **K9 Recon Corps:** A unique UCF branch, the K9 Recon Corps employs cybernetically enhanced **neodogs** bonded to their handlers. These units excel at reconnaissance, tracking, and infiltration, often operating far ahead of the main force to provide real-time intelligence. Neodogs are not just tools but trusted partners — trained for loyalty, adaptability, and lethal precision in combat.

- **Military Intelligence:** The eyes and ears of the Federation, Military Intelligence focuses on **counterintelligence, psychological warfare, and battlefield analysis**. Its operatives gather and exploit data to predict enemy movements, sow confusion, and neutralize threats before they materialize. Known for ruthless efficiency, Military Intelligence often works in the shadows, ensuring that no internal or external enemy remains a threat for long.

The UCF's military strategy revolves around **surgical precision** and **relentless pressure**. Unlike the Continuum's mass-mobilization or the Republic's emphasis on strategic deterrence, the Federation relies on **speed, flexibility, and relentless pursuit of high-value targets**. Hit-and-fade tactics, combined with overwhelming force at critical points, allow the UCF to punch above its weight. While every battle is fought to win decisively, the true strength of the Federation lies with each individual soldier — each one trained to adapt, survive, and turn even the bleakest situation into an opportunity for victory.

#### The Citizen's Path

Service is not limited to combat roles. Volunteers can serve in numerous capacities:

- **Human Experimentation Programs:** Testing survival equipment or serving as subjects for advanced medical research.
- **Terraforming & Frontier Construction:** Working on hazardous projects to develop new colonies or maintain planetary infrastructure.
- **Civil Administration:** Filling critical non-combat roles in logistics, governance, and education.

Upon completing their term, Citizens are granted full voting rights and priority access to public resources, including education, healthcare, and business licenses. Those who choose to remain in service for 20 years may become part of the Federation's elite cadre, earning "reserved jobs" in high-ranking civil or military positions.

#### Focused Thought

One of the theories the UCF operates under is known as "Focused Thought" which is the embodiment of the recognition that education and civic duty shapes future generations. Focused Thought is instilled into society via other agencies and technologies.

**FedWatch:** Originally founded an Extranet security company to combat rampant Republic propaganda, its success quickly transformed it into an institution. With the rise of FedNet, these cyber experts specialize in enforcing Focused Thought, securing data fortresses, pursuing unlicensed VIs, neutralizing hacks, removing software threats and apprehending troublesome cyber runners. Though it's known, the moment one steps over the line, some eager rookie in the nearest FedWatch division will have a file on you in no time.

**FedNet:** The primary computer, telecommunications, media, and news network in the Federation. This service is monitored by FedWatch and which drives Focused Thought through removing "distract-information" and "malinformation."

**Federal Transportation Authority (FTA):** Transport administration across all Federal systems and colonies, a Federal Service position that many "breezers" want to join due to it being typically the one of the easiest.

**Starside R&D:** A research and development agency. Highly coveted Federal Service positions are often filled by high achieving and curious minds.

#### Culture & Daily Life

Life in the Federation is structured and purposeful. Laws are few but strictly enforced. While taxes are low, citizens are expected to contribute to society through hard work and self-discipline. Crime is rare, as harsh penalties ensure order.

Education plays a vital role in shaping future generations, with a focus on history, moral philosophy, and civic duty. The Federation's media emphasizes unity, sacrifice, and the honor of service. Heroes of the Federation are celebrated, their stories immortalized to inspire future generations.

#### Relations with Other Powers

- **Republic of Man:** Once a source of many breakaway systems that formed the Federation, the Republic is now seen as a corrupt plutocracy. The Federation views its elitist government as the antithesis of true meritocracy, often clashing diplomatically over border disputes and political influence in neutral systems. Thus, relations are tense, marked by covert operations and diplomatic feints.
- **Coalition:** Though fractured and weakened, the Coalition still shares deep historical ties with the Federation. Several of the Federation's founding systems seceded from the Coalition during its period of decline. While cautious diplomacy exists, tensions remain due to differing political philosophies and unresolved claims over shared colonies.
- **Novoset Continuum:** The Federation respects the Continuum's discipline but views its authoritarianism as a betrayal of human freedom. Relations are wary, with both powers frequently clashing over ideological differences and strategic resources.

United by shared revulsion toward superpower exploitation and ideological absolutism, these habitats rejected both lingering Republic claims and the rising theocratic tide, forging a polity devoted to individual liberty, multicultural coexistence, and relentless outward expansion.

The flag's colors embody this ethos: vibrant orange for the boundless optimism of the first settlers carving new lives from raw void, pristine white for the clean slate of a society unburdened by old dogmas, and steadfast blue for the enduring perseverance that has carried the nation through blockade, embargo, and proxy skirmish. The crescent moon evokes the quiet birth of a sovereign people emerging from eclipse, while the eight-pointed star — radiating in all directions — signifies both the rise of a new stellar power and the unbreakable unity of the many ethnic enclaves that call the States of Juno home.

#### Leadership & Organization

The States of Juno are governed by a unicameral Assembly elected through proportional representation from planetary and habitat districts, producing coalition governments led by a powerful Prime Minister. A largely ceremonial President serves as head of state and moral compass. A robust bill of rights enshrines freedom of expression, assembly, and belief — deliberately secular — while an independent judiciary protects minority voices against majority overreach.

What sets the Juno polity apart from any known real-world model is the mandatory Frontier Service: every citizen, upon reaching adulthood, must complete a two-year term on an unsettled frontier world, exploration vessel, or deep-void outpost. This universal rite blends military training, scientific research, and pioneering labor, ensuring that every voter, lawmaker, and entrepreneur has firsthand experience of the lethal beauty of raw space. The result is a society of hardened innovators whose high-tech startups and rapid-prototyping culture are fueled by memories of vacuum, radiation, and the thrill of first footfall.

Economy surges on diversified trade, cutting-edge R&D, and innovative agro-domes; private enterprise thrives with light regulation, though strategic resources and frontier claims remain under federal oversight to prevent foreign domination. The conscript-based Juno Defense Forces — supplemented by veteran reservists from every walk of life — are renowned for improvisation, asymmetric void warfare, and effective diplomatic interventions.

Though the Manichaean Republic dominates the industrial core and old superpowers nurse lingering enclaves, the States of Juno command the outer volumes and vital trade lanes — an adaptable, ever-expanding federation where old-world cultures mingle freely under station lights, ever watchful yet ever hopeful beneath the orange-and-blue banner that flutters above New Dawn Spire.

### Technate of Odnu

![[Technate of Odnu.jpg]]

> **Government Type:** Temporal Conclave
> **Founding Documents:** The Temporal Pact
> **Capital:** Nornwell, Odnu System

#### Overview

The Technate of Odnu is an enigma shrouded in the flickering brilliance of the Lament Star, a celestial anomaly at the heart of the Odnu System. The Lament Star's unstable luminosity casts shadows that wax and wane unpredictably, creating an eerie, ever-shifting landscape. Its erratic radiation affects the environment and the psyche of those who dwell beneath it; crops are engineered to adapt to the flux, and citizens wear protective garb woven with radiation-dampening materials. The star's electromagnetic surges disrupt unshielded electronics, necessitating advanced engineering and lending the Technate's technology an aura of inscrutability. The culture reflects this volatile brilliance, embracing unpredictability as a cosmic truth. Some claim the star's fluctuating light has inspired an almost mystical connection to time and causality, fueling a philosophy of foresight and preparation.

Few dare challenge the Technate, not for fear of its human populace, but for the unsettling swarm of drones and bots that outnumber them. These machines, eerily lifelike yet disturbingly inhuman, embody the uncanny valley. Among them are Lament Sentinels, humanoid security drones with seamless synthetic skin, whose lifeless, glassy eyes seem to follow even the slightest movement, and Harvest Scribes, arachnid-like bots that carve intricate farming patterns into resilient soil. Their fluid, hyper-efficient motions lack the hesitations of human workers, creating a discomforting juxtaposition of familiarity and alien precision. Silent and untiring, reflecting the Technate's rejection of inefficiency and its embrace of perfection.

#### Leadership & Organization

The Temporal Architects, veiled in secrecy, govern the Technate through cryptic decrees bound by the Temporal Pact. They create policies that influence every aspect of governance, from strategic planning to societal norms, based on complex simulations of potential futures, ensuring a pragmatic balance between action and foresight which are then enacted by Seer-Syndics. Their rule is authoritarian yet quietly revered, as their perceived insights into the flow of time lend them a near-prophetic authority.

While bots dominate most labor, the Technate's sparse human population thrives in intellectual and advisory roles, often drawn to an allure of purpose and precision. Some arrive seeking refuge from chaotic systems, enticed by the Technate's promise of stability, order, and integration into a society where every individual is part of a grander scheme. For the devoted, life under the Lament Star offers a rare harmony between human potential and mechanical perfection.

### United Systems of Cressa (USC)

![[The Unified Systems of Cressa.jpg]]
![[Cressa.jpg]]

> **Government Type:** Intergovernmental Organization / Representative Democracy
> **Founding Documents:** The Freeman's Pact-USC
> **Capital:** Volera, Cressa

#### Overview

Cressa, one of the leading democracies in the Sector, belongs to the Unified Systems of Cressa (USC), which is a union of democratic states that are organized together for mutual economic and security benefits. Cressa, being the founding member, serves as the administrative capital for the USC, which also includes the Callwood United Peoples, the Lanyan Republic, and more recently, Gamarii.

Overall, the USC typically pursues isolationist policies with regards to intergalactic affairs and instead focuses its militaristic efforts towards colonization within their immediate systems, as well as mutual defense of each other's territories.

As a union, the USC has welcomed immense economic success due to their economic cooperation, trade, and commerce. Overall, the standard of living is much higher within the USC when compared to many other states. However, with success comes prying eyes, and larger more powerful groups, like The Republic of Man, have sought to undermine their efforts via acts of subterfuge, sabotage, terrorism, & espionage.

#### Leadership & Organization

Cressa & the USC each have their own democratically elected leader. Cressa's leader must be a local citizen, whereas the USC can have a leader chosen from within the member states. For example, the current leader of the USC, Chancellor Vero Wynn, comes from the Callwood United Peoples and is serving his first of potentially three 10 year terms. Meanwhile, President Trista Yeung of Cressa, a highly popular and successful politician, is in her fourth 5 year term as leader of Cressa where there aren't set term limits.

### Gamarii

![[Gamarii.jpg]]

> **Government Type:** Participatory Democracy
> **Founding Documents:** Carta Populi
> **Capital:** Liberica

#### Overview

Located on a small semi-arid/tropical planet relatively near the Vyrex System and its flagship planet Cressa, Gamarii is a recently founded participatory democracy that formed after overthrowing their oppressive dictatorship nearly a century ago. Today, they hold their democratic values deeply and put most decision making tools in the power of their highly educated population. There is no central command, but instead, power is distributed among the people who agree to convene weekly to make bureaucratic decisions. More pressing matters may instead require daily discussions, but via technology they are able to manage their population of 500,000 people with relative ease.

Conversely, there is a darker side that the population agrees to have in order to sustain their democratic values. For example, being a highly educated and technologized society, they also invest heavily into eugenics and genetic engineering with the belief that the population must remain highly intelligent and able if they can sustain this way of governance. Additionally, Gamarii lacks a true military presence, leaving itself vulnerable to raiders, corsairs, and other violent actors so in an attempt to preserve their planet, they joined forces with the United Systems of Cressa (USC), who guarantee their safety in exchange for technological advancements and open trade/borders. Even though Gamarii is mostly isolationist, citizens of the USC can move freely within participating territories, which has led to a dramatic increase in Gamarii's population. Currently, the people are debating if the participatory democracy is sustainable at this size and have started to entertain the idea of a representative democracy instead.

#### Leadership & Organization

Gamarii has no central authority and decisions are made by a highly educated voting block of citizens aged 25 and older. Citizens must attain a certain level of education before they can vote, as per the planet's governing documents, that also clearly delineates the processes for law & order. Gamarii also is a member state in the pro-democratic USC, which serves as a union of allied democracies that act in mutual protection.

## Independent Systems

Independent, or "unpeopled" spaces exist throughout the Sector. Political maps often obfuscate the presence of unpeopled, unimproved, undeveloped, or unadministered lands by still assigning them to claimant nation state(s), but independent systems exist.

"Independent" systems found on the starmap are, more accurately, largely unpeopled or undeveloped systems as the Sector has more systems than can be reasonably settled or peopled with the current population. These systems are usually not officially controlled by any nation, and also have a comparatively small permanent population that lives there or, at least, only has a semi-permanent population depending on seasons. Some are more governed than others. Leadership varies.
