# Universe

Humanity left a dying Earth and built an empire among the stars — only to nearly lose it to the monsters its own elites became. The Savage Wars are over. The Cold War has begun. Three superpowers glare at each other across the embers while megacorporations sharpen their knives and something ancient stirs in the dark between stars.

This section covers the history and politics of the Antevic Sector. For everyday life, language, and customs, see [[Culture/Culture|Culture]].
