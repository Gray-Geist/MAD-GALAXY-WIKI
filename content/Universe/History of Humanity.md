# History of Humanity

> *"Long ago, the elites of humanity left a dying Earth. They left to create disassociated, tailor-made utopias aboard massive lighthuggers."*

---

## The Departure

The elite class of Old Earth, faced with civilizational collapse of their own making, abandoned humanity aboard massive **lighthuggers** — slower-than-light colony ships that crawled through the darkness between stars for centuries. They expected the billions left behind to perish.

They were wrong.

---

## The Dregs Inherit the Stars

Those left on dying Earth adapted. They discovered **faster-than-light technology** and used it to colonize a region of the Perseus Arm they named the **Antevic Sector** — arriving centuries *ahead* of the elites who abandoned them. There they built star nations that surpassed the wildest dreams of Old Earth.

This FTL breakthrough traces directly to **[[Polymath & Poly-psykers|Project Perseus]]** (-109 BP / Before Pilgrimage), a covert Resistance initiative to solve "the FTL Problem." Agamen Ul'Rekar, a mathematician on the project, claimed communion with an entity called the **Netjer**, and became the first poly-psyker — cracking the first Cosmic Strand, **Alpha Omega**, and leading humanity's escape into the stars.

---

## The Savages

From the depths of endless space, something watched, prodded, and then struck.

**The Savages**: post-human monsters descended from the elite lighthuggers — cut off from humanity for centuries, shaped by isolation and internal dynastic warfare into something unrecognizable. They believed themselves gods. They came to remake civilization in their own violent and pathological image.

Their technology, evolved along unique paths during the long dark, could not be fathomed or reverse-engineered. Their internal alliances were tenuous — among the many dynasties, the struggle for supremacy never stopped. All knew there could be only one dynasty. One leader. One truth.

### The Hounding — Alatyr System
A Coalition cruiser unknowingly made contact with a Savage recon force. The Savages followed it home. Over 399 days they infiltrated population centers, inserted doppelganger agents into vital positions, and released a debilitating virus before striking. **Silo Station** (5 million aboard) was destroyed in an hour. Sot Prime went dark.

### The Disaster at Sot Prime
The **Combined Forces** swept toward Sot Prime expecting easy victory — the Savages had always fled before. Instead they were annihilated. The **Alatian Accords** forced the Coalition into humiliating neutrality: cede Alatyr and stand aside.

### The Astral Alliance
Eventually, a coalition of star nations formed the **Astral Alliance** and amassed forces against the Savage Marines. The fight for Sot Prime became a brutal stalemate.

Commander **Tyrus Martell** of the Vulkus Tactical Assault Paladins (VTAP) — *the Butcher of Andalore* — made the decision. He unleashed a **Chained Nuclear Trigger Ordinance (CNTO)** on Sot Prime, glassing the planet entirely. Billions of humans under Savage control died. The planet was left poisoned with radiation in perpetuity.

> *"There was no humanity left on that planet, just the illusion of it."* — Tyrus Martell

Key dates:
- **-109 BP**: Project Perseus begins
- **57 AE**: Resistance falls apart; Project Perseus cells carry on; Ul'Rekar cracks Alpha Omega
- **1153 AE**: The Extranet is invented
- **Post-Savage Wars**: Republic of Man formed; Cold War begins

---

## The Cold War Today

The Savage Wars ended. Three superpowers rose from the embers. The Republic of Man took Primus from the Coalition as reparations for the Coalition's non-aggression pact with the Savages.

Today dissent grows. Proxy wars rage on the Edge. Megacorporations profit from strife. Conspiracies fester beneath polite civilization. And something unseen stirs in the unstable flux.
