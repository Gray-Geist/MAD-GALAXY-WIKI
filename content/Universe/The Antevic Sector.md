# The Antevic Sector

> *"Known space" equates to about one thousandth of a percent of the galaxy.*

![[antevic_sector_art.png]]

---

## Overview

The **Antevic Sector** is a region of the **Perseus Arm** of the Milky Way galaxy — humanity's home in the stars, hard-won from a dying Earth and soaked in the blood of the Savage Wars.

As of current combined census records, approximately **two hundred billion people** inhabit the Antevic Sector across roughly **2,000 physically discovered star systems** — though advanced observation technology has logged approximately **1,000,000 systems** in known space.

The Milky Way galaxy has a diameter of approximately **105,700 light-years**, containing roughly **100 billion stars** and **800 million habitable star systems**. What humanity calls "Known Space" represents about one-thousandth of one percent of this — and of that, only two-thousandths has been physically visited.

## Star Nation Structure

Each star nation organizes its territory into four broad regions:

| Region | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Core Worlds** | Most wealthy, most advanced. Per capita elite of the nation. |
| **Mid-Core (Mids)** | Thriving worlds providing essential services to the core. |
| **Edge Worlds (The Edge)** | Law is anemic. State and corporate proxy wars. Wild and untamed. |
| **Grave Regions** | Wholly uncharted or perpetually changing charts. Extremely hazardous. |

> *Navy personnel who venture beyond the Edge are said to be "going to the Grave."*

---

## Clusters & Sub-Clusters

Units of space that cross national lines are called **clusters** — groupings of at least 10 star systems. Clusters are divided into **sub-clusters** (several systems minimum), and battlefleets are often named for the cluster they protect (e.g., *Republic Navy Battlefleet Primus*).

Regions between sub-clusters — containing nebulae, hazardous systems, unexplored space, uninhabitable worlds — are called **inter-clusters**, more commonly known as:
- Wilderness zones
- Forbidden zones
- Frontier space

## The Three Superpowers

The Sector is dominated by three superpowers locked in a bitter **Cold War** following the [[History of Humanity|Savage Wars]]. Their swollen fleets and covert agencies never strike outright, but proxy conflicts rage on the Edge. Hubris corrupts national leaders; insidious conspiracies gnaw at fragile unity.
