# MAD GALAXY

![[mad_galaxy_banner.png]]
![[MAD GALAXY Timeline.jpg]]

---

*The galaxy is made of stone and filled by low men. Earth is a myth in the vast expanse of the galaxy and the embers of conflict burn hot and unyielding. Like sparks from a celestial forge, the history of this turbulent cosmos is etched in violence, betrayal, and unending struggle.*

*Long ago, the elites of humanity left a dying Earth. They left to create disassociated, tailor-made utopias aboard massive lighthuggers that crawled with no light speed through the darkness between the stars. But the people they left behind on a dying planet didn't perish in the dystopian nightmare the elites had themselves created. Instead, they adapted, discovering faster-than-light technology, and used it to colonize a part of the galaxy centuries ahead of the elites, they called it the Antevic Sector. There they would struggle to eventually form fantastic new civilizations, star nations, that surpassed the wildest dreams of Old Earth. But from the depths of endless space, something watched, prodded, and then struck.*

*They were called the Savages: post-human monsters who believed themselves gods — intent on remaking civilization in their own violent and pathological image. There were advantages to the Savages having been cut off from the main of humanity, in the dark between worlds, for so long. No one facing them could guess how things had evolved within each dynastic lighthugger, what their technology had become as it moved down the path they had chosen and, in some cases, worshiped. Like some accidental time-traveler checking the calendar in a room they had suddenly appeared in, the Savages didn't know what they'd be up against either and unleashed occult technology that still to this day cannot be fathomed or reverse engineered. Yet their internal alliances were tenuous. Among the many dynasties, the struggle for supremacy raged on. All knew that in the end there could be only one dynasty. One leader. One truth.*

*Eventually a coalition of star nations, the Astral Alliance, amassed their forces to defeat the dreadful Savage Marines who'd come to sack and enslave. Nearly a century later the Savage Wars ended, the horrors of which would eternally scar the Sector, and would haltingly fade, leading to the celebration of Unity Day and the groundwork for the superpowers.*

*Today, the Sector is dominated by three superpowers who stand among the searing embers of the recently ended Savage Wars, titans of astro political mastery. To some, they are beacons of order and unity among the broken stars whose wounds from the greatest war known to man have not yet healed. To others, a threat, their ideologies clashing in a bitter Cold War. Around them orbit the great powers, aspirational yet constrained, and the minor powers, flickering pyres in the maelstrom. Beyond the polished façade of the prosperous superpower core world's ultimate peace lies a simmering cauldron of ambition and fear. Each act as both a deterrent and a threat, their swollen fleets and covert agencies never striking outright but always poised to do so, yet their dominion is anything but peaceful. Hubris and ambition corrupt national leaders and insidious conspiracies fester, gnawing away at the very fabric of the novel unity. Without the ultimate struggle of the Savage Wars, dissent grows, fueling fractious factions that now writhe among the stars threatening to erupt and tear humanity itself asunder. Proxy wars erupt in distant systems, and the edges of their spheres of influence teem with insurgencies, piracy, and corporate opportunism. For the people caught in the crossfire, the Cold War is a crucible, shaping lives and futures as the mighty clash in an endless shadow game of dominance and survival.*

*Humanity is enhanced and improved but also battered and bitterly divided. Technology allows the re-shaping of bodies and minds but also creates opportunities for oppression and mass destruction. Elusive forces lurk in the shadows. Cloaked in darkness and armed with unaccountable technology, they strike with precision and anonymity, sowing discord, and pitting nations against one other. The motives of these surreptitious forces remain a mystery, but their desire to unleash utter chaos upon the Sector is undeniable. Amidst the madness, whispers of errant AI and occult Savage technology echoes across the void of space.*

*The boots of the superpowers crush dissent in the name of the belief that should they fall, the Sector would descend into a blazing furnace of screams and gnashing teeth, the stars gone berserk where deranged warlords rule fiefdoms, survival is an unending deadly dance, and only the maddest of the mad could dare claim dominion over the stars. If only those who perpetuate this belief knew they were already within what they fear, blinded by the numbing veil of polite civilization. Perhaps they do, perhaps they seek to be the boot.*

*Speculation grows that the Cold War churns, and the mega-corporations rub their hands in anticipation of the profits that strife has always brought. But there is an unseen change lately in the unstable flux of power and intrigue, of stroke and counterstroke. An almost prehistoric dance of death has begun, as the nations birthed in forges of war and so used to being connoisseurs of final breaths sustain the trend, violence oozing forth between the cracks of society and leaders who seek to add more bones to a galaxy already full of them. Some will rise, others will fall, and the fate of this violent cosmos will be decided in a blaze of gunfire and bloodshed. This is a Mad Galaxy, after all.*
