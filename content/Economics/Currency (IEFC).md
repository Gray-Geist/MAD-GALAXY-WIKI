## Interstellar Exchange Format Currency

The most common unit of legal tender in the Sector is the **Interstellar Exchange Format Currency (IEFC)**, universally known as **Credits** (abbreviated **Cr**).

Depending on which national sphere you're in, Credits are backed by the financial power of that nation or group of nations. For example, Republic Credits are informally known as the **CREP** (Credit Republic). The IEFC tech system was formed during the Savage Wars and is backed by the massed economies of different alliance or nation member worlds, supplemented by colonies and protectorates.

## Forms of Currency

### Digital Credits
The vast majority of transactions are digital transfers — especially for legitimate corporate, banking, and government activity. Digital security uses **blockchain technology** to prevent counterfeiting.

### Cred-Chips
**Semi-hard currency** stored on a physical chip. Functions like physical currency — deducts from its stored balance when used. Can be used for anonymous payments. However, cred-chips can be used by anyone who possesses them, so theft is a concern.

### Credit Bills (Hard Credits)
![[currency_art2.png]]


Truly hard credits — sometimes called **"credit bills"** — are used primarily in illicit, illegal, and street-level transactions. Megacorps use credit bills for black operations to avoid leaving digital trails. Many off-grid settlements exclusively use hard currency. **Zeroes** (people who don't officially exist and cannot get bank accounts) can only transact with hard currency.

Credit bills are nearly impossible to counterfeit: plastic fibers are combined under heat and pressure, extruded as a rectangular bundle, and then sliced to paper thinness. The pattern is formed into the structure of the note — not printed on top. An alphanumeric combination adds uniqueness to each bill.

## Sub-polity Currencies

Within and without nations, sub-polities may issue their own currency:
- **Planetary Currencies**: Individual worlds may issue currencies that may or may not be accepted elsewhere
- **Megacorporation Scrip**: Corporations may issue scrip; acceptance outside their corporate environment is uncertain

IEFC Credits can generally be converted in most places in the Sector.

## Interstellar Exchange Banks


![[currency_ie_bank5.png]]



**IE Banks** connect Sector-wide currency exchange and trade. Every officially "civilized" settlement — from the greatest megacity to the farthest colony — houses at least one.

Each bank contains sophisticated apparatus and data vaults connected to a nearby [[Communications#The Extranet|Extranet tower]] facility.

### In Megacities
![[currency_ie_bank1.png]]
Multiple IE banks per city. These are often the most **fortified buildings** in any settlement — data loads so heavy that banks become massive fortresses with their own power plants, soaring above most skyscrapers.
![[currency_ie_bank3.png]]
