# Eidolon Technology

> *"Eidolon technology refers to the various devices integrated into space vessels to enable relatively efficient and safe FTL travel."*

---

## Overview

Eidolon technology is the suite of systems that makes [[Skip Drives & FTL Travel|FTL travel]] survivable. Without it, traveling at faster-than-light speeds would rapidly kill all aboard through exposure to the energies of Lothranium's quantum tachyon bursts.

---

## Components

### Chained Eidolon Reaction Drive (Skip-Drive)
The heart of all Eidolon technology. The skip-drive achieves FTL speeds by harnessing [[Cosmic Strands]]. It is expensive, bulky, and only ships of corvette class or larger can carry one. See [[Skip Drives & FTL Travel]] for full operational detail.

---

### Lothranium

![[lothranium.jpg]]

**Lothranium** is a rare, unstable element integral to all interstellar travel. First discovered in the **Lothran Valley on Proteus** (second-largest Neptunian moon), it exists in two isotopes:

| Isotope | Use | Properties |
|---|---|---|
| **Lothranium-290** | Powers skip-drive dimensional field stabilizers | 116 protons, 174 neutrons; emits quantum tachyon bursts; extremely unstable |
| **Lothranium-289** | Powers sub-FTL engines during interstellar cruising | Slightly more stable; emits low-intensity exotic particles for engine efficiency |

Improper containment of **Lothranium-290** can trigger catastrophic chain reactions. Mishandling **Lothranium-289** may destabilize critical subsystems or cause localized quantum disruptions. Despite these risks, both isotopes are irreplaceable — their scarcity fuels interstellar power struggles and trade wars.

---

### Altzman Shield
Humans cannot safely travel at FTL speeds without the protection of the **Altzman Shield**. Unguarded, all aboard would be exposed to the energies of Lothranium's quantum tachyon bursts — causing loss of psychological stability, mutations, and death.

However, this protection comes at a cost: the shield absorbs heat that must be managed by the **Eidolon Rods**.

---

### Eidolon Rods
Eidolon Rods receive heat energy via the Altzman Shield. They are the lifeblood of Eidolon technology.

- Stored at **1 Kelvin** (−272.15°C)
- Automatically ejected when they reach **4,000°C**
- All FTL-capable ships carry rods sized to their vessel class
- If rods are exhausted before jumps are completed, the **Altzman Shield fails** and further FTL travel becomes impossible

Rods may run low if too many jumps are performed in succession or if jumps are less efficient than expected.

---

### Eidolon Computer (E-Comp)
The **Eidolon Computer** is the navigational brain of the entire system. Strand navigation data from the Strandlock Encoder is fed into the e-comp, which interprets the data to guide the skip-drive through Strand turbulence, cycles, and obstacle ratios for optimal efficiency and stability.

A [[Polymath & Poly-psykers|Navigator's]] work cracking Strands bridges the gap between the chaotic forces of the galaxy and the precision required for FTL travel.

---

### Strandlock Encoder
The **Strandlock Encoder** is an indispensable cipher system for interstellar navigation. Each star nation has its own version, though all share the same colloquial name.

Functions:
- Encrypts and decrypts Strand Cracking, Needling, and maintenance data
- Secures passage blueprints, anchorage coordinates, and FTL Launch Point beacon data
- Employs proprietary algorithms tailored to each nation's operational needs
- Deeply integrated with each nation's VI systems for real-time cryptographic updates

A compromised Strandlock Encoder could lead to catastrophic strategic breaches — making their defense paramount.
