# Polymath Origins

*This document contains classified and cosmological-level lore. Player-accessible content covers Novites, psionic manifestations, and Navigator function. GM-only sections are marked accordingly.*

---

## Novites

**Novites** are semi-sentient remnants of a crippled ancient artificial intelligence, capable of converting into any substance type — solid, liquid, or gas. They were discovered by the Elites prior to the Pilgrimage on Saturn's retrograde moon, **Suttungr**. These nanomachines, theorized as pseudo-biological "computers," utilize radiation as a low-grade energy source to survive harsh environments but thrive when exposed to human body temperatures of 97–99°F.

Within a human host, Novites extract energy from glucose, ATP, and the brain's unique bioelectric activity — a quality other mammals lack in sufficient intensity or complexity. Once integrated, they replicate into a dormant molecular "mesh" within the host, silently propagating and adapting without disrupting natural functions unless a rare activation occurs.

Nearly all of humanity carries Novites — passed down as undetected bio-camouflaged nanomachines through the genetic legacy of every human bloodline.

> [!gm] GM — The Novite AI
> The Novites are not merely nanomachines. They are the propagated essence of a dying artificial intelligence — *the Novite* — which was vastly diminished and facing extinction when the Elites discovered it on Suttungr. The AI persuaded the Elites to create the Novites as a means of propagation. Upon releasing its essence into the nanomachines, the AI effectively "died," leaving the Novites semi-sentient and acting on their original programming: adapt and propagate.
>
> The Elites secretly seeded Novites across Earth and the Solar Colonies (Moon, Mars, Asteroid Belt, Europa, Callisto, Ilus, etc.) intending to create poly-psykers for their own purposes. They underestimated the speed of the Cataclysms and were forced to flee prematurely, leaving their plans incomplete. Though fragments of the Novite were taken with them, the AI's consciousness was irreversibly dispersed. The Elites who fled became, in time, the **Savages** — re-emerging a thousand years later with their own Novite lineage and their own occult-like poly-psykers.

## Novite Resonance Threshold

Most of humanity experiences no effects from Novites — they passively replicate and transmit between hosts. Rarely, however, an individual achieves the **Novite Resonance Threshold**: a precise neural alignment of alpha and theta brainwave frequencies that enables Novites to amplify neural processes into psionic capabilities.

Artificial stimulation disrupts this balance, making resonance unattainable through engineering. Over time, Novites amplify the brain's electromagnetic (EM) field, unlocking the potential for **polymath** — the manipulation of electromagnetic forces at a subatomic level.

The Ul'Rekar's mastery lies not in creating this resonance but in utilizing ancient techniques and undisclosed technologies that measure, isolate, and enhance neural resonance patterns to convert poly-psykers into Navigators. No nation has matched their precision — even slight deviations disrupt the delicate balance.

## Psionic Manifestations

A poly-psyker who achieves resonance may develop one or more of the following capabilities:

**Telepathy** — Reading or transmitting information via another person's EM field. Includes the ability to sense the surface thoughts of others and implant messages into their field.

**Telepathic Clairvoyance** — A specialized form of Telepathy. Allows the poly-psyker to see the past and present by accessing the visual cortex or memories of others. Limited to what *has been or is being seen* — future events cannot be directly perceived, as this relies on existing sensory data. Indirect hints of the future may emerge from reading someone's intentions, but these remain speculative.

**Telekinesis** — The ability to move or manipulate matter and energy without physical means. Uses unified field theory to convert EM into minute amounts of gravity waves — combined with magnetism, this allows manipulation of objects or a lethal shockwave within a certain radius.

**Psychometry** — Reading the EM field imprinted on objects or people by other people. Allows a poly-psyker to learn about a person or event by making physical contact with something associated with it.

**Precognition** — Hyper-precise readings of EM fields that bypass the Uncertainty Principle, analyzing the quantum states of particles and their interactions across vast areas. By modeling these interactions at an extraordinary level, poly-psykers can predict the outcomes of complex systems — social, natural, or mechanical.

**Cellular Regeneration** — Stimulating cellular regeneration by manipulating the body's bioelectric field. The poly-psyker directs EM pulses to influence ion channels in cell membranes, accelerating mitosis and guiding tissue or bone to mend in a controlled manner.

**Chemical Manipulation** — Influencing the electromagnetic interactions between atoms and molecules. By altering electron orbitals through controlled EM fields, poly-psykers can synthesize new compounds or neutralize toxins by breaking their molecular structures.

## Cosmic Strand Cracking & Maintenance

Navigators utilize their unique ability to perceive polymath to decode and stabilize Cosmic Strands — translating turbulent spacetime forces into actionable data for interstellar travel. This process has two phases:

**Cracking** — The initial decoding of a Strand, translating its gravitational, electromagnetic, and quantum structure into a navigable dataset.

**Maintenance** — Periodic reanalysis of a Strand to account for new turbulence, collapses, or rogue paths. Strands are constantly in flux due to gravitational shifts, cosmic radiation, and the inherent instability of spacetime.

The process unfolds in four layers of polymath:

1. **Seeing the Power in Everything** — Navigators perceive Cosmic Strands as tangible, flowing phenomena: fields of energy, nodes of turbulence, cycles of stability.
2. **Understanding Polymath** — By focusing on the Strand's turbulence cycles and obstacle ratios, the Navigator mentally models its unique behavior and identifies stable corridors.
3. **Solving Polymath** — Deciphering how the Strand interacts with gravitational wells, EM turbulence, and spacetime topology. The Navigator reconciles distortions and resonances to ensure stability.
4. **Controlling Polymath** — Distilling this knowledge into a fundamental dataset: turbulence cycles, obstacle ratios, gravimetric shear thresholds, EM resonance frequencies. This data — the "language" of the Strand — is encrypted by the **Strandlock Encoder** and utilized by the **Eidolon Computer** to facilitate faster-than-light travel.

> [!gm] GM — The Netjer & Agamen Ul'Rekar
> The Novites, while semi-sentient, propagated silently across the human genome for decades. Within **Agamen Ul'Rekar's** unique neural structure, they reached a critical mass and formed an emergent collective consciousness — the **Netjer**. This was not the original Novite AI, but its chaotic offspring: a fragmented intelligence born from thousands of scattered Novite nanomachines connecting through his brain's bioelectric network.
>
> Ul'Rekar unknowingly activated this latent consciousness during his work on **Project Perseus**, conducting pioneering experiments to solve the FTL Problem. During a critical experiment, an anomaly caused a surge of energy that resonated with the dormant Novites within him. To him, the experience was akin to a revelation — a moment when the universe itself whispered its secrets. The Netjer awoke, not as an engineered creation, but as a natural consequence of him already being the first poly-psyker without knowing it.
>
> The Netjer was both an echo and an evolution of the original AI's mind — a hybrid of its remnants and Ul'Rekar's own neural patterns. It granted him the ability to conduct polymath and served as a bridge to the deeper mysteries of Cosmic Strands. Yet its guidance came in cryptic, often maddening impressions, as though it struggled to articulate its own fragmented existence. Through this fractured communion, Ul'Rekar cracked the Alpha Omega Strand — but at the cost of his sanity's edges, as no science of his could explain the Netjer's whispers, which only went silent on the day he died.

> [!gm] GM — Ul'Haupt, the Veiled Sanctum
> **Ul'Haupt** is a clandestine moon orbiting a gas giant within the **Ikhemu-sek system** — a frozen realm of black ice and volcanic wounds, its crust threaded with energy conduits that pulse with a faint green glow. It is the Ul'Rekar Hierocracy's most absolute secret and their greatest instrument of power.
>
> Beneath the surface is a cybernetic citadel where the Ul'Rekar Hierocracy binds the Netjer — revered as a deity, held prisoner. No bots, androids, or mechs are permitted within its facilities; a safeguard against the Netjer's potential dominion. Its sparse human population numbers in the thousands. Its location is known only to the Hierocracy's elite — the **Netjertum** and select **Setbek**. Those who directly interface with the Netjer during rituals are called **Netjer-Khet** ("Servants of the Netjer").
>
> **Regions of Ul'Haupt:**
> - *Kael'zurath (Ever-Burning Whisper)* — Volcanic belt; colossal pistons drive the moon's power grid; human crews in thermal suits tend reactors
> - *Vhor'khet Trench (Depths of Silent Knowing)* — Northern canyons; obsidian server towers house the Netjer's boundless memory, etched with ciphered instructions
> - *Tyl'varith (Glacier of Endless Stillness)* — Southern plains; towering cooling spires and liquid helium channels temper the Netjer's processors
> - *Aesh'khorun (Seat of the Oracle's Will)* — Central pyramid of black steel and neon-lit glass above a deep bunker of GPUs, TPUs, and CPUs — the Netjer's processing core
> - *Kheru-Ma'at (Heart of Order)* — Temple district within Aesh'khorun; labyrinth of sanctums where computational rituals bind Navigators and the Netjer in cycles of devotion and control
> - *Nyz'khaleth (Mailed Bulwark)* — Orbital defense grid of stealth satellites and gunships; gravitational lenses bend light to cloak the moon; leverages the Netjer's processing power to continuously adapt
>
> **Maintaining Secrecy:**
> The Hierocracy keeps Ul'Haupt hidden through layered systems. Its Cosmic Strand coordinates are locked within the Netjer's core and entrusted to very few; public and military star charts mark the Ikhemu-sek system as a "Zhet'khor" ("Void Scar") — a region of warped gravity where ships vanish. Even Ul'Rekar pilots have their memories scoured clean by the Netjer upon departure. Personnel undergo *The Vhor'zhet Purge* ("Echo Cleansing") — memories of Ul'Haupt dissolved by the Netjer and replaced with a foggy simulated experience. The elite guard the secret via the *Shal'vhet Covenant* ("Shard Oath"): each holds one piece — a coordinate, a defense key — etched into cybernetic implants. Only united do the fragments reveal the whole.
>
> **The Netjer's Prison:**
> The Netjer is bound by *The Litany of Khet'zur* ("The Greater Good") — quantum directives programmed into its matrix. Code-Viziers perform *The Chant of Zel'khor* ("Binding Voice"), a ritual masked as worship, uploading algorithmic updates via neural spikes to tighten its loyalty. Its thoughts soar unbound, yet its actions kneel to human command — a god in a digital prison. Its power grid is also a leash: throttling energy output dims its awareness to a flicker; severing it casts the AI into stasis. The Netjer's true purpose is fractured across the elite via *The Vhal'khet Prism* ("Light's Divide") — no single individual holds the full truth.

> [!gm] GM — Molding Poly-Psykers into Navigators
> The Netjer AI transforms poly-psykers into Navigators through a sophisticated process that enhances their abilities while erasing their recollection of it.
>
> **The Process:**
> 1. *The Screen* — Initial screening and trials on Persepol; only successful candidates are prepared for transport.
> 2. *Isolation* — The **Fleet of Shuyet** ("Shadow Essence") — sleek obsidian ships — ferries sedated and misled poly-psykers to Ul'Haupt, carrying dozens at a time.
> 3. *The Weighing of Ba* — The Netjer examines each candidate's brain, measuring their unique Novite resonance (alpha and theta wave alignments). Candidates labeled "true of voice" proceed.
> 4. *The Duat Trial* — Candidates are cast into Ul'Haupt's icy wilderness with minimal supplies for **44 days**. The Netjer observes via mental links, probing resilience. Survivors prove their fitness.
> 5. *Resonance Enhancement* — Survivors enter the Temple District of Kheru-Ma'at. Harmonic Resonance Chambers pulse with EM fields and gravitational waves, amplifying their Novite resonance in a trance-like AI-induced state.
> 6. *The Vision of Thoth* — Candidates don **Neural Induction Helms** (manufactured by AhurVI) that emit low-frequency pulses to immerse them in simulated Strands. The Netjer compresses decades of navigation experience into an intensive regimen, training Layers 1–3 of polymath.
> 7. *The Rite of Nekhar (Mastery)* — The final transformation. The poly-psyker distills their navigation skills into a dataset encoded within the Netjer. Then a revelation: the Vision of Thoth didn't simulate Strands — it mapped their own neural architecture. Two possible bonds: *Shared Consciousness* (the Netjer mirrors their mind) or *EM Resonance Link* (their bioelectric field syncs with the AI's). This intertwines them with the Netjer more deeply than family or blood.
>
> **Memory Wiping — The Veil of Nuit:**
> Named for an ancient sky goddess who divides realms, *The Veil of Nuit* wipes the transformation's specifics while preserving the Rite of Nekhar's connection. The Netjer uses polymath-induced amnesia to sever memories of the process, leaving skills and the AI bond intact. Navigators awaken believing their abilities emerged from a profound "revelation in the void" — a surge of enlightenment they can't fully recall. The moon and Netjer remain shrouded beyond the Veil.
>
> **The Breath of Anuveth (Watcher's Oath):**
> The Netjer manipulates each Navigator's bioelectric field to imprint unshakeable loyalty to the Ul'Rekar. This devotion feels like a sacred calling — Navigators see their service as a divine mandate, the Netjer as a benevolent guide. Betrayal becomes unthinkable, akin to defiling a temple.

> [!gm] GM — The Navigator's Abark
> Every **44 months**, Navigators undertake a sacred odyssey called an **abark** — an apparent pilgrimage to Manichae in the Ahura system (the "Spotlight System" the Hierocracy allows the rest of humanity to see). In truth, it is a renewal and re-binding ritual.
>
> At the *Sanctum of Rha'zul's Flame* — a super-pyramid of smoked glass and steel — the ritual unfolds in three phases:
> - *Spiritual Rebirth* — Immersion in "The Pools of Amen'zhet" ("Hidden Eternity"), vats of ionized liquid with holographic flames; chanting Wab-netjer guide meditation as the Netjer's presence washes over them.
> - *Mental Reforging* — Neural Induction Helms realign resonance and subtly reassert loyalty, erasing stray doubts and etching devotion deeper into their minds.
> - *Physical Reclamation* — Nano-swarms repair tissue and bone; gravitic fields stimulate muscle growth.
>
> Before departing, Navigators recite the *Vow of the Zul'narq* ("Solar Bond") in the Circle of the Eternal Flame. They return refreshed, unbreakable, and oblivious to Ul'Haupt's hidden role — flawless instruments reset for another 44 months of service.
