## Ammunition

![[ammunition_intro_icon.png|481]]
![[ammunition_25mm Yoke.png]]

After the Savage Wars' refinements, the majority of chemical arms across the Sector adopted **caseless ammunition** — the body of the round itself is the propellant, eliminating the need to eject cases.

**Advantages of caseless:**
- Lighter ammo; less space; lighter weapon or more rounds carried
- Higher rates of fire — no case ejection cycle needed
- Ignition via **electrothermal-chemical (ETC) propellant** — high ignition point prevents accidental cook-off
- Gun barrels constructed with advanced polymer alloy for heat resistance

### Ammunition Types

| Round | Description |
|---|---|
| **.480mm Pedal Slug** | Low-velocity expanding slug for ship boarding — minimizes hull breach risk, maximizes stopping power against unarmored targets. No effect on most armored opponents. |
| **25mm Yoke** | Custom chemical arm projectiles weighing over half a pound each. Fired from sniper rifles so large they require power armor or a bipod that locks to the ground. |
| **40mm MK75 MOD 2 HEDP** | High explosive, dual-purpose standard explosive munition. |
| **40mm XM18P EMP Warhead** | Generates an electromagnetic pulse at impact. Capable of circumventing EMP shielding and temporarily disabling vehicles. |
| **D-U (Depleted Uranium)** | Very powerful armor-piercing round for chemical arms. |
| **Lava Rounds** | Non-standard slag round designed to ablate on contact and overwhelm defensive positions. |
| **Volcano Rounds** | Non-standard slag round designed to pierce the heavy armor of light vehicles and ACPA. |
| **SMAFF** | Refractive smoke rounds. Anti-ship detection system — foils enemy EM and IR sensors. Visibility within a SMAFF cloud: ~3–5m. |
| **Stingers** | Non-lethal incapacitating rounds. |

## Weapon Types

### Asynchronous Linear Induction Motor (ALIM) Arms

![[Assets/Technology/_wiki/alim_intro.gif]]

*Colloquially: "Linear Motor Arms." Technology unlocked in 1445 AE when the massive recoil problem of such weapons was finally overcome.*

- Accelerates projectiles to extreme velocities — rapid penetration and long-range effectiveness
- Disadvantage: the weapon must be considerably larger than chem arm counterparts
- **Anvil Round:** Creates an EMP pulse at point of impact
- **"Splashers" (APLP/F):** Armor-piercing, limited penetration/frangible — designed to disintegrate on impact, dumping all energy into the target; catastrophic against lightly armored or unarmored targets
- **"Rippers" (HV/FTHPP):** High-velocity ferric-tungsten hollow-point — punches through armor, then expands violently while keeping much of its mass intact, maximizing energy transfer to soft targets within armor
- Can be flash-suppressed, but the projectile exceeding the sound barrier still creates a sonic boom
- Precision targeting advantage over slaggers
- Relatively low energy consumption compared to same-size slaggers

### Chemical Arms (Chem Arms)
*Also: chemers, firearms, slug throwers. Originated from Old Earth; Sector-wide use. Majority use caseless ammunition.*

- Uses explosive chemicals to launch a solid projectile ("slug")
- Normal rounds not effective against modern armor
- Can be ALIM-assisted — ALIM-assisted rounds are typically Teflon-coated tungsten: a narrow tungsten arrow surrounded by a light Teflon-edged sabot for maximum barrel exit velocity
- Can use suppressors and non-tracer rounds (for surprise and confusion)

### Slaggers

![[Assets/Technology/_wiki/slagger_intro.gif]]

*Military-authorized use only. Invented during the Savage Wars specifically to counter Savage armor.*

- Superheats metal, then fires it as a molten mass of plasma ("slag")
- Less effective at long range compared to ALIM arms
- Much higher stopping power than chemical arm counterparts
- Highly effective against all armor and — depending on ammo — fortifications
- **Cannot be flash-suppressed or silenced**
- Special "lava rounds" can splash and disrupt fortified positions

### Smart Weapons

![[Assets/Technology/_wiki/smart_weapons_intro.gif]]

*Only chemical arms can be Smart.*

- Use minijet technology to fire caseless guided ammo
- Efficient and pin-point accurate
- Tied to weapon grips and optics to scan and track target movement
- Aim for the wielder using smart rounds that lock on and seek their targets — must be within the weapon's tracking box
- Can curve around obstacles to hit their target, but travel slower than conventional bullets
- **[[NeuroSyn & Cybernetics|SmartLync]] cyberware is required** for smart-targeting; better quality SmartLyncs improve tracking ability
- Enemies can jam smart-targeting ability
- Smart weapons **cannot use suppressors or other scopes**

## Drones

**Inquisitor Drone Kit:**

![[Assets/Technology/Drones/drone_Inquisitor.png|217]]

A swarm of micro-drones, each no larger than a horse fly, operating via combined nano data strings to deliver simultaneous digital mapping, audio feed, and video feed. Pure recon — the Inquisitor Drone Kit provides no weapons capability. Highly effective against technologically unsuspecting or inferior opponents; well-oriented security measures can mitigate usefulness significantly. Without countermeasures, the absolute choice for recon operatives operating indoors, where the swarm excels and environmental conditions pose no hazard due to unit size. Outdoors it is effective at Short to Medium range but can be affected by adverse environmental conditions. Highly expensive; military and paramilitary supply chains only.

**XT908 "Kestrel":**

![[drone_xt908.png]]

Fixed-wing tactical strike UAV. Data-link controlled beyond line of sight. Multi-spectral O-Targeting System with variable aperture infrared cameras and gamma ray, biological, and chemical detection. Built-in radar evasion. Armed with tactical micro-missiles or 50-lb laser-guided surgical bomb units — loadout selected at deployment. Weapons limit range and maneuverability. Operates autonomously or under operator direction via TCC-4 or Battleboard.

**XT909 "Wraith":**

Improved-specification variant of the XT908 platform. 45 continuous hours of operation, 4,256-mile (6,849 km) range, operational altitude 55,000+ ft (16,764 m). Crewed by a pilot and two sensor operators at a ground station; command latency approximately 0.5 seconds at maximum range. Enhanced sensor suite detects stationary targets — unlike standard motion-tracking systems. The platform of choice when the mission envelope demands long loiter, deep reach, and no radar signature.

## Nanomolecular Laptop Systems

*A new and rare weapon category. Nanomolecular Laptop systems are engineered from molecular-level components disguised as parts of ordinary personal objects — data cases, writing instruments, travel hardware. Before use, components undergo a hardening process called "baking" that fuses the molecular structure into a functional weapon. Bake time increases with weapon size. Once assembled, these systems are operationally indistinguishable from standard firearms.*

*Their defining characteristic: they defeat standard weapon detection scans entirely. Security checkpoints, passive sensor sweeps, and standard OBSERVATION-based searches cannot identify them. Only dedicated forensic scanning with specialist equipment has any chance of detection. Countermeasures technology is actively trying to close this gap and has not succeeded. The weapons are illegal under virtually all faction law and found only on the black market and in the hands of intelligence operatives.*

*Avalon-Arienai manufacture.*

**Nanomolecular Laptop Pistol:**

![[Assets/Technology/Weapons/Nanomolecular Laptop Pistol.png|447]]

The entry point of the Laptop family — the smallest bake, the smallest component footprint, the fastest to assemble and conceal. A standard sidearm in every way that matters in a firefight. Carried by operatives who need a weapon that simply will not be found. The limited magazine is the price of the construction; the operative who carries one has already accepted that the mission profile does not call for volume of fire.

**Nanomolecular Laptop SMG:**

![[Assets/Technology/Weapons/Nanomolecular Laptop SMG.png|447]]

Full-auto capability in a scan-transparent platform. The SMG is the choice for operatives whose missions may require suppressing fire or clearing a confined space after the initial approach. Bake time is longer than the pistol; the components are more numerous and the assembly more involved. What comes out the other end is a submachine gun that walked through every checkpoint between procurement and use.

**Nanomolecular Laptop Snub-Shotgun:**

![[Assets/Technology/Weapons/Nanomolecular Laptop Snub-Shotgun.png|500]]

Maximum stopping power at close range in a concealed platform. The snub-shotgun is the close-quarters specialist of the Laptop family — an operative who needs to guarantee a result at Engaged range and has the bake time to spare. Each shell is a precision-engineered nanomolecular component; the magazine capacity reflects that reality. Used when the mission calls for a guaranteed stop, not a sustained engagement.

**Nanomolecular Laptop Auto-Shotgun:**

![[Assets/Technology/Weapons/Nanomolecular Laptop Auto-Shotgun.png|596]]

The most materially complex close-range system in the family. Full-auto shotgun capability assembled from components that passed through security as luggage. The bake time is significant; an operative deploying this platform has planned for it. The result is a weapon that answers the question of what happens when the mission goes loud inside a secured facility — and the answer does not favor the facility.

**Nanomolecular Laptop Sniper Rifle:**

![[Assets/Technology/Weapons/Nanomolecular Laptop Sniper Rifle.png|630]]

At this scale, the component objects are no longer small — a full laptop case, a hard-shell camera bag, a reinforced instrument case. All of them blameless under any scanner that exists. The bake time is six hours; an operative deploying this weapon has established a position, prepared the components, and waited. What follows is a precision armor-piercing shot from a weapon that was never supposed to be in the room. The Laptop Sniper Rifle is the end of the sentence that began when someone decided a target needed to be removed from a location thought to be secure.

## Weapon Accessories & Attachments

**Muzzle Suppressor:** AV: R. Sound suppressor for CHEM-class firearms. Prevents a shot from automatically revealing the shooter's position. Available for pistols, SMGs, carbines, and rifles. Not compatible with LMGs, shotguns, heavy weapons, ALIM, or SLAGGER platforms — the nature of those systems makes meaningful suppression impossible. Cost varies by caliber and barrel threading.

**Optical Scope:** AV: C. Magnification scope for pistols and rifles. Extends effective range when taking careful aimed shots. The simplest and cheapest force-multiplier for a rifleman. Incompatible with sustained automatic fire — the sight picture breaks under recoil.

## Personnel Ordnance

**APER M101 Claymore Mine:**

![[Assets/Technology/Ordnance/APER M101 Claymore Mine.png|596]]

Small infantry-deployed anti-personnel mine. Three explosive charges in a fan shape; numerous ball bearings that deform into shards on explosion. Effective range 75m; dangerous to 250m. Uses motion detector, forward-facing trip lasers, and IFF detector.

**C5 Blast Brick:**

Adhesive plastic explosive block. Sticks to any surface and detonates on remote signal or timer. General-purpose demolitions charge — breaches doors and interior walls outright; heavier structures require multiple charges. Issued in pairs. Single use.

**Det-Cord:** Explosive pressed into long cord coils on reels. Useful for punching holes in non-fortified walls or doors. Only responds to a command keyed specifically to the cord.

**Flash-Bang Bolo:** A pair of small banger grenades connected by a heavy wire. Used by hunters to immobilize and stun an opponent.

**H-35/H-37 Lasmore Mine:**

Low-profile proximity mine in two variants. The H-35 uses a laser-trigger beam across the zone — effective indoors, detectable with careful observation. The H-37 uses a magnetic trigger — detonates on vehicle, warbot, or ACPA contact; infantry do not trigger it; undetectable without a sensor sweep. Both variants disarm with HEAVY MACHINERY if the location is known.

**K4 Breaching Charge:**

![[Assets/Technology/Ordnance/K4 Breaching Charge.png]]

Shaped directional charge designed to focus its entire blast into a single surface. The placing side takes no damage — all force is directed through the target. Breaches doors and interior bulkheads outright; heavier barriers require specialist assessment. Single use.

**ATMN-2 Magnetic Anti-Tank Mine ("Tank Toaster"):**

![[Assets/Technology/Ordnance/ATMN-2 Tank Toaster Anti-Tank Mine.png|597]]

Magnetic anti-vehicle mine. Latches onto any ferrous surface — vehicle hulls, warbots, ACPA. Detonates on contact with a vehicle-class target; unaugmented infantry do not trigger it. Armor-piercing. Hull or chassis breach on detonation regardless of crew survival. Cannot be detected by observation alone — requires a sensor sweep. Avalon-Arienai manufacture.
