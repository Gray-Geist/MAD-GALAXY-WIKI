# Netrunning

*Netrunning is the hacking, slicing, and navigation of the digital realm — the Extranet or other networks — allowing infiltration and control of systems and data.*

---

## Overview

**Netrunners** (also called **slicers** or **hackers**) use specialized equipment, skills, and cybernetic enhancements to interface directly with the digital world. They access and manipulate data, control devices, and bypass security systems. At higher tiers, netrunners "jack in" to the Extranet — a vast cyberspace where they interact with systems and disrupt adversaries' operations.

> Scientists have found that **females adapt NeuroSyn use to netrunning more effectively** than male counterparts, leading to a non-trivial advantage in the field. This does not mean there are no male netrunners — but the field is disproportionately populated by females, especially in military and paramilitary contexts, where female operators often further augment themselves physically to keep up with male counterparts.

## Tiers of Netrunning

### Tier 0 — Old-School Battleboards
Pre-Gen 1 cyberware portable battleboards. Outdated to most, but still useful. Can be upgraded for better performance and moderate data transfer rates. Handheld design offers mobility — but without a [[NeuroSyn & Cybernetics|Syn]], manual operation slows reaction time and limits overall utility.

### Tier 1 — Personal Link
The basic form of modern hacking. Uses a **personal link** (interface plug) — cyberware usually installed in the wrist — to control systems by physically connecting to a port. Enables simple tasks: controlling doors, vehicle computers, and other devices through illicit malware. Great for easy data transfer and minor hacks; its low capacity prevents access to deep cyberspace. Nearly ubiquitous since all standard Syns include a personal link.

### Tier 2 — Cybergoggles / Virtu
Riskier yet more effective. Uses a personal link alongside **cybergoggles** (often modified braindance helmets) or **Virtu** to navigate the Net in 3D. Allows deeper access and faster data transfer — but physical strain escalates exponentially. **Overheating nervous systems** can lead to dangerously high body temperatures, necessitating external cooling (ice-filled bathtubs, modified air conditioners, adapted refrigerators). Mostly favored by low-level gangers, adventurous novices, or those with no other choice.

### Tier 3 — Professional Street Netrunner
Specialized equipment akin to Tier 2, upgraded in every aspect:
- High-capacity NeuroSyn linked directly to the brain for enhanced performance and rapid data transfer
- **Full-body cooling suit** (extreme transfer rates could otherwise boil the netrunner's blood)
- **Netrunner station** — specialized chair (sometimes a modified dental or massage chair) for prolonged comfort and additional cooling without hampering circulation; includes surveillance, medical, and vital monitoring tools

With this setup, skilled users can access deep Net data, control entire building systems, and compete against megacorp or government netrunners.

### Tier 4 — Corporate/Government Elite
Top-tier gear exclusively for elite professionals hired by megacorporations or star nation governments. Features cutting-edge station upgrades: **life support, cryostasis, and blood-cooling systems**. These netrunners are supported by extensive server banks and often work in **teams** — engaged in both offensive actions against rival companies and defending their employers' systems.

### Tier 5 — Black Ops
Black operations-level netrunning gear. At first glance unremarkable compared to Tier 4's flashy setups — appears to be only augmented individuals with neural boosters and cybernetically enhanced equipment. However, Tier 5 is primarily used by **hunter-killer agents** tasked with combating renegade hackers and rogue intelligences.

Key capabilities: **exceptional performance without requiring a netrunning station**; users retain **full mobility and awareness**, enabling remote cyberspace access from anywhere, direct attacks on enemy netrunners, and the ability to counter unauthorized network intrusions.

## Cyber Running Terminology

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **ICE** | Intrusion Countermeasures Electronics — security programs protecting data and preventing intrusion |
| **Black ICE** | Anti-personnel ICE capable of killing the intruding slicer (e.g., Hellhound, Firestarter, Soulkiller) |
| **Demon** | Black ICE intelligent systems operating Control Nodes; can target a Cyber Runner in physical space (Meatspace) |
| **Cyberdeck** | A computer that converts brainwaves into electrical impulses; required for Cyber Running |
| **Program** | Software slotted into a Cyberdeck — divided into Boosters, Attackers, and Defenders |
| **Slot** | A chunk of Cyberdeck memory allocated to a program; better Cyberdecks have more slots |
| **Control Node** | A virtual space location enabling control of a real-world device |
| **Floor** | Cyberspace area containing one program or virtual object |
| **Elevator** | Cyberspace object allowing travel between floors |
| **Deep Dive** | Connect into the Net via deep dive port; requires full Virtu capability |
| **Meatspace** | The physical world (derogatory Cyber Runner slang) |
| **Meat Action** | Actions made in the physical world (derogatory) |
| **NET Action** | Action taken inside virtual cyberspace |
| **Shell Trader** | Nomadic buyer/seller of illegal programs, usually based in Netspace |
| **Bodyweight Suit** | Suit for Cyber Runners with built-in armor, Cyberdeck, capable storage, and sometimes life support |
| **Netrun** | To interface with the Net and/or use it to slice into Data Fortresses |

## Security System Types

*"When safety is first, you last."* — MegaCorp advert tagline

### Advanced Systems
*(Listed in descending difficulty to counter — most difficult first)*

| System | How It Works | Why It's Hard to Beat |
|---|---|---|
| **Multi-Latch** (Multi-Modal Auth) | Combines Bio-EM, vascular patterns, and Syn recognition | Requires coordinated compromise of ALL systems simultaneously |
| **Bio-EM** (Bio-Electromagnetic Field Scanning) | Reads each person's unique bio-electromagnetic field and its fluctuations | Nearly impossible to replicate without perfect cloning of neural structure |
| **PR-ID** (Pulse Resonance ID) | Sends harmless resonance pulse through the body; maps internal organs, bones, and tissues | Internal anatomy is far harder to forge than fingerprints or retinas; severed limbs fail without live circulatory response |
| **Syn Key** (Syn Recognition) | Reads specific Syn brainwave patterns from pre-registered thoughts or memories | Requires the actual mind of the authorized person; detects coercion via stress hormones |
| **VPR** (Vascular Pattern Recognition) | Infrared scanners analyze blood flow through veins; thermal imaging confirms live authorized individual | Requires live blood circulation; detects coercion via adrenaline surges |
| **Vicog** (Sentient VI Recognition) | VI analyzes microexpressions, vocal patterns, and behavioral cues in real-time | Hard to spoof even with advanced impersonation tech |
| **Spectro** (DNA Spectroscopy) | Laser spectrometers analyze DNA fragments in real-time from skin cells or saliva | Detects spliced or artificially edited DNA |
| **Bios** (Behavioral Biometrics) | Monitors how an individual moves, types, or interacts with devices | Gait, typing rhythm, and hand gestures are nearly impossible to forge precisely |

### Basic Systems
*(Common, affordable, and exploitable)*

| System | How It Works | Common Countermeasures |
|---|---|---|
| **Keystring** (Passcode Panel) | Numeric/alphanumeric passcode via keypad, holographic interface, or Syn notification | Social engineering, surveillance, brute-force software |
| **Mag-Key** (Magnetic Keycard) | Physical card swipe or digital Syn-to-HoloSync receiver swipe | Cloning, theft, interception, weak encryption exploits |
| **RFID Tags** | Embedded chips in wearables, prosthetics, or under skin communicating with nearby readers | Signal-mimicking devices; though implanted tags add complexity via biological data integration |
| **Touch-ID** (Biometric Touch Scanners) | Fingerprints or handprints compared to pre-registered database | Severed limbs, synthetic replicas, hacked databases |
| **VoxLock** (Voice Recognition) | Vocal pitch, tone, and cadence analyzed against registered identity | Recordings, synthesized voice software, or forcing the authorized person to speak |
| **Analog Latch** (Mechanical Lock and Key) | Physical key inserted into traditional lock | Lockpicking, key duplication, brute force |
