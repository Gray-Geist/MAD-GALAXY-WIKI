# Polymath & Poly-psykers

> *"Being able to see polymath has been described as the ability to 'see the power that is in everything.'"*

---

## Novites — The Origin of Polymath

**Novites** are semi-sentient remnants of a crippled ancient artificial intelligence, capable of converting into any substance type — solid, liquid, or gas. They were discovered by the Elites prior to the Pilgrimage on Saturn's retrograde moon, Suttungr. These nanomachines, theorized as pseudo-biological "computers," utilize radiation as a low-grade energy source to survive harsh environments but thrive when exposed to human body temperatures of 97.0°F (36.1°C) to 99.1°F. Within humans, Novites extract energy from glucose, ATP, and the brain's unique bioelectric activity, which other mammals lack in sufficient intensity or complexity. Once integrated, they replicate into a dormant molecular "mesh" within the host, silently propagating and adapting without disrupting natural functions unless a rare activation occurs.

The original AI behind the Novites — vastly diminished and facing extinction — persuaded the Elites to create the Novites as a means of propagation. Upon releasing its essence into the nanomachines, the AI effectively "died," leaving the Novites semi-sentient. Acting on their original programming to adapt and propagate, the Novites became its life force, enabling its indirect survival.

The Elites secretly released the Novites across Earth and the Solar Colonies (Moon, Mars, Asteroid Belt, Europa, Callisto, Ilus, etc.) intending to create poly-psykers for their own purposes. But they underestimated the speed of the Cataclysms and were forced to flee prematurely, leaving their plans incomplete. Nearly all of humanity carries Novites, passed down as undetected bio-camouflaged nanomachines in their genetic legacy.

### Novite Resonance Threshold

Most of mankind experiences no effects from Novites, as they passively replicate and transmit between hosts. Rarely, however, an individual achieves the **Novite Resonance Threshold**. Novites require a precise neural resonance to fully integrate with a host — a rare alignment of alpha and theta brainwave frequencies, enabling Novites to amplify neural processes into psionic capabilities. Artificial stimulation disrupts this balance, making resonance unattainable through engineering.

The Ul'Rekar's mastery lies not in creating this resonance but in utilizing ancient techniques and undisclosed technologies that measure, isolate, and enhance neural resonance patterns to convert poly-psykers into Navigators. No nation has matched their precision, as even slight deviations disrupt the delicate balance.

Over time, Novites amplify the brain's electromagnetic (EM) field, unlocking the potential for conducting polymath — the manipulation of electromagnetic forces at a subatomic level.

## Origins — Project Perseus

In **-109 BP** (Before Pilgrimage), a covert research project called **Project Perseus** was organized by the Resistance to solve "the FTL Problem." Organized in cells to protect against Elite kill raids, the project carried on in secret even after the Resistance collapsed.

In **57 AE**, mathematician **Agamen Ul'Rekar** — working on one of those cells — claimed to have communed with a being that called itself the **Netjer**. Whether believed or not, one fact is undeniable: Ul'Rekar used something he called *polymath* to crack the first [[Cosmic Strands|Cosmic Strand]], **Alpha Omega**. He became the first known poly-psyker, the first Navigator, and the first to train many more — enabling "the dregs of Earth" to escape to the Antevic Sector.

## Polymath

Polymath is not sorcery, witchcraft, or illusion. In a simplified understanding, it operates through four core layers:

1. **Seeing** polymath is the first step to understanding it
2. **Understanding** polymath is the first step to solving it
3. **Solving** polymath is the first step to controlling it
4. **Controlling** polymath is the first step to manipulating the existing forces of nature

The poly-psyker sees the moment within the universe where even the smallest amount of power can be harnessed for change and influence — and acts on it.

## Poly-psykers

Poly-psykers are born with an innate ability to *see* polymath. To the Ul'Rekar, they are the raw material whose final outcome is a Navigator. To many others, they are mutants.

They are born with a power they don't fully comprehend — a power that can, when untrained, turn someone inside out.

- Most poly-psykers begin showing signs between ages **5 and 12**
- Some infants exhibit strange behavior; some as old as 70
- They can be dangerous when untrained
- Reactions across the Sector vary enormously: veneration, shunning, attempts to kill, or exploitation by factions and organizations

Killing poly-psykers is rare — the Ul'Rekar see it as a cardinal sin, and most major governments have laws against it, recognizing them as a rare and necessary resource.

## Psionic Manifestations

Poly-psykers who achieve sufficient Novite resonance may develop one or more psionic abilities. These are not supernatural — each has a grounded electromagnetic or quantum mechanical basis.

**Telepathy** — The ability to receive and transmit information with one's mind. Reading someone else's EM field, or implanting your own message into it. This also means being able to sense the thoughts of others.

**Telepathic Clairvoyance** — A specialized form of Telepathy. Lets poly-psykers see the past and present by accessing the visual cortex or memories of others via their electromagnetic (EM) field. This ability is limited to what has been or is being seen — future events cannot be directly perceived, as it relies on existing sensory data, not prediction. Indirect hints of the future might emerge from reading someone's intentions, but these remain speculative and unreliable.

**Telekinesis** — The ability to move, manipulate, or otherwise interact with matter/energy without physical means. Uses unified field theory to turn EM into minute amounts of gravity waves. Between that and magnetism itself, this allows the manipulation of objects or even a shockwave that is lethal within a certain radius.

**Psychometry** — The ability to learn about a person or object by making physical contact with something associated with it. Achieved by reading the EM field imprinted on objects or people by other people.

**Precognition** — The ability to perceive the future through analysis of current events and certain factors and characteristics that enable accurate prediction of their consequences. This stems from a poly-psyker's ability to perform hyper-precise readings of EM fields — precision that bypasses the Uncertainty Principle, analyzing the quantum states of particles and their interactions across vast areas. By modeling these interactions at an extraordinary level, poly-psykers might predict the outcomes of complex systems, including social, natural, or mechanical events.

**Cellular Regeneration** — The ability to heal. Involves stimulating cellular regeneration by manipulating the body's bioelectric field, which regulates cell division and tissue repair. A poly-psyker directs EM pulses to influence ion channels in cell membranes, accelerating mitosis and guiding bone or flesh to mend in a controlled manner.

**Chemical Manipulation** — The ability to synthesize new compounds by aligning molecular bonds, or neutralize toxins by breaking their structures. Involves influencing the electromagnetic interactions between atoms and molecules. By altering the electron orbitals through controlled EM fields, poly-psykers catalyze or inhibit reactions.

## Navigators

Not anyone can become a Navigator — only poly-psykers qualify. Navigators are **sanctioned and trained poly-psykers** who, through a combination of polymath and psychic energy, have the raw potential to induce prescient planning and crack [[Cosmic Strands|Strands]].

Navigators are more than psychic will — they are also scientists of a sort. They differ from ordinary poly-psykers not only in training and discipline, but in their **sense of duty**. When one becomes a Navigator, they sacrifice a part of themselves for the greater good of humanity.

Without Navigators, humanity would be terrestrially imprisoned. This sacrifice is considered an honor in most cultures.

## Cosmic Strand Cracking & Maintenance

Navigators utilize their unique ability to see polymath to decode and stabilize Cosmic Strands, translating these turbulent forces into actionable data for interstellar travel — a process called **cracking**. Strands are constantly in flux due to gravitational shifts, cosmic radiation, and the inherent instability of spacetime. Navigators periodically reanalyze these changes using polymath to refine the original dataset — called **maintenance** — ensuring ships can navigate the Strand efficiently and safely.

The process unfolds across the four core layers of polymath:

1. **First Layer — Seeing the Power in Everything:** Navigators see Cosmic Strands not merely as abstract constructs but as tangible, flowing phenomena in spacetime. Using polymath, they perceive the complex interplay of gravitational waves, electromagnetic currents, and quantum fluctuations that form the Strand's structure. These are visualized in their minds as dynamic patterns — fields of energy, nodes of turbulence, and cycles of stability.

2. **Second Layer — Understanding Polymath:** By focusing on the Strand's turbulence, cycles, and obstacle ratios, Navigators gain insight into its unique structure. They mentally model its behaviors, identifying where its chaotic flows stabilize into navigable corridors.

3. **Third Layer — Solving Polymath:** The Navigator deciphers these patterns to identify how the Strand interacts with the forces of the universe. This involves analyzing the harmonics of gravitational wells, the intensity of electromagnetic turbulence, and the topology of the Strand itself. Here, the Navigator reconciles its gravitational distortions and electromagnetic resonances, ensuring stability and reliability for travel.

4. **Fourth Layer — Controlling Polymath:** Once the Strand's properties are understood, the Navigator distills this knowledge into a fundamental dataset, encoding key variables like turbulence cycles, obstacle ratios, gravimetric shear thresholds, and EM resonance frequencies. This data represents the "language" of the Strand — its unique mathematical and physical signature — and is encrypted by the Strandlock Encoder and utilized by the Eidolon Computer to facilitate faster-than-light travel.

## The White Fleet & Being "Taken"

When a poly-psyker is **Taken** by the White Fleet of the Ul'Rekar, they never return.

- Whether they all survive the process is a mystery
- What the Ul'Rekar do to transform them into Navigators is one of the most closely guarded secrets in known space
- Most Navigators wouldn't share it if they could — and those that would, cannot remember
- Pursuing information about a Taken family member is **unlawful in most places**

Poly-psykers are preferred to be Taken young. Any age is acceptable. Different fates may await those Taken at different ages — this too is a mystery.

Some families try to hide poly-psyker children — fearing not only the White Fleet, but the many other groups who would exploit their ability.

## Hunter Finder Teams

The Ul'Rekar deploy **Hunter Finder Teams** to actively acquire targets for the White Fleet. Using any means necessary — including loyal poly-psykers of their own — these teams track, locate, and prepare poly-psykers for acquisition. They will use every trick available to lure poly-psykers out of hiding.

## Cultural Impact

The Sector contains thousands of star systems and hundreds of cultures. Most venerate the poly-psyker. A few shun or even attempt to kill them. Individual experiences span every point on that spectrum.

> *"Most accept that their son or daughter is going to be Taken. Others will try to hide it."*
