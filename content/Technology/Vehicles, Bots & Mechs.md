# Vehicles, Bots & Mechs

*"Bots execute. Mechs endure."* — Novoset Loadout Chief, post-Inx Incursion After-Action Report

---

## Bots vs. Mechs

In Sector operations — where private military contractors chase fleeting contracts through contested Cold War proxy conflicts and kill-zones the size of star systems — the distinction between automated drones and piloted mech walkers defines tactical thresholds.

### Bots
VI-driven platforms from the likes of Tri-Tachyon assembly lines or Umbra wartime surplus. Bots prioritize **relentless execution**. They require no sustenance, no rest cycles, and no psychological latency — their architectures process threats with unyielding precision until structural failure or EMP cascades the system.

Entry-level units remain cost-effective for swarm tactics. Advanced variants — such as the Avalon-Arienai NPK-series with adaptive kill-algorithms — command premium pricing for their lethal autonomy. However, they lack the emergent complexity of human oversight.

**Critical Vulnerability:** Bots succumb to electromagnetic pulses via overloaded circuits and frozen servos — their digital architectures are vulnerable to waveform saturation.

### Mechs
Mechs integrate the pilot as a **symbiotic core** within hulks like the Arkadien M-47 "Ravager" or the Republic Vexar-series. The operator interfaces via bioelectric conduits along the neural spine — merging proprioceptive feedback with machine actuators. Pilot intent translates directly to torque; visceral instinct overrides subroutine delays, yielding emergent performance exceeding the sum of isolated components.

This **"Merge"** amplifies response curves — but exacts tolls. Neural feedback loops imprint phantom loads, leaving pilots with oil-tanged vertigo long after egress.

> *Scientists have found that females excel in utilizing the neural spine most effectively, leading to a non-trivial advantage when operating a mech — similar to the advantage found in [[NetOps]].*

**EMP Resilience:** Unlike bots, mechs employ **bio-shielded cores** — neural dampers woven with Faraday lattices that shunt surges through the pilot's wetware. The human nervous system's inherent redundancy acts as a biological buffer, grounding and redistributing the overload. This hybrid filtration is viable only in piloted systems; without the organic pilot's adaptive bioelectric field, the lattices destabilize, risking catastrophic feedback in purely automated frames.

## Ground Vehicles

**All Terrain Tactical Engagement Carrier (ATTEC):** A large repulsor vehicle with heavy armor and heavy weapons; often used as a command vehicle. First used by VTAC stationed on Phasia.

**Main Battle Tank (MBT):** Various types. Some are older models equipped with 120mm guns and tracks. The Republic Armorworks model has a 300mm main gun firing shells at incredible speeds. Some have both treads and repulsors — if one propulsion system fails, the other can still move the vehicle. Weight ranges from 50 to 200 metric tons depending on the type.

**Combat Sled (CS):** Armored personnel carrier originally designed by Republic Armorworks. Repulsor driven; quick and agile. Two pilot-drivers in the cockpit with room for a field commander, separated from the cargo bay. A relay screen mounted in the bay displays external holocams and receives holo-transmissions from command. Bay lit with red interior lights; carries 6 passengers in jump seats plus a gunner in a turret aft of the cockpit manning **"the Twins"** (twin-repeating chemical arm turret). Button in the bay opens the rear ramp — half opens down, the other half up.

**Med-Sled:** Armored ambulance built on the Combat Sled chassis. Stripped of its turret and jump seats; repulsor litters magnetically anchored to the walls; auto-doc installed where the turret was.

**Motorized Utility Light Equipment (MULE):** Fast attack ground vehicle armed with an N-50 and automatic rocket pods.

**Repulsor Bike ("Repo Bike"):** Single-seat open vehicle powered by repulsor drives. Three to four can be carried aboard stealth shuttles as auxiliary transportation.

**Tango:** A fast attack speeder.

**SWIM-marine:** Underwater personnel vehicle used to transport troops.

## Mechs

**HK-PP Mech *(Hunter-Killer Planet Pounder):*** Twelve-meter-tall walking armored battle mech. Bristling with heavy cannons above its squat torso and oversized legs. Some have shoulder-mounted missile launchers or the devastating **Mauler Gauss system cannons**. Equipped with chaff.

**Krieger:** A massive mech from the Savage Wars — the precursor to modern HK-PP mechs. If properly maintained, capable of going up against any current heavy mech.

**SLIC *(Close Support Gunships):*** Generally armed with autocannon pods and AGMs; larger versions can carry additional weapon systems, cargo, and passengers. The cargo deck has mounts for automatic chemical arms to fire out the open bay doors (one per side).

## Vehicle Weapons

**Anti-Air Torpedo:** Mobile (not man-portable) ground-to-air missile system. Larger than an Aero-Precision rocket — heavier payload and longer range.

**Burst Turret *(Vehicle-Mounted):*** Small gun turret mounted on ships or large vehicles. On a ship: intended for anti-personnel fire suppression from low altitudes.

**"The Twins":** Turret-mounted twin heavy repeating arms. Fires pressurized, explosive, rapid heavy rounds.

**N-80:** Special multi-barreled slagger — essentially eight N-50s spinning together to unleash a near-constant stream of chemical arm fire. **Vehicle or mech-mounted only** — too heavy for power armor.
