# Technology

Interstellar civilization runs on a handful of technologies so fundamental that their disruption would collapse human society overnight. The skip drive. The Cosmic Strands. The Navigators who crack them. The Extranet that carries information between the stars. Strip any one of these away and the Sector falls silent. Around this critical infrastructure, humanity has layered centuries of innovation — cybernetics, weaponry, artificial minds both legal and forbidden — each advancing the eternal project of making life longer, faster, deadlier, and stranger.
