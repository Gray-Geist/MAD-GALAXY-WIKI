# NeuroSyn & Cybernetics

*"Any sufficiently advanced technology is indistinguishable from magic."* — Ancient Earth proverb

---

## Overview

The **NeuroSyn** (colloquially *"Syn"*, plural *"Syns"*) is the **star of Gen 3 cybernetic technology** — a centralized operating system for all Gen 3 cybernetic implants. It operates as a control package loaded with cyberware and is considered so safe and unobtrusive that **most children are fitted with one**.

Learning to use a Syn is part of many **primary school curriculums**. Early installation correlates with better outcomes in adulthood. Those **not** fitted with a NeuroSyn are at a significant disadvantage: lower educational attainment, lower financial achievement, and reduced access to the Sector's ubiquitous connected infrastructure. Predatory high-interest loans are commonly offered to families who can't afford one. Others go the black market route.

---

## Standard NeuroSyn Package

Every NeuroSyn includes the following cyberware:
### Biomonitor
Monitors pulse, temperature, respiration, blood sugar, and other health indicators. Broadcasts data to the user's HUD in real time.

### Chipware Sockets (2)
Two sockets behind the ear or in the neck allow quick installation of up to two pieces of chipware, or the reading of memory chips.

### Holophone
A fully functional phone capable of local Extranet access, talk, text, and scheduling. Brain-controlled — no vocal commands required (though it responds to spoken or subvocalized commands for privacy). Mid-to-advanced add-ons convert a stream of thought into speech in real time. Runs a multitude of apps; many require a visual output (Chyron) or external touchscreen/controller for full functionality.

### CHYRON-HUD (aka Chyron or HUD)
Information from the Holophone, Biomonitor, and other cyberware is projected into the user's field of vision via direct connection to the optic nerve. Programs react to the user's thoughts or installed databases to: label objects and people, provide conversation transcripts, translate foreign languages in real time.

### Neural Link
The core hardware backbone for the NeuroSyn OS — provides physical neural connections and 5 dedicated neuralware slots, while the operating software integrates and controls all bundled features.

### Personal Link (Interface Plug)
A pull-out cord, usually embedded in the wrist, allowing direct linking to most electronic devices.

### Virtu Capability (Virtuality)
With upgraded software, allows the user to experience virtual presentations, performances, and simulations. Also allows Netrunners (slicers) to fully experience the Extranet during a deep dive. **Does not** allow Braindance experiences without additional hardware.

## Key Syn Features

### HoloForm
Without external cameras, how does a Syn represent you to others? Through **HoloForm software**. Because the Syn is connected to the brain, muscle movement data is deciphered and replicated as an animated 3D avatar — a near-perfect face emulation in real time. When you exchange holo numbers, a 3D virtual representation of your face is included, and when called, your representation is rendered in the other person's vision, animated live.

The Syn reads your brain patterns — which is how users dial contacts and communicate without speaking aloud. "Bad connection" bugs (repeating words, lag) can occur if the user is highly stressed or cognitively occupied.

### SAVI — Self-Adaptive VI
The **Self-Adaptive VI** ("SAVI," pronounced "Savvy") inside each Syn is not a true Virtual Intelligence, but its algorithm-driven learning routines feel close to one. It:
- Learns and stores your schedule, contacts, and day-to-day preferences
- Searches the Extranet data pool for contact information on anyone you ask about
- Prompts you about important events related to you and your contacts
- Can automatically send gifts to loved ones if you forget
- Available with a range of downloadable personality templates — voice types, quirks, accents *(rule of thumb: the more attractive the personality template, the more expensive it is)*

### Extranet Data Pool
Syns excel at turning information requests into useful summaries by crawling the Extranet data pool and filtering/summarizing results. They also access a wide range of entertaining content including text, audio, video, holo, and games. Storage is measured in **years** (based on statistical average usage) and can be upgraded.

### HoloSync
Short-range wireless protocol connecting the Syn to a wide range of devices — from appliances and vehicles to other Syns, drones, and cyberware. Through HoloSync, a Syn can:
- Monitor and manage household gear and supplies
- Transfer images, video, and money
- Display, record, or download from other connected devices
- Connect to TechHair for style and pattern downloads
- Connect to Biomonitors for gamified "damage displays" on a soldier's HUD
- Allow remote operation of modified vehicles via Garden Patches

### Weapon SmartLync
Every Syn is capable of running SmartLync — cyberware that links a weapon's sights to the user's HUD. Displays remaining ammunition, heat level, and diagnostic information in real time. **SmartLync ≠ Smart Weapon**: all Smart weapons are SmartLynced, but not all SmartLync weapons are Smart. Smart weapons require a Personal Link (Interface Plug) or Subdermal Grip to operate — they are fully brain-connected for maximum accuracy.

### No-Net Backup (Security Mode)
Syns run in ultra-security mode and **do not constantly expose themselves to the Extranet**. They interact with networks but cannot be sliced remotely to disable the user — they are designed to be "cut off" from other systems.

**Consequence:** There is **no automatic cloud backup**. All complex files of rule structures, learned behaviors, and customizations exist only in the physical device's onboard memory. These files can be **stolen, copied, or destroyed** — making a Syn a potential hostage or target. A Syn can be surgically removed.

*"Syn Stealer crimes"* are a known phenomenon enabled by spoofing tech that makes Syns believe they are being operated by their original user.

## The Black-Syn Market

Highly skilled cyberneticists can "jailbreak" a Syn — for example, making one's HoloForm transmission entirely anonymous. This process almost inevitably causes **degeneration**:

1. Jailbroken Syn begins as a **Gray-Syn** (degrading state)
2. Gray-Syn always eventually becomes a **Black-Syn** (fully degenerated)
3. Others' Syns display a warning: *"Potentially Dangerous"* when receiving a Black-Syn transmission

Reversing Black-Syn degeneration to appear normal is extremely difficult — and even if achieved, the Syn may always carry the "scar" of having been jailbroken.

**Why most forgo this:** Existing in society without a functioning Syn is very difficult. You cannot simply get another — it is the most personal cybernetic a person has.

*Speculation:* Some theorize there is a path to anonymity through self-programming — convincing one's own Syn (and thus oneself) of a new false identity by genuinely believing it.

## Syn Neuralware Add-Ons

### Veracity Suites (Neural Lie-Detection)
*"Truth-jacks"* or *"brain ticklers"* on the street — premium neuralware packages from manufacturers including Tri-Tachyon's **Empath-9**, Arienai's **Veritas Line**, and several boutique firms.

The suite allows the operator to establish a temporary direct neural link with a consenting — or restrained — subject via interface plugs. Once linked, the operator receives a real-time feed of:
- Subject's biomonitor data
- Micro-tremor patterns in vocal cords
- Subtle pupil response shifts
- Pheromone traces (if olfactory augments are present)
- Low-level emotional resonance bleed from the subject's own NeuroSyn or nervous system

Sophisticated algorithms flag probable deceptions with color-coded HUD overlays: **Green = likely truth · Amber = uncertainty · Red = probable falsehood**

**Limitations:** No Veracity Suite is infallible. Confidence rarely exceeds 85–90% under ideal conditions. Skilled operators can be trained to compartmentalize. Anti-interrogation cybernetics can spoof or dampen signals. Pain, drugs, or deliberate biofeedback can muddy readings entirely. Courts across the Sector treat Veracity evidence as **corroborative at best** — never definitive.

> *"The Suite doesn't tell you the truth — it only tells you when someone's working very hard not to."* — Sicheramt interrogators' joke

## HardLight Window Technology

**HardLight Window** tech combines a **solid durable barrier** with cutting-edge photoluminescent technology — allowing high-definition real-time projections on its surface that replicate direct sight while maintaining a physical divide.

Unlike glass (susceptible to shattering) or holograms (no physical substance), HardLight Windows provide:
- Complete isolation with clear observation
- Two-way toggleable visibility (connection or controlled anonymity as needed)
- Psychological advantage in interrogations or negotiations — face-to-face interaction without vulnerability
- Resistance to environmental hazards like pressure differentials, dangerous anomalies, or airtight quarantine requirements

**Applications:** transportation (cars, vessels), prisons, research facilities, starship brigs, and laboratories. Installed in most vehicles as a safety feature.

## Written Communication

Handwriting and physical paper are **quite rare**. The Sector's communication and record-keeping are dominated by digital systems — the Extranet and augmented reality render traditional writing practically unnecessary.

**What saved paper from extinction:** security. In an age where data can decapitate or coronate, securing information away from net-running slicers is invaluable. A physical document cannot be remotely accessed.

Education focuses on digital literacy, data coding, and VI management. True wood-derived paper has become a **relic** — valued as an artifact, a symbol of wealth, nostalgia, or rebellion against digital conformity. In remote or fringe areas of the Sector, handwriting and paper persist out of necessity.

Cross-culturally, handwritten messages carry **deep sentimental or symbolic meaning** — reserved for personal or ceremonial use. Subcultures exist that value handwriting and paper as **a form of resistance or artistry**.
