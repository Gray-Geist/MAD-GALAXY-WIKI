# Cyberware & Implants

*"More implants means more vulnerability to slicing."* — Standard Field Warning

> **Availability Codes:** Every implant and augmentation in this section carries an AV code indicating where and how it can be acquired. See [[Availability Codes]] for the full reference.

---

## Overview

Cyberware refers to cybernetic implants that enhance or replace biological systems with mechanical or electronic ones. Most combat and specialist cyberware requires a **military-grade cortical interface** to function — see the Cortical Interface section below.

> **The tradeoff:** Every additional implant increases vulnerability to electronic warfare and slicing. Heavy augmentation also carries documented psychological risk — a condition clinically termed **cyberpsychosis**, in which the accumulation of non-biological systems progressively erodes a person's coherence, identity, and grip on their own humanity. Military programs screen for it. Some operators push past the thresholds anyway.

## Cortical Interface

The cortical interface — colloquially **"thread"** — is the neural OS layer underlying most cybernetic augmentation in the Sector. A sub-cranial implant bonded to the prefrontal and limbic interface layers, it handles machine-language parsing, device communication, HUD output, and the command routing that advanced cyberware depends on.

The underlying protocol is standardized across manufacturers. Dozens of competing hardware lines exist — from civic-grade consumer units to restricted military substrate — but they speak the same fundamental language. What varies is capability ceiling, hardware quality, and who can legally obtain one.

**Civic-grade thread** handles communications, identity verification, financial transactions, basic HUD functions, and low-level device interaction. In the Sector's megacities and major stations, it is so deeply embedded in daily infrastructure that operating without one creates constant friction — not impossible, but every transaction, checkpoint, and connected system assumes you have one. Most people in connected urban environments are threaded. Most people on the frontier are not, or are running older hardware that the infrastructure has long since outpaced.

**Military-grade thread** is a different class of hardware entirely — not a better version of the civic unit but a fundamentally different substrate. Combat cyberware requires military-grade thread to function. A SmartLync implant running on civic hardware does nothing. The processing architecture, neural bonding depth, and bandwidth simply aren't there. Military-grade thread is restricted, expensive, faction-issued or black market, and encountered almost exclusively in active military personnel, intelligence operatives, and well-funded private operators.

Not everyone is threaded. Frontier populations, communities with religious or cultural objections to augmentation, the deliberately off-grid, and the very poor may have none at all. In those contexts the absence is unremarkable. In a megacity, an unthreaded person is marginal — someone the infrastructure was not built for.

The thread cannot be remotely disabled without specialized electronic warfare equipment targeting neural hardware specifically. It does not maintain open network connections — it interacts with external systems on demand. This is a security feature. The tradeoff is that there is no automatic backup. Everything the thread has processed, configured, and stored exists in the physical hardware. It can be taken. It can be destroyed.

In intelligence and military contexts a recovered thread — extracted from a captured or killed operative — is a significant asset. The hardware holds operational history, communication records, installed cyberware configuration, and behavioral data accumulated over years. Security measures exist: biometric locks, hardware encryption, dead-man wipe protocols that brick the device on unauthorized removal. None of them are perfect.

Surgical extraction under controlled conditions is one thing. Removal under field conditions causes neurological trauma — the thread is bonded to living tissue, not seated in a socket. Severity depends on method and hardware generation. The dead-man wipe exists partly for this reason: bricking the hardware on unauthorized removal may cause collateral neural damage, but that outcome is considered preferable to letting the thread be pulled intact. Operatives with high-value thread configurations understand what that calculus means for them in the field.

### Cortical Interface Tiers

Three hardware tiers are in circulation. Capability scales sharply between them — the civic unit handles daily infrastructure; only the military-grade and above support combat cyberware, electronic warfare, and NetOps operations.

| Tier | Formal Name | Cost | AV |
|---|---|---|---|
| Civic | Civic-Grade Cortical Interface | $1,800 | R |
| Military | Military-Grade Cortical Interface (CI) | $4,500 | MRR |
| Hardened | Military-Hardened Cortical Interface | $9,000 | MRRII |

**Civic-grade** is available through licensed augmentation clinics and a well-supplied grey market. Sufficient for infrastructure hacking and general COMTECH-dependent operations. Cannot be used for active target hacking or electronic warfare.

**Military-grade** is the minimum threshold for combat NetOps — active target hacking, electronic warfare, and all EW actions. Standard issue for Network Operations Specialists. Acquired through military procurement, specialist augmentation programs, or restricted black-market channels.

**Military-hardened** is the top-tier platform — full-spectrum signal hardening, counter-intrusion shielding, and extended broadcast range across all EW actions. Replaces military-grade rather than stacking on it; counts as one implant. Found in elite special operations programs and high-end black-market augmentation.

## Neural & Cognitive Implants

### Neural Interface

A cortical co-processor bonded to the prefrontal interface layer. It accelerates machine-language parsing and command execution, making everything from terminal work to weapons system interaction fractionally faster — and in practice, that fraction matters. One of the most widely available augmentations on the commercial market, it has become standard equipment for tech specialists, pilots, and anyone whose livelihood depends on fast system interaction.

> **Mechanics:** +1 COMTECH, passive and always active.

### B-DAT Neural Computer

*Biological-Digital Adaptive Translator.* A sub-cranial co-processor bonded to the language and data-processing centers. Where the Neural Interface optimizes command execution, the B-DAT integrates biological pattern-recognition with digital processing, making it superior for novel system analysis and linguistic translation. Researchers, intelligence analysts, and field operators working in unfamiliar systems tend to prefer it. The mechanical result is the same; the architecture underneath is not.

> **Mechanics:** +1 COMTECH, passive and always active. Functionally identical to the Neural Interface — the architectural distinction is lore only.

### Neural Shielding Implant

A subdermal mesh of bioelectric-dampening polymer woven around the neural column and cranial base. Provides complete immunity to neural arc effects — the bearer is unaffected by Valkyrie Neural Arc Grenades and any weapon or effect that disrupts unshielded neural tissue. It does not shield against TacLink disruption, SmartLync jamming, or other electronic warfare systems; those target external hardware, not neural tissue. Visible only under medical imaging. A specialist implant sought most urgently by those who have seen a neural arc detonate inside a formation.

> **Mechanics:** Complete immunity to neural arc effects — unaffected by the Valkyrie Neural Arc Grenade and any [NEURAL]-tagged weapon or effect. Does not protect against TacLink disruption or SmartLync jamming.

### Panic Inhibitor

A limbic system regulator implanted at the amygdala interface. It does not prevent panic — it caps it. When a bearer's fear response would spiral past the threshold of controlled fear into frenzy or catatonia, the inhibitor clamps the result: the worst the bearer experiences is flight rather than breakdown. The inhibitor has limits — it will not hold indefinitely, and a second catastrophic break in the same stretch resolves without its intervention. Widely used by veterans who know the difference between controlled fear and losing their mind under fire.

> **Mechanics:** Once per Stretch: automatically caps a panic result of 11 or higher at 10 — the worst outcome is Flee rather than Frenzy or Catatonic. Does not reduce the panic roll result, only caps the outcome. Does not prevent panic rolls. One activation per Stretch — a second qualifying roll in the same Stretch resolves normally.

## SmartLync Systems

SmartLync is the generic designation for a family of neural-optical interface implants that enable **smart-weapon** capability — the ability to use firearms with integrated tracking, curve-fire, and target-lock systems. Without a SmartLync implant installed, smart weapons fire as conventional firearms.

Three tiers are in circulation:

### SmartLync Basic

The commercial entry point. Implanted at the ocular nerve cluster. Enables smart weapons at full capability. Does not provide tracking bonuses — the bearer can use smart weapons, but the system provides no active enhancement beyond enabling the technology. Available through licensed augmentation clinics and a significant grey market.

> **Mechanics:** Enables [SMART]-tagged weapons at full capability. Without this implant, smart weapons fire as standard CHEM. Does not provide tracking bonus dice — that requires SmartLync Military or Elite.

### SmartLync Military

The upgrade used by most professional military operators. Includes all SmartLync Basic capability and adds active target-tracking firmware: when firing against a target that was in the bearer's line of sight at the start of the engagement, the system provides a meaningful aim enhancement. Harder to jam than the commercial unit — disrupting it requires genuine electronic warfare capability. Standard issue for Republic special operations and Novoset Neural Enforcer units.

> **Mechanics:** All SmartLync Basic capability, plus: +1 bonus die on RANGED COMBAT rolls when firing a [SMART] weapon against a target that was in line of sight at the start of the Round. Harder to jam than the Basic unit — jamming requires COMTECH at −1 die. Replaces SmartLync Basic; counts as one implant.

### SmartLync Elite

The top-tier platform. Includes all Military capability, enhanced jamming resistance, and predictive intercept firmware. The system can extrapolate a target's position through a single round of full cover — it tracks movement vectors, not just visual contact. Used by intelligence operatives and the most advanced special operations elements. Replaces Basic or Military hardware rather than stacking on top of them.

> **Mechanics:** All SmartLync Military capability, plus: jamming requires COMTECH at −2 dice. The bearer may maintain tracking on a target that moves into full cover for one Round — the +1 bonus die applies on the next attack against that target through cover (standard cover penalty still applies). Replaces Basic or Military; counts as one implant.

## Optical Implants

### Low-Light Optical Implant

Replacement optical units with adaptive photon-amplification. Removes all observation penalties from low-light and near-darkness — the bearer sees in dim conditions as clearly as in full illumination. Does not provide thermal imaging or penetrate complete darkness. Visible as a faint luminescence in the iris under direct light; concealable with tinted contacts. One of the more common military augmentations, issued or purchased by soldiers who expect to fight where the lights are out.

> **Mechanics:** Removes all OBSERVATION penalties from low-light and near-darkness conditions. Does not provide thermal imaging or penetrate complete darkness.

### Cybernetic Eyes

Full ocular replacement units. The basic variant restores functional vision — no enhancement, but corrects all impairment and replaces destroyed eyes. The full-spectrum variant integrates low-light amplification, a thermal layer, and variable magnification: the bearer sees in low light without penalty, detects heat signatures through light cover and smoke, and magnifies at a level equivalent to field binoculars. The thermal layer is always active; the magnification is on demand. Visible as a subtle luminescence under direct light. Counts as one implant regardless of how many eyes are replaced.

> **Mechanics:** **Basic:** Functional replacement only — no mechanical bonus. **Full Spectrum:** Removes low-light OBSERVATION penalties (passive). +1 OBSERVATION on all sight-based rolls. Thermal layer detects heat signatures through light cover and smoke at GM discretion. Variable magnification equivalent to field binoculars at Short–Medium range.

### Advanced Optics

Full-spectrum military optical replacement with a multi-layer sensor stack — thermal, low-light, variable magnification, and ballistic overlay. The thermal layer negates low-light penalties passively, always. The bearer can focus the system's processing for enhanced performance in a given context — long-range precision or environmental combat compensation — but the system cannot optimise for both simultaneously. While highly effective, the system must be given time to sync with biological components and thread at the start of each operation. A more expensive and capable platform than Cybernetic Eyes, used by dedicated marksmen and long-range operators.

> **Mechanics:** Thermal layer negates low-light OBSERVATION penalties passively. Choose one benefit per Shift at the start of each Shift (cannot change mid-Shift): **(A)** one free re-roll on a RANGED COMBAT roll — no Stress cost, once per Shift; or **(B)** ignore cover penalties on RANGED COMBAT rolls at Medium range for the Shift.

## Combat Augmentation

### Reflex Augmentation

A spinal reflex amplifier and fast-twitch motor override. Provides a reaction speed advantage specifically in combat contexts — initiative and evasion from incoming fire. It does not improve general physical performance, agility, or movement; the upgrade is narrow and deliberate, targeting the milliseconds between perceiving a threat and responding to it. Recipients describe a subtle perceptual lag: the body moves before the conscious decision registers. That sensation never fully goes away.

> **Mechanics:** +1 AGILITY on initiative draws and dodge actions (MOBILITY rolls made to dodge ranged attacks) only. Does not apply to general AGILITY-based rolls, movement, or other MOBILITY uses.

### Serrick Combat OS

![[cyberware_serrick_os.png]]

A sub-cortical firmware layer developed by Serrick Defense that compresses the gap between threat perception and physical response to something that stops looking like human reaction time. In the moment the OS fires — triggered consciously or not — the bearer moves through a combat beat at a speed that bystanders describe less as fast and more as *wrong*. The lag between decision and action disappears. The body is already done before the mind finishes the thought.

Recipients report the activation as a discontinuity: one moment they are assessing the threat, the next it is resolved, and there is a half-second of catching up to what their hands already did. Serrick documentation describes the involuntary activations as a feature. Operators who have served alongside fully Serrick-loaded personnel use words like *unsettling* and *not quite right* and, when pressed, *I'm glad they're on our side*.

Military black programs and the deep black market only. The hardware is restricted not because it is rare but because the people who carry it become something the chain of command finds difficult to fully account for.

> **Mechanics:** **Passive — Neural Priority Queue:** Always draw 2 initiative cards at the start of each Round; keep the better one. **Active — Serrick Burst:** Up to 3 activations per combat, no action cost. Each activation: Push one skill roll without gaining Stress, and take one additional Quick Action beyond the normal limit on that turn. **Costs (cumulative): 1st** — no cost. **2nd** — after combat ends, cannot clear Stress Responses for one full Shift (Stress still reduces normally during that window). **3rd** — as 2nd, plus immediately make a Stress Response roll at current Stress level. The Shift cost does not stack in duration — a bearer who hits all 3 activations carries one Shift of locked Stress Responses plus the immediate Stress Response roll from the 3rd activation.

## Stealth & Infiltration Implants

![[cyberware_implants_optical_camo.gif]]

### Optic Camouflage

A subdermal light-bending layer across the body surface, slaved to a neural activation trigger. When active, the bearer's visual profile becomes a faint translucent distortion — present at the limits of the technology, but functionally invisible to unaided eyes at any distance. Provides no heat masking whatsoever: IR sensors, thermal imaging, and TacLink threat feeds detect the bearer as normal. The system disrupts under kinetic stress — firing a weapon or taking damage breaks the matrix; reactivation requires a moment's attention.

Acquisition requires either military channels or the specialist black market. In civilian contexts, usage without authorization is heavily restricted — encountering optic camouflage in the field is a meaningful tactical signal about who you're dealing with.

**Detection methods used by high-security facilities:** seismic and weight sensors in flooring, air pressure and microphone arrays sensitive enough to detect movement, and dedicated anti-camouflage thermal scanning.

> **Mechanics:** When active: all visual OBSERVATION rolls to detect the bearer are at +2 difficulty. No heat masking — IR sensors and thermal imaging detect normally. Activation/deactivation: Quick Action each. Disrupts when the bearer fires a weapon or takes damage; reactivation requires a Quick Action.

### Thermo-Optic Camouflage

Where standard optic camouflage masks visual profile only, thermo-optic systems mask heat signature as well — the full package. Found only on the most advanced corporate strike teams and elite government units.

The constraint is thermal physics. The heat has to go somewhere. The system holds it internally until it can be bled off during deactivation or rest. Sustained active use generates significant discomfort that compounds into genuine physiological strain: operators can maintain the system for a limited window before the heat load forces deactivation. When the system goes offline after extended use, it vents the suppressed heat as a brief thermal flare — a sharp IR signature that, for one moment, makes the bearer maximally visible to the sensors that were looking for them.

Top-tier black market or military acquisition only. The limitation is not the technology. It is the body wearing it.

> **Mechanics:** When active: all OBSERVATION rolls to detect the bearer — visual and thermal — are at +2 difficulty. Activation/deactivation: Quick Action each. After 3 consecutive Rounds of active use, make a STAMINA roll — on failure, system must deactivate; suppressed heat vents as a thermal flare (OBSERVATION rolls to detect by heat signature automatically succeed for 1 Round). Disrupts when the bearer fires a weapon or takes damage.

### Behavioral Imprint-Synced Faceplate

A synthetic facial overlay with deep muscle-mimicry servos slaved to a loaded Behavioral Imprint data package. The hardware is inert without a compatible Imprint installed — the Faceplate is the chassis; the Imprint is what makes it function.

**Behavioral Imprints** are neural-behavioral data packages that define who the Faceplate becomes, acquired through three methods that determine quality: *Partial* imprints sourced from surveillance records or public biometrics; *Full* imprints from neural scans or restricted biometric databases; *Perfect* imprints from a fresh corpse or complete behavioral mapping. Each Imprint is target-specific — a new Imprint is required for each identity. An experienced operator equipped with a high-quality Faceplate is virtually indistinguishable from their target at any level of scrutiny the Imprint quality supports.

> **Mechanics:** Inert without a Behavioral Imprint consumable installed. When active with a loaded Imprint, grants MANIPULATION bonus dice when impersonating the target: **Partial** +1 die (duration: 1 Stretch); **Full** +2 dice, automated scans pass (duration: 1 Shift); **Perfect** +3 dice, all scans pass (duration: until deactivated). Compromise triggers are: a) Stress while active (MANIPULATION roll to maintain), b) Panic (auto-deactivates), c) extended wear 4+ hours (Stress +1/hour beyond 4 hours). Deactivation: Quick Action. The Behavioral Imprint consumable has no Biochemistry slot cost — only the Faceplate hardware counts against cyberware capacity.

## Armor & Protection

### Subdermal Armor

A thin ballistic-polymer layer bonded beneath the dermis, covering the torso. The protection is real and permanent — it stacks with worn armor. It is lighter and far less visible than the Combat Chassis Subdermal, detectable under medical imaging but not through clothing or casual contact, and carries no social consequence. The standard protection implant for security contractors, bodyguards, and intelligence operatives who need armor they can't visibly wear.

> **Mechanics:** +1 AR permanently, stacks with worn armor. Detectable under medical imaging; no social penalty.

### Combat Chassis Subdermal

Rigid ballistic-ceramic plating bonded beneath the dermis across the torso, upper arms, and thighs. Heavier and more extensive than standard subdermal armor — the protection reflects that. The tradeoff is visibility: the plating creates unnatural rigidity and surface contour beneath clothing that a trained eye reads immediately. Medical scanning reveals it without question. Military personnel require program authorization. Civilian ownership is illegal under most faction law. Standard issue to T4 Augmented Operatives who need maximum survivable protection built in from the start.

> **Mechanics:** +1 AR permanently, stacks with worn armor. Social interactions where physical appearance matters: −1 die on MANIPULATION rolls. BCL-3 Battle Armor requires custom fitting to wear; MCA-7 and lighter fits normally.

## Cybernetic Limbs

Cybernetic limb replacement ranges from functional prosthetics to purpose-built military hardware. All share the same basic architecture: an internal power supply, a mechanical actuation system, and a surface finish that varies from obviously mechanical to cosmetically indistinguishable from biological tissue.

### Basic Replacement

The utilitarian baseline. Functional, unmistakably mechanical, and serviceable. Standard skill rolls requiring the replaced limb suffer a penalty — the actuation lag of mechanical systems compared to biological. The basic unit is fully modular; it can be upgraded at any point with additional components fitted by a competent ripperdoc or military surgeon:

- **Armored Shell** — ballistic plating over the limb chassis; provides additional protection on the affected location
- **Boosted Motorics** — high-performance actuators that eliminate the skill penalty and bring the limb to full human performance
- **Built-in Tool** — a concealed integrated tool of choice: cutting torch, surgical kit, lock interface, or similar
- **Extendable Blade** — a retractable nano-edge blade housed in the forearm; improves close combat capability
- **Lightweight Materials** — carbon-composite swap; reduces the limb's carry weight
- **Living Skin Shell** — synthetic skin overlay that passes casual visual inspection; conceals the replacement from all but medical scanning or hands-on examination
- **Synthetic Nerve Switches** — bio-electronic nerve bridging that allows the bearer to push rolls involving the replaced limb
- **Biomimetic Shunts** — deep biological integration hardware; expands the bearer's cyberware capacity by one slot

> **Mechanics:** −1 die on skill rolls requiring the replaced limb. Power Supply 3 — make a power supply roll after each Stretch of heavy use; at Supply 0 the limb locks in its last position until recharged (Full Action with power cell). Fully modular — upgrades can be fitted at any point (see upgrade list above).

### Enhanced Replacement

A pre-fitted limb with enhanced motorics and living skin shell installed at manufacture. Full human performance without the skill penalty. The skin shell passes casual visual inspection — medical scanning or physical examination by someone who knows the bearer will reveal it, but it holds at social distance. The standard choice for operators who need function and concealment without bespoke configuration.

> **Mechanics:** No skill penalty — Boosted Motorics bring the limb to full human performance. Living Skin Shell passes casual visual inspection; medical scanning or hands-on examination reveals the replacement. Power Supply 3. Further upgrades can be fitted.

### Combat Replacement

Military-grade. Armored shell, enhanced motorics, and synthetic nerve switches pre-installed — full performance, armor bonus on the affected location, and the ability to push rolls involving that limb. Visibly mechanical: no skin shell, construction immediately apparent. Social friction is the price of combat capability. Standard issue for Tier 4 Augmented Operatives requiring battlefield-optimized replacement.

> **Mechanics:** No skill penalty. +1 AR on the replaced limb, stacks with worn armor on the affected location. Skills using this limb can be Pushed. Power Supply 3. Visibly mechanical — no skin shell. Social interactions where physical appearance matters: −1 die on MANIPULATION rolls.

## Physical Enhancement

### Muscle Augmentation

Full-body synthetic muscle fiber overlay bonded to the existing musculature. Grants significant permanent increases distributed between strength and agility at installation — the distribution is locked in at surgery and cannot be changed. The procedure carries substantial surgical difficulty and is considered restricted military augmentation; civilian black market access exists, but surgery quality is unreliable in ways that become apparent under stress. The most transformative physical augmentation available through standard channels — and one of the most permanent decisions a body can carry.

> **Mechanics:** +3 points distributed freely between STR and AGI at installation — distribution is permanent and cannot be changed. No single attribute may exceed its tier cap from this bonus alone. Surgery difficulty: MEDICAL AID −3.

## HUD Software & Enhancements

![[cyberware_viv_demo.gif]]

### Electronically Enhanced Vision (EEV)

A "dumb mode" software suite running through the HUD. Allows the user to scan electronic-enabled objects for intelligence — primarily as a means of remotely assessing electronic devices from a distance. When the HUD focuses on an object long enough, the EEV completes its scan and displays one of several icons: whether slicing is possible, whether the object contains explosive or toxic hazards, whether it can be designated for a strike, or whether it can be accessed remotely.

### Virtual Intelligence Vision (VIV)

An onboard computer that runs a virtual intelligence designed to optimize armor combat performance or dispense medical care to the wearer. The most current generation — coupled with deeper cyberware integration — uses VI technology to continuously monitor the wearer's brainwaves and adapt the implant's performance to maximize potential in hazardous or combat environments.

*Note: VIV can also be mounted on vehicles or mechs. It is too heavy for power armor use.*

## Bioware

Bioware sits in the gap between medicine and augmentation — enhancements that work *with* the body rather than replacing parts of it with hardware. Where cyberware installs machinery, bioware rewrites biology: nanite colonies threaded through living tissue, vat-grown organic material grafted seamlessly onto existing musculature, engineered antibody strains circulating alongside the body's own immune system. The result is an augmented person who, in most cases, shows no outward sign of being augmented at all.

That invisibility is the primary reason bioware commands a premium over equivalent cybernetic enhancement. It carries no social signal. It does not trip metal detectors or flag on standard security scans. It cannot be jammed. It has no power cell to drain. For operators who need the edge without the tell, it is often the preferred option — and for communities with religious or cultural objections to mechanical implantation, it may be the only acceptable one.

The tradeoff is time. Bioware integrates with living tissue, which means the body does much of the work, and the body works at biological speed. Most enhancement procedures take weeks to fully establish. Rejection risk, while lower than with cybernetics, is not zero. Maintenance requirements vary by product and provider, and the more sophisticated the enhancement, the more closely it tends to be tied to whoever sold it to you.

The Sector's civilian bioware market is dominated by [[Syx Biodynamics]], whose licensed clinics provide the primary legal access point for most enhancement procedures. Military procurement runs separate channels, with specialist applications handled through star nation contracts or, at the classified end, programs that do not appear in public budgets.

---

### Grafted Muscle

Vat-grown organic muscle tissue, genetically matched to the recipient and surgically integrated into the existing musculature. When properly installed by a qualified practitioner, it is completely indistinguishable from natural tissue — no imaging system short of a full genetic workup reveals anything unusual, and even then, only to someone who knows what they're looking for. The strength gain is real and permanent, distributed across the body according to the graft map used at installation.

Grafted Muscle is a flagship product of [[Syx Biodynamics]] and a standard component of high-end biosculpting packages for clients who want functional enhancement alongside cosmetic work. It is also the most commonly counterfeited bioware in the Sector — unlicensed practitioners running gray-market vat equipment produce grafts that integrate poorly, reject unpredictably, or degrade over years rather than lasting a lifetime. The difference between licensed and unlicensed work is not always visible until something goes wrong.

> **Mechanics:** +1 STR, permanent. Licensed (Syx Biodynamics) grafts: always active. Unlicensed grafts: roll STAMINA at the start of each Shift — on a failure, the STR bonus drops to 0 for that Shift. Integration time: 2 weeks (no benefit during this window). Does not use cyberware capacity.

---

### Muscle & Bone Lace

A nanite-administered procedure that threads microscopic synthetic fibers through existing muscle and bone tissue over the course of several weeks. Produced by [[Syx Biodynamics]] and available through their licensed clinic network, it is among their most technically demanding procedures — and one of their most requested by clients who want functional enhancement that leaves no visible trace.

The fibers do not replace biological material — they reinforce it, creating a composite structure stronger and more resistant to physical trauma than unaugmented tissue. Recipients describe the process as weeks of low-grade aching followed by a gradual awareness that things they used to strain at have stopped being difficult.

The full procedure takes three to four weeks from first injection to full integration. During that window the recipient is not meaningfully enhanced — the fibers are threading, not functional — and physical stress during integration is actively discouraged. Rushing the process produces incomplete lacing and unpredictable weak points.

Muscle & Bone Lace enhances overall strength and durability — but not to the degree of an actual cyberlimb. It is enhancement, not replacement. Recipients who understand this tend to be satisfied; those who expected transformation tend not to be.

> **Mechanics:** +1 to all STAMINA rolls, permanent. When broken and rolling D66 for a critical injury, subtract 10 from the result (minimum 14). Integration time: 3–4 weeks. Does not use cyberware capacity.

### Skin Weave

A dermal nanite procedure developed and distributed by [[Syx Biodynamics]], Skin Weave threads protective fibers through the skin's sub-epidermal layers. The result is a meaningful increase in the skin's resistance to physical trauma — bladed weapons have to work harder to penetrate, and the weave reduces the severity of ballistic injury at the margins where a graze that would open an artery on unaugmented skin does not. It will not stop a rifle round. What it does is shift the threshold of what the body survives and how badly, which in the right circumstances is everything.

The procedure takes approximately two weeks to fully establish. Later-generation formulations incorporate limited self-repair capability — nanites that remain active post-integration and gradually address damage to the weave structure over time. The repair rate is slow, operating at roughly the pace of natural tissue healing, which means it offers no tactical advantage in the field. What it means is that the weave remains effective over years rather than degrading with accumulated wear.

A less-advertised effect of the weave's continuous low-level repair activity is its impact on the dermal layer itself. Skin maintained in a state of ongoing nanite-assisted upkeep ages more slowly and presents with a sustained vitality that the cosmetics industry has spent decades failing to replicate artificially. Syx markets this openly to their civilian clientele. For a significant portion of their customer base — private citizens who will never be in a knife fight — it is the primary reason they bought the procedure. For the security contractors and bodyguards who make up the bulk of the rest, the protection is the point and the youthful skin is a tolerable side effect.

> **Mechanics:** +1 AR permanently, stacks with worn armor. Completely undetectable — no social penalty. Integration time: 2 weeks. Does not use cyberware capacity.

### Zurin FleshWeave

A military-specification dermal and sub-dermal weave developed by **Zurin Applied Biosystems**, a specialist bioware research firm operating primarily under government and military contract. Where standard [[#Skin Weave]] threads protective fiber through the dermal layer alone, FleshWeave extends the nanite matrix into sub-dermal tissue — a deeper, denser, and significantly more expensive integration.

The defining characteristic of the FleshWeave is its active repair rate. The nanite population embedded during installation remains metabolically active indefinitely, continuously monitoring tissue integrity and initiating repair at speeds that dramatically exceed natural healing. After approximately ten minutes of reduced physical stress — not rest, simply the absence of ongoing trauma — the system can address damage that would otherwise require hours or days to resolve through normal biological processes.

This is not regeneration in any dramatic sense. The FleshWeave cannot replace destroyed tissue or reconnect severed structures. What it does is accelerate the repair of damage that the body could heal on its own, collapsing the timeline from days into something an operator can recover from between engagements rather than between campaigns.

Zurin Applied Biosystems does not sell to the civilian market. FleshWeave is procured through military channels or, at significant cost and through connections that are not straightforward to establish, via specialist brokers on the black market. The hardware required to install it — and the expertise to use that hardware correctly — limits access further. A botched FleshWeave installation does not simply fail; it produces chronic inflammatory responses that can be debilitating.

> **Mechanics:** +1 AR permanently, stacks with worn armor. After 1 Stretch of not being in active combat, recover 1 lost Health Point. Botched installation: persistent −1 STR until surgically corrected (MEDICAL AID −3). Does not use cyberware capacity.

### Enhanced Antibodies

Fully organic bioengineered antibodies produced by [[GaiaTech]], introduced via injection and integrated into the recipient's immune system over one to two weeks. They are not synthetic — no artificial components, no nanite involvement — which makes them among the most biologically conservative augmentations available and acceptable even in communities that reject other forms of enhancement on religious or ethical grounds.

The antibodies are engineered to be significantly more efficient than their natural counterparts at identifying and neutralizing pathogens. Recovery time from illness is reduced substantially, and resistance to infection is meaningfully higher than baseline. They do not provide protection against engineered biological agents or toxins — those require different interventions — but against the natural spectrum of disease encountered across colonial environments, they represent a significant advantage.

GaiaTech developed the formulation out of their broader pharmaceutical research program, positioning it as a practical medical enhancement for colonists, frontier personnel, and anyone whose work takes them into environments where exposure risk is high. [[BioMega]] holds competing patents and has challenged GaiaTech's formulations in ICC court on multiple occasions, arguing prior art on the underlying antibody engineering methodology. GaiaTech contests both the patents and the timeline. The litigation is ongoing, and given the revenue at stake, it is expected to remain so.

> **Mechanics:** +2 dice on STAMINA rolls to resist disease and natural infection. Recovery time from illness halved (GM discretion). Does not protect against engineered biological agents, chemical toxins, or xenobiological threats. Does not use cyberware capacity.

### Toxin Binders

A nanite colony engineered to identify and neutralize toxic compounds in the bloodstream. [[BioMega]] produces the most widely distributed version — developed, though the company does not advertise this, in parallel with their chemical and biological weapons research programs, an origin that regulators have noted with interest and been unable to act on with consequence. The practical application is civilian and military both: introduced via injection and self-sustaining once established, the system continuously monitors blood chemistry and binds to toxin particles before they can reach effective concentration in critical tissue.

The process does not eliminate toxins — it sequesters them for gradual metabolic processing — but the practical effect is that poisons and chemical agents that would incapacitate or kill an unaugmented person produce significantly reduced effects in a recipient with active Toxin Binders. The system does not provide immunity. Sufficiently high concentrations, or compounds specifically engineered to defeat binder chemistry, will still cause harm. What Toxin Binders do is shift the threshold — a dose that would be lethal becomes manageable, a dose that would merely incapacitate may pass with only discomfort.

[[GaiaTech]] offers a competing formulation marketed primarily to colonial workers in industrial and chemical environments, developed through their occupational medicine research program and positioned as a safety product rather than an enhancement. BioMega has challenged the formulation in ICC court, citing patent interference on binder sequestration methodology. GaiaTech's legal team characterizes the suit as anticompetitive harassment. Both products are available through legitimate medical channels. Both are also available through other channels, to people in lines of work where asking a licensed clinic to document the procedure is inadvisable.

> **Mechanics:** +2 dice on STAMINA rolls to resist poisons, toxins, and chemical agents. For toxins without a Sickness Roll (instant damage, no roll), halve damage taken (round down). Engineered compounds specifically designed to defeat binder chemistry bypass this effect entirely. Does not protect against disease. Does not use cyberware capacity.

### Nanosurgeons

A specialized nanite colony designed to repair physical damage at the cellular level — microscopic tears, internal hemorrhage, bone stress fractures, and the kind of subcellular trauma that accumulates from repeated physical punishment without ever producing a single obvious injury. Unlike [[#Enhanced Antibodies]], which address disease, or [[#Toxin Binders]], which address chemical threat, Nanosurgeons work on structural damage. They are the body's maintenance crew.

In medical practice, Nanosurgeons are a standard component of the installation process for cyberware — the nanite colony prepares tissue, manages post-surgical inflammation, and accelerates integration in ways that have made procedures that once required weeks of recovery achievable in days. Outside surgical contexts, they are used by high-performance operators whose bodies accumulate damage faster than natural healing can address it.

[[GaiaTech]] developed the foundational Nanosurgeon protocol during their colonial medicine research program and continues to produce the most widely used civilian variant. Military-specification versions with faster response profiles exist under classified procurement contracts; their manufacturers are not publicly attributed.

> **Mechanics:** Once per Shift of reduced activity (any Shift not involving active combat or heavy physical exertion), recover 1 lost Health Point. Surgical recovery time for any implant procedure is halved. Does not address destroyed tissue, severed structures, or critical injuries requiring surgery. Does not use cyberware capacity.

### Biosculpting

Today's biosculpting technology makes altering the body's fundamental architecture a matter of resources, not possibility. Bone structure can be shifted, musculature redistributed, organs relocated, and skin of any type — including variants derived from animal biology — grafted onto a human frame. The technology has advanced to the point where the limiting factor is no longer what can be done but what a given practitioner has the skill to do safely.

The cost gradient is steep. At the high end, a skilled surgical team working with premium [[Syx Biodynamics]] materials can produce results indistinguishable from natural biology — or deliberately, magnificently unnatural, depending on the client's intent. At the low end, unlicensed practitioners in back-corridor clinics produce work that functions, mostly, and looks like it cost what it cost.

People who have undergone extensive biosculpting — animal features, dramatic structural alterations, biological forms that read as non-human at a glance — are called **Exotics**. The term is neutral in most contexts, derogatory in some, and worn as a badge in others. Gang culture across the Sector has developed strong biosculpting traditions, using shared aesthetic modifications as identity markers that are considerably harder to fake or remove than tattoos or clothing.
