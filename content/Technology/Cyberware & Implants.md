# Cyberware & Implants

*"More implants means more vulnerability to slicing."* — Standard Field Warning

---

## Overview

Cyberware (colloquially **"chrome"**) refers to cybernetic implants that enhance the human body with mechanical or electronic systems. All cyberware integrates with — and is managed through — the [[NeuroSyn & Cybernetics|NeuroSyn]] implant. For standard NeuroSyn package components (Holophone, HUD, Neural Link, etc.), see the [[NeuroSyn & Cybernetics]] page.

> **The tradeoff:** Every additional implant increases vulnerability to slicing and raises the risk of **Cyberpsychosis** — a dissociative condition where heavy augmentation erodes a person's sense of humanity.

## Armor Modules / HUD Enhancements

![[cyberware_viv_demo.gif]]

### Electronically Enhanced Vision (EEV)
Utilized via the HUD, this "dumb mode" software allows the user to scan electronic-enabled objects for intelligence gathering — especially as a means of remotely slicing electronic devices from a distance. When the HUD is focused on an object long enough, the EEV finishes scanning and displays one of five icons:
- Slice Possible (calculated)
- Explosive and/or Toxic Danger
- Laser Designation (calling in airstrikes)
- Remote Accessible (object can be used from a distance)

### Virtual Intelligence Vision (VIV)
An onboard computer that can optimize armor's combat performance or dispense medical care to the user. The newest VIV — coupled with cyberware at level 4 and higher — uses VI technology to constantly monitor the user's brainwaves and adapt the implant's performance to maximize potential in hazardous or combat environments.

*Note: VIV can also be mounted on vehicles or mechs. It is too heavy for power armor use.*

## Stealth & Infiltration Implants

![[cyberware_implants_optical_camo.gif]]
### Optic Camouflage
An active stealth system that projects ambient conditions of the opposing side onto the user's surface — rendering the masked object **transparent by transmission**.

**Limitations:**
- Does not mask heat signature
- Cannot compensate for sudden changes, physical impacts, or close observation
- A faint translucent distortion is visible at the limits of the technology
- Usage without a warrant is heavily restricted; this is the exception, not the norm

**Detection Methods used by upper-tier facilities:**
- Seismic/weight sensors hidden in flooring
- Air pressure, seismic, or sensitive microphone sensors detecting sound of movement

### Thermo-Optic Camouflage
Cutting-edge — found only on the most advanced corporate strike teams and elite government units. Identical uses to optic camouflage, but **does not give up a heat signature**.

**Limitation:** The heat has to go somewhere. The system holds heat until it can be bled off later — making this **uncomfortably humid and hot to wear**. The window of reliable IR masking is limited before it becomes too uncomfortable to keep active.

### Behavioral Imprint-Synced Faceplate
Advanced intelligence agency spytech based on abandoned megacorp research. Pairing it with a behavioral imprint allows the user to **take on the appearance of another person** as well as replicate their facial expressions and speech patterns. An experienced agent equipped with a faceplate is virtually indistinguishable from their target — capable of clearing even sophisticated security scans.

## Combat Operating Systems

![[cyberware_serrick_os.png]]

### Serrick Operating System
A standard go-to for soldiers and mercenaries. Overclock the nervous system to the point where the brain receives stimulus information so rapidly it makes everything else appear to move in **slow motion** — while accelerating neural impulses to muscle to move the user faster. Combined with physical cyberware, could theoretically move at **inhuman speeds**.

**Drawback:** The strain that accelerating nerve impulses puts on the biological nervous system is severe. Only using it in **short bursts** mitigates the damage. Most people are incapable of handling a Serrick at all — or risk **Cyberpsychosis**.

### Relic Biochip

![[cyberware_relic.png]]

An interface and component of [[Arienai Corporation|Arienai]]'s experimental flagship project, publicly known as **"Secure Your Soul."** Uses personality transferal — converting the data in the human nervous system into a digitized engram stored as a construct on the Relic biochip.

The stated purpose: allowing wealthy clients to **communicate with deceased loved ones** whose personality constructs are preserved on the chip. In practice, death is no longer the ultimate barrier — for those who can afford it.

> Arienai is currently not sharing any information on further plans for the Relic program. It does not take a genius to realize the potential goes significantly beyond grief counseling.

## Fashionware

![[fashionware_chemskin.png]]
### ChemSkin
Dyes and pigments infused into the skin to change its hue. Applications range from hiding blemishes to neon-green skin. Pigments can optionally be temperature-sensitive or reactive to hormone changes. Any human shade or exotic pattern (leopard, crocodile, bioluminescent, etc.). Can be temporary, long-term, or permanent.

![[fashionware_light_tattoo.png]]
### Light Tattoo
Subdermal patches that store light and project colored tattoos under the skin. The larger the piece, the more installations required.

### SynthSkin
Upgrade from ChemSkin — embedded into real skin with a display layer that emits light and patterns, capable of a range of special effects.

### RealSkinn *(GaiaTech Brand)*
Cosmetic covering for cyberware — synthetic skin almost impossible to distinguish from real skin. Originally developed for war veterans to more easily accept prostheses. Now standard covering for most implants. Variants include an all-organic version infused with green fluorescent protein.

### Ghost™ Transparent Skin *(GenomeTek)*
Replaces flesh with a transparent substance that shows what's inside.

### Camo-Skin & MoodSkin *(Dermtech)*

**Camo-Skin:** Strategically placed implants linked via microprocessor controlling a reservoir of tailored nanoids. Upon command, nanites modify skin pigmentation. Requires one hour to become established and "set." Capable of up to eight basic camouflage patterns (jet black, green, brown, etc.).

**MoodSkin:** Pigmentation reactive to mood or hormone state.

### HoloSkin *(HoloTek)*
Turns the body into a holoscreen using a patented **HoloGrid™** system. Tune the chip to make the screen any size and anywhere on the body.

### TechHair
Artificial hair with properties similar to ChemSkin — any color, or a color-light-emitting artificial variant. Can be temperature-sensitive, motorized to extend/retract, or reactive to hormone changes. Replaces real hair or adds to it like a weave. Can be applied to any part of the body.

![[fashionware_threading.png]]
### Threading
Thin silver lines run in circuit-like patterns across the body. Popularized by an Antevic media sensation decades ago. Many believe they act as a "Faraday cage" against radiation and EMP effects — **no scientific backing** for these claims. Primarily worn as a fashion statement.

## Bioware

Bioware is a mix of wetware and cyberware — enhancements that rely on nanotechnology based on human anatomy and physiology rather than electronics or mechanics. Bioware can have some non-organic components, but the enhancement improves a *biological* function rather than replacing it with technology.

**Why bioware is often preferred over cybernetics:**
- Little impact on appearance (stealth consideration — you may not want people knowing you're enhanced)
- Fewer possible complications since it enhances existing natural functions
- Some people reject cybernetics for religious reasons or tissue rejection

*Note: Life-extending biowares are often expensive to maintain — beyond the means of most average Antevic citizens.*

### Examples

**Grafted Muscle:** Vat-grown organic muscle functioning identically to natural muscle. Enhances both appearance and strength. When properly grafted, completely indistinguishable from natural muscle. Common component of biosculpting.

**Muscle & Bone Lace:** Nanites thread microscopic artificial fibers through muscle and bone tissue. Stronger and more resistant to damage. Full process takes a few weeks. Enhances overall strength and durability — but not to the degree of an actual cyberlimb.

**Skin Weave:** Nanites thread fibers through skin tissue. Result: bare skin with resistance to damage similar to light body armor. Transparent to the naked eye but detectable with careful observation. Takes a couple of weeks. Later versions capable of self-repair given enough time.

**Zurin FleshWeave:** Military-grade research weave. After 10 minutes of non-strenuous activity, nanomachines repair the body and head.

**Enhanced Antibodies:** Bioengineered (fully organic) antibodies more efficient than natural ones. Cuts recovery time from disease; boosts disease resistance.

**Toxin Binders:** Nanites that bind with poison particles in the body. Does not grant immunity but provides high resistance to toxins and poisons.

**Nanosurgeons:** Nanites designed to repair damage at the cellular level. Common component for installing cyberware. Unlike Enhanced Antibodies, nanosurgeons repair physical damage — not disease or poison.

### Biosculpting
The galaxy's version of plastic surgery. Today's technology makes altering appearance near-trivial: bones, muscle, and organs can be shifted; skin of any type can be grafted (including animal skin, fur, feathers). Higher quality is more expensive, but biosculpting is cheap enough that the average person can afford some form of it. People extensively biosculpted are called **Exotics**. Many gangs make extensive use of biosculpting.
