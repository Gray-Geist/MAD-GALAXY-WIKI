# Weapons & Equipment

![[equipment_header.jpg]]

*Six centuries of warfare have produced a weapons ecosystem ranging from caseless chemical arms to ALIM railguns, plasma slaggers, and smart-guided rounds. Below is the current state of the art.*

---

## Ammunition

![[ammunition_intro_icon.png|481]]
![[ammunition_25mm Yoke.png]]

After the Savage Wars' refinements, the majority of chemical arms across the Sector adopted **caseless ammunition** — the body of the round itself is the propellant, eliminating the need to eject cases.

**Advantages of caseless:**
- Lighter ammo; less space; lighter weapon or more rounds carried
- Higher rates of fire — no case ejection cycle needed
- Ignition via **electrothermal-chemical (ETC) propellant** — high ignition point prevents accidental cook-off
- Gun barrels constructed with advanced polymer alloy for heat resistance

### Ammunition Types

| Round | Description |
|---|---|
| **.480mm Pedal Slug** | Low-velocity expanding slug for ship boarding — minimizes hull breach risk, maximizes stopping power against unarmored targets. No effect on most armored opponents. |
| **25mm Yoke** | Custom chemical arm projectiles weighing over half a pound each. Fired from sniper rifles so large they require power armor or a bipod that locks to the ground. |
| **40mm MK75 MOD 2 HEDP** | High explosive, dual-purpose standard explosive munition. |
| **40mm XM18P EMP Warhead** | Generates an electromagnetic pulse at impact. Capable of circumventing EMP shielding and temporarily disabling vehicles. |
| **D-U (Depleted Uranium)** | Very powerful armor-piercing round for chemical arms. |
| **Lava Rounds** | Non-standard slag round designed to ablate on contact and overwhelm defensive positions. |
| **Volcano Rounds** | Non-standard slag round designed to pierce the heavy armor of light vehicles and ACPA. |
| **SMAFF** | Refractive smoke rounds. Anti-ship detection system — foils enemy EM and IR sensors. Visibility within a SMAFF cloud: ~3–5m. |
| **Stingers** | Non-lethal incapacitating rounds. |

## Armor

![[equipment_acpa.png]]

### ACPA — Assisted Combat Personnel Armor
Also known as **Power Armor (PA)**. A combat environment suit reinforced with composite armor that assists the wearer in carrying its own weight plus additional equipment and weaponry. Its major advantage is a highly powerful advanced linear frame called a **"chassis."** For HUD modules and VIV systems, see [[Cyberware & Implants]].

## Weapon Types

### Asynchronous Linear Induction Motor (ALIM) Arms

![[equipment_weapon_types_alim_intro.gif]]

*Colloquially: "Linear Motor Arms." Technology unlocked in 1445 AE when the massive recoil problem of such weapons was finally overcome.*

- Accelerates projectiles to extreme velocities — rapid penetration and long-range effectiveness
- Disadvantage: the weapon must be considerably larger than chem arm counterparts
- **Anvil Round:** Creates an EMP pulse at point of impact
- **"Splashers" (APLP/F):** Armor-piercing, limited penetration/frangible — designed to disintegrate on impact, dumping all energy into the target; catastrophic against lightly armored or unarmored targets
- **"Rippers" (HV/FTHPP):** High-velocity ferric-tungsten hollow-point — punches through armor, then expands violently while keeping much of its mass intact, maximizing energy transfer to soft targets within armor
- Can be flash-suppressed, but the projectile exceeding the sound barrier still creates a sonic boom
- Precision targeting advantage over slaggers
- Relatively low energy consumption compared to same-size slaggers

### Chemical Arms (Chem Arms)
*Also: chemers, firearms, slug throwers. Originated from Old Earth; Sector-wide use. Majority use caseless ammunition.*

- Uses explosive chemicals to launch a solid projectile ("slug")
- Normal rounds not effective against modern armor
- Can be ALIM-assisted — ALIM-assisted rounds are typically Teflon-coated tungsten: a narrow tungsten arrow surrounded by a light Teflon-edged sabot for maximum barrel exit velocity
- Can use suppressors and non-tracer rounds (for surprise and confusion)

### Slaggers

![[equipment_slagger_intro_gif1.gif]]

*Military-authorized use only. Invented during the Savage Wars specifically to counter Savage armor.*

- Superheats metal, then fires it as a molten mass of plasma ("slag")
- Less effective at long range compared to ALIM arms
- Much higher stopping power than chemical arm counterparts
- Highly effective against all armor and — depending on ammo — fortifications
- **Cannot be flash-suppressed or silenced**
- Special "lava rounds" can splash and disrupt fortified positions

### Smart Weapons

![[equipment_smart_weaponm_intro_gif1.gif]]

*Only chemical arms can be Smart.*

- Use minijet technology to fire caseless guided ammo
- Efficient and pin-point accurate
- Tied to weapon grips and optics to scan and track target movement
- Aim for the wielder using smart rounds that lock on and seek their targets — must be within the weapon's tracking box
- Can curve around obstacles to hit their target, but travel slower than conventional bullets
- **[[NeuroSyn & Cybernetics|SmartLync]] cyberware is required** for smart-targeting; better quality SmartLyncs improve tracking ability
- Enemies can jam smart-targeting ability
- Smart weapons **cannot use suppressors or other scopes**

## Weapon List

### ALIM Arms

**T19 Needler:**
![[equipment_t19_needler_alim.png]]
One of the most distinctive weapons of modern times. Unusual angular frame with disc magazine and unique tech that makes it an ALIM repeating revolver. The barrel holds four electrically charged shafts which, combined with expensive birdshot-sized bullets, form opposing electromagnetic poles — releasing the bullet at lightning speeds. Can pierce armored vehicles and bulletproof vests but is only accurate in a tight burst within 20m. Perfect for assassinations.

**XEL-9 Electromagnetic Launcher:**
![[equipment_xel9.png]]
Usually requires ACPA or mounting to fire. Uses an ALIM to produce a bipolar magnetic field capable of launching a 25×130mm projectile at incredible speeds. The great velocity is the key to its stopping power and exceptional armor penetration at impressive ranges.

**XEL-10 Electromagnetic Launcher:**
![[equipment_xel10.png]]
Same profile as the XEL-9 — fewer exposed parts, reduced profile, larger ammo capacity, and implementation of new ALIM and rail channels. Reduced development costs.

### Chemical Arms

**A6 Biretta (PDW):**
![[equipment_a6_biretta.jpg]]
Recoil-operated, magazine-fed. Fires 4.3mm large-caliber caseless rounds. Remarkably large and overpowered for its class — a cross between a pistol and SMG in size. Exceedingly rugged and reliable. *A6H2 variant:* Modernized; black polymer finish; semi-automatic; increased 18-round magazine; increased accuracy; Projection sight for smart link; protective metamaterial coating.

**AC-4 "Grit":** Highly reliable sidearm — *Avalon-Arienai Combine.*

**AS-4 "Boa'":** Highly reliable auto-suppressed sidearm — *Avalon-Arienai Combine.*

**BR40 Service Rifle:**
![[equipment_br40_alim.png]]
ALIM-assisted chemical arm. Notable sound suppression system, powerful three-round burst fire, and short-range scope accurate to 400m. More efficient at range than assault rifles; less effective than a sniper.

**Mark 950 Repeater (12.7mm Anti-Mech Infantry Rifle):**

![[equipment_mark950_chem.png]]

Highly reliable, cheap to produce, large-bore chemical arm. Primarily used for anti-vehicle, anti-mech, or anti-fortification.

**MTEC-43 Marksman Rifle:**
![[equipment_mtec43_chem.png]]
Armat-manufactured semi-automatic; medium to long range. Proprietary anti-recoil system counterbalances shoulder impact. Standard 7.62×35mm Integrated rounds; effective range 800m. Compatible with suppression mode, aim stabilizer, extendable bipod, and adjustable sights — all HUD-integrated via smart link.

**N-4/G Rifle:**

Primary chem arm of the [[RTAC (Paladins)|RTAC Paladins]]. Magazines deplete from green to black. "G" variant has an auto-feed frag launcher — *Avalon-Arienai Combine.*

**N-18:** Long-barreled sniper rifle. Highly reliable and even more accurate — *Avalon-Arienai Combine.*

**N-50A1 ("Fifty"):**

![[equipment_n50a1.png]]

Heavy crew-served 12.7mm chem arm. Staple across the Sector since the early Savage Wars. Fills the anti-vehicle or heavy support role. Requires power armor, tripod, or pintle-mount.

**N-50A2 ACSW ("Hacksaw"):**

![[equipment_n50a2.png]]

Advanced version of the N-50. Pulse action system with free-floating recoil-dampened rotating breech. Fires 12.7mm HEAP caseless rounds at an effective range of 1,500m. Under-barrel 30mm automatic grenade launcher. 500-round main gun box; 50-round grenade launcher. Battery powered (up to 50,000 rounds). 256×256 multispectral scope. **Doubles as an unmanned sentry gun** wirelessly controllable by terminal or HoloSync. Features a **"Dead Man's Switch"** — if the gunner releases both grips for longer than 60 seconds, the gun switches to auto-tracking and firing mode.

**N-92 ("The Ripper"):** Modern ALIM-assisted squad automatic chem arm. Fires 230-grain 10×28mm HEAP caseless rounds at a near-impossible-to-avoid rate of fire. Designed to be used in pairs for interlocking crossfire in narrow spaces.
![[equipment_n92_lmg.png]]
**NB-14:** Battle rifle with greater range and power than the N-4, but less wieldy due to size — *Avalon-Arienai Combine.*

**NPK-4 "Viper" SMG:** Collapsible mini-SMG. Faster target acquisition in close quarters. Commonly used by assassins, undercover operatives, and VIP security — *Avalon-Arienai Combine.*

**NS-4 SMG:** Commonly used in kill teams — *Avalon-Arienai Combine.*

**R6A:**
![[equipment_r6a.png]]
Rugged and reliable standard chem rifle with HUD-integrated sights. Effective short to mid range.

**RA12-S ALIM Assisted Marksman Rifle:**
![[equipment_ra12s.png]]
A fusion of Chemical Arms and ALIM innovation by ROFTech (1512 AE). 14-inch bullpup frame in a frost-white hexagonal lattice finish; 9.5 lbs loaded. 60-round high-capacity drum magazine. 1,500m effective range. Suppressible — though the ALIM sonic boom still betrays it. Electro-Optical Stack Interfacing Optics with wide/medium/narrow fields of view. Koenig Short-Range Thermal Imager. Laser Guidance.

**Rh-6 Pulse Rifle:**
![[equipment_rh6.png]]
Coalition Marine standard rifle. Fires 10×24mm caseless rounds — a 210-grain explosive-tipped round embedded in a rectangular propellant block.

**ST-14:**
![[equipment_st14.png]]
Old but still widely seen chemical rifle. First used by RTAC Paladins during the early Savage Wars.

### Smart Weapons (List)

![[equipment_nano_laptop_weapons.png]]

**Nanomolecular Laptop Weapons:** Very new and expensive. Made of nanomolecules often hidden as parts of personal objects. Put through a hardening process called **"baking"** and used in covert operations — almost all scans miss these.

**SASG-99 ("the Sasquatch"):**
![[equipment_sasg99.png]]
Squad Automatic Smart Gun. Fires ALIM-assisted 230-grain 10×28mm SHEAP caseless rounds. Features an **Aiming Assist Computer (AAC)** that monitors a 30° cone in front of the gun — transmitting thermal images to the user. If a target is detected, the tracker overlays a lighted box over their center mass; the Smart Stock aligns the gun and acquires a target lock; fired rounds steer toward the target.

**STG-2 Linear Motor Smart Shotgun:**
![[equipment_stg2_shotgun_alim_smart.png]]
![[equipment_sub_s_shotgun_emp.png]]
Smart chemical arm weapon for CQB — "because you barely have to aim the thing and anything it hits will be very dead." Not effective against bot opponents.

**Sub-S EMP Shotgun:** Close-range electromagnetic pulse weapon. Designed to disable warbots and powered fortifications.

### Launchers & Miscellaneous

**Aero-Precision "AP" Missile Launcher:**
![[equipment_ap_missile_launcher.jpg]]
Man-portable missile launcher capable of damaging or piercing light to medium tanks and mechs. Telescopes to a smaller size when on the move. Capable of auto-targeting, smart-link, and manual targeting. Shoulder-fired. Attacks both ground and air vehicles.

**Auto Defense Turrets:**
![[equipment_auto_defense_turrets.gif]]
Automated weapon system responding to motion/threat detectors. Can be set to automatically engage any moving target within a defined range.

**G45A2 Multi-Shot Grenade Launcher (MGSL):**
![[equipment_g45a2_g45b1_msgl.png]]
Light squad support weapon (~15 lbs loaded). Fires many grenade types. Smart-linkable with HUD. Two firing modes: single trigger pull = standard impact-detonation with a bounce; hold after firing = alternate fuse that detonates only upon trigger release — useful for indirect fire, setting traps, or as a dead-man's trigger.

**HED-27 Stun Gun:**
![[equipment_hed27_stun_gun.png]]
Non-lethal; found throughout the Sector. Effects: Muscle Lock (involuntary contractions; temporary paralysis), Neuromuscular Scramble (overloads nerve signals; loss of coordination and disorientation), Neuro-Spike (disrupts inner ear balance and pain receptors; dizziness, headache, vomiting).

**Light Anti-Armor Weapon (LAW):**
![[equipment_law_missile_launcher.png]]
Fires a small 70mm armor-piercing explosive rocket from disposable magazine tubes. Used for demolishing equipment, clearing light armor, and clearing obstacles. Faster reload than the AP Missile Launcher; lighter — but lacks the AP's power.

**Nano Melee Weapons:**
![[equipment_nano_melee_weapons.png]]
Built with nanomolecular edges deadly against flesh and standard light armor. Self-repair and self-sharpen unless critically damaged.

**Plasma Thrower:**
![[equipment_pt54_plasma_thrower.png]]
Incendiary weapon that shoots a stream of ionized gas at the target. Requires oxygen to work — cannot be used in a vacuum.

**Weapons Baton/Whip:**
![[equipment_weapon_baton_types.png]]
Very common weapon type found throughout the Sector. Effects: Muscle Lock, Shockwave Disruptor (concussive pulse; knocks the wind out), Neuromuscular Scramble, Neuro-Spike, Agonizer (overstimulates pain receptors).

## Personnel Equipment

**Aegis Sealant:** State-of-the-art shape-memory nano-polymers conforming to the irregular contours of damaged composite armor. Seals out environmental hazards and restores some ballistic protection.

**Enerchains:**
![[equipment_enerchains.png]]
Begin as wrapped-up coils; snake out and wrap around a target to immobilize them. Can also be set to handcuff mode if the target needs to remain mobile.

**Enerchain Anchors:**
![[equipment_enerchains_anchor.png]]
Ground-based circular contraption (~10 inches diameter) that adheres to various surfaces and creates an anchor point for enerchains. Selectable distance settings from 0–5 meters.

**FieldSeal Patch:** Thermally activated patch that softens when heated by a portable applicator and rapidly cools to form a durable, flexible seal. Seals compromised armor against corrosive environments and radiation. Does not restore ballistic protection but prevents further degradation.

**ME2 Hand Welder ("Cutting Torch"):**
![[equipment_me2_hand_welder.png]]
![[equipment_me2_hand_welder2.jpg]]
Compact handheld welding torch — cutting and welding in the field. Used for sealing electronic doors, constructing barricades, and freeing persons from enerchains.

**Plasma Cutter:**
![[equipment_plasma_cutter.png]]
Heavy, two-handed tool firing multiple ionized plasma bolts that combine into a cutting stream capable of slicing through most surfaces. Highly unwieldy but very effective at breaching fortified positions.

## Personnel Ordnance

**APER M101 Claymore Mine:**
![[equipment_aper_m101_claymore.png]]
Small infantry-deployed anti-personnel mine. Three explosive charges in a fan shape; numerous ball bearings that deform into shards on explosion. Effective range 75m; dangerous to 250m. Uses motion detector, forward-facing trip lasers, and IFF detector.

**ATMN-2 Magnetic Anti-Tank Mine ("Tank Toaster"):**
![[equipment_atmn2_anti_tank.png]]
15 kg. Magnetic — latches onto vehicle armor.

**C5 Blast Brick:**
![[equipment_c5_brick.png]]
High explosive that sticks to surfaces. Detonated remotely.

**CF-24 Cluster Frag Grenade ("Fragger"):**
![[equipment_cf24_cluster.gif]]
Dual fragmentation grenade. First detonation = standard frag from the main body. Second = 24 clusters creating intense shrapnel that shreds all organic material in the area.

**Det-Cord:** Explosive pressed into long cord coils on reels. Useful for punching holes in non-fortified walls or doors. Only responds to a command keyed specifically to the cord.

**Flash-Bang Bolo:** A pair of small banger grenades connected by a heavy wire. Used by hunters to immobilize and stun an opponent.

**Fuel Cell Bomb:**
![[equipment_fuel_cell_bomb_nuclear.png]]
Engineered from scavenged industrial and military-grade fuel cells. On detonation: unleashes a miniature starburst of superheated plasma that vaporizes nearby infrastructure.

**G-22 Grav Grenade:**
![[equipment_g22_grav.png]]
Non-lethal. Creates a gravitational pull via reverse repulsor technology that drags targets inward toward it.

**H-8 Holographic Grenade:**
![[equipment_h8_holographic.png]]
Deployed "skins" project holographic emitters creating multiple false combatants or small vehicle outlines in the immediate area.

**H-35 & H-37 Lasmore Tactical Mines ("Lasmore mines"):**
![[equipment_h35_h37_lasmore_tacmines.png]]
Proximity mines that shoot into the air and explode when movement interrupts their active laser sensor beams. Magnetic — capable of sticking to metal surfaces.

**K4 Breaching Charge:**
![[equipment_k4_breach.png]]
Highly effective explosive charge for breaching fortified areas.

**M-3B SMAFF Grenade:**
![[equipment_m3b_smaff.png]]
Refractive smoke grenades. Foils enemy EM and IR sensors; laser and sensor retardant smoke. Visibility: ~5m. Lasts 50–90 seconds.

**M-9A Seeker Grenade:**
![[equipment_m9a_seeker.png]]
Small deployable robot that seeks out enemies and self-destructs.

**R-19 Repulsor Grenade:**
![[equipment_r19_repulsor.png]]
Non-lethal. Pushes targets away from the center of radius. Useful for hostage situations.

**SG-48 Stick Grenade:**
![[equipment_sg48.png]]
Older version of the SG-84. High explosive and incendiary versions. Highly prevalent across the Sector for over sixty years.

**SG-84 Stick Grenade:**
![[equipment_sg84.png]]
Newer design based on the SG-48. Audible, HUD visual, and detonator control options. Comes in **Nanoshrapnel** (micro-engineered self-propagating shrapnel that penetrates body armor) and **High Explosive** versions.

**SMT-M22 Incendiary Thermite Grenade ("Burner" / "Thermite"):**
![[equipment_smt_m22_thermite.png]]
Burns very hot — some varieties hot enough to turn sand into slag. Used by kill teams to erase evidence, destroy weapons caches, or demolish heavy structures. Will reduce a human body to carbon ash.

**TS-34 EM-FRAG EMP Grenade ("Bot Popper"):**
![[equipment_ts34_em_frag.png]]
Ion grenade generating a micro-EMP designed to fry a bot's operating system. Also affects battery-operated weapons.

**XTP-3 Flashbang ("Ear-Popper" / "Banger"):**
![[equipment_xtp3.png]]
Detonates with a blinding flash and a truly deafening boom. Hearing loss is often permanent; the flash can render those at ground zero blind or dazzled for up to five standard minutes.

## Nation-Level / Heavy Ordnance

![[nation_level_equipment_cnto.png]]

**CNTO (Chained Nuclear Trigger Ordinance) — "Trigger Nuke" / "Sin-toe":** The first classified **Total Biome Kill (TBK)** weapon. Once complete, it ignites the nearest hydrogen atoms and chains to the next — completely burning off the atmosphere and glassing everything capable of ever supporting life. **No terraforming is possible afterward.** *See: [[The Savage Wars|Requite Dilemma]]*

**MARO (Massive Antimatter Reaction Ordinance):** Can be used in torpedoes, missiles, or mines. Outlawed since the Savage Wars.

**Romula Nuclear Space Mine:** Savage Wars-era fusion bomb deployed in space; detonates on collision with a capital-class starship.

**STG Deep Cluster Bomb:** Standard weapons for Tri-bombers. Self-guided as they track their targets.
