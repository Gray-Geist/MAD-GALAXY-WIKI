## Overview

**Ikhemu-sek** is the home system of the Ul'Rekar Hierocracy and the official governing seat — but its location is **largely unknown** except to a small privileged few. The only people typically permitted to visit are strict Ul'Rekar adherents undertaking their *abark* (holy pilgrimage), or select individuals with an aptitude for poly-math being trained to become Navigators.

The only foreigners ever allowed in Ikhemu-sek are the latter group. Most Ul'Rek citizens — **even the powerful elite** — will never step foot in this system. All travel is directly authorized by the **Netjertum**.

**Central Star:** ERROR\_UNKNOWN  
**Ethnicity & Culture:** Ul'Rek (presumably)

## The Mystery of Ul'Haupt

Those who return from Ikhemu-sek each have **differing experiences that rarely align** — except for one consistent thread: they return filled with a profound **sense of duty and purpose** toward completion of a holy mission. Even under duress, they can only clearly recall that purpose.

Descriptions of the capital planet **Ul'Haupt** range wildly:
- A vast verdant landscape
- A technological hypercity
- A cold place that demands much to survive

Most remarkably — **Navigators who visit cannot recall the coordinates even under Complidon** — the most potent interrogation cocktail in the Sector.

> *Some skeptics theorize that Ikhemu-sek does not exist in physical reality at all — that it is merely a simulated experience, unique to each individual permitted to access it. Regardless, it remains one of the biggest and best-kept secrets in the Sector.*

---

## Government

Ikhemu-sek is governed by the elusive **Netjertum** — the supreme leader of the Ul'Rekar Hierocracy. His name and identity are **completely unknown**. He is referred to only by his title. He allegedly actively governs the entire Ul'Rek government and takes direct reports from **The Proxy** on [[Ahura System#Manichae|Manichae]].

## Ul'Haupt

**Population:** ERROR\_UNKNOWN  
**Capital:** Hermopol  
**Head of State:** The Netjertum *(real name unknown)*  
**Industry:** The **only known place** where poly-psykers can be trained to become [[Polymath & Poly-psykers|Navigators]]

The only known direct link to Ul'Haupt is **The Gate** — a unique space structure on [[Ahura System]], closely monitored and regulated by the Ul'Rek government. It allows Cosmic Strand travel to Ul'Haupt without revealing its coordinates.

There is very little known information on Ul'Haupt beyond its singular function. Alien wildlife, plants, and weather: **Unknown.**
