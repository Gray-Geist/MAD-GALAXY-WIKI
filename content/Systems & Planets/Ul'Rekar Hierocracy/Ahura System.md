# Ahura System

**Section:** Core  
**Location:** Drowned Nebula  
**Nation:** Ul'Rekar Hierocracy  
**Status:** The Ul'Rekar "Spotlight System" — the public face of the Hierocracy

---

## Overview

Referred to as the **"Spotlight System"** — what the Ul'Rekar Hierocracy allows and wants the rest of humanity to see. The true holiest of sites, a planet called **Ul'Haupt** in the hidden [[Ikhemu-sek System]], is known only to a select few. All travel to Ul'Haupt must go through **The Gate** — a unique space structure outside Ahura's capital that allows Cosmic Strand travel to Ul'Haupt without revealing its coordinates.

Only those deemed worthy of Navigator training and Ul'Rek adherents who have sufficiently advanced in their spiritual journey are permitted to travel there. Fewer than **1% of the Ul'Rek polity** achieve this level, earning their *abark* (holy pilgrimage).

**Unique Geography:** All of Ahura's settlements are found on **moons orbiting the gas giant Zurva** *(Zoroastrianism: the primordial being that brought Ahura Mazda and Angra Mainyu into existence; god of time and space)*. Zurva's **four moons** are roughly the same size and each orbits at the same distance from one another. Some say this arrangement was **purposefully placed** — such is the strange perfection of their geometry.

**Central Star:** Samas *(ancient Semitic word for sun/sun-disc; Mesopotamian sun god)* — a blue M-Type main sequence star, midway through its lifecycle  
**Ethnicity & Culture:** Ul'Rek

## Government & Society

Ahura is an **economic powerhouse** and source of envy from the Republic. Unlike the other major star nations, the Ul'Rekar Hierocracy is a **strict and rigid monoculture** — rich, prosperous, and insular, refusing immigration from outside groups.

Ul'Rek settlements have sections permitting intra-Sector communities and commerce, but these are **closely monitored** and non-citizens are not afforded citizen status. This does not mean foreigners are treated poorly — Ul'Rek culture places enormous emphasis on **respect, order, and politeness**. Many foreign businesspeople prefer forgoing their rights to reside in Ul'Rek territories specifically because of these traits.

**The Proxy Netjertum** ("The Proxy") governs all known Ul'Rek space and reports directly to the Netjertum in Ul'Haupt. All diplomacy with foreign governments takes place in Ahura. The Ul'Rekar are **historically peaceful and neutral** but boast a supremely powerful military — the **Rhodian Legion** (named for their distinctive metallic white armor) — and the **White Fleet**, which calls Ahura home. Smuggling in Ul'Rek space is **extremely dangerous**.

## Mazandaran

![[ahura_mazandaran_main.png]]
![[ahura_mazandaran_rostam.png]]

**Population:** 2 billion (stable/slowly growing)  
**Capital:** Rostam City  
**Setbek:** Sirvan Yektar  
**Industry:** Export of raw minerals, metals, and elements

A large resource-rich rock moon serving as the **primary source of metals** used to build Ul'Rek armors and ships. Water is manufactured from the oxygenated atmosphere and hydrogen imported from Zurva.

**[[AhurVI]]** is headquartered in Rostam City, supplying the Sector with VI software and semiconductors used to facilitate Cosmic Strand operations. It is widely theorized that AhurVI is **a front for a clandestine Ul'Rekar Hierocracy operation**.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Metal Storms (ferocious metallic-particle winds; strip paint; cause severe lacerations); Cryoquakes (super-cooled gas blasts; instant freezing; encase everything in ice within minutes); Electro Rain (statically charged precipitation; short-circuits electronics; dangerous electrical discharges)
- **Flora:** Ironbloom (metallic flowers; draw iron from soil; roots yield pure iron); Silvaroots (silver-barked trees; stabilize soil; draw up minerals for industrial use); Glintvines (reflective coated vines; disperse light in dark cavernous regions)
- **Fauna:** Lavamanders (amphibious; survive molten rock and liquid water; heat-resistant mucus); Shale Raptors (agile pack hunters with rock-slicing claws); Quarrelwyrms (giant blind burrowing creatures; vibration detection from miles away); Electroserpents (generate electrical currents; powerful defensive and offensive shocks); Ash Drakes (winged; camouflaged; sense and ingest minerals for sustenance)

## Manichae

![[ahura_manichae_main.png]]
![[ahura_manichae_persepol1.png]]
![[ahura_manichae_persepol2.png]]
![[ahura_manichae_persepol3.png]]

**Population:** 1 Trillion (stable)  
**Capital:** Persepol  
**Proxy Netjertum:** Qaed Danek  
**Industry:** Intra-Sector trade and finance; Navigator recruitment and contracting; resource management and planetary surveying

What most of humanity thinks of as the Ul'Rek capital — and the **richest settlement in the known galaxy**. Its primary industry: a Sector-wide system for **detecting and recruiting poly-psykers**. Individuals with the ability are located, brought to Persepol, physically examined, and put through **the Trials** — a series of dangerous physiological tests. Those who pass are reportedly sent to Ul'Haupt for Navigator training. On return, governments, corporations, and mercenary groups **bid on the graduating Navigator class**.

Despite historical neutrality, the Ul'Rekar would love to **seduce the Merovinist Triumvirate** away from the Republic — gaining access to a seemingly endless Lothranium supply. They have never openly attacked a human settlement; their expansion occurs only on newly discovered planets. Many organizations on Manichae are devoted to locating and surveying new systems for Hierocracy incorporation — or **sale to the highest bidder**.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Plasma Storms (neon-colored lightning; intense electromagnetic pulses; spectacular plasma arcs); Crystalline Rains (microscopic crystal precipitation; beautiful but damaging to crops and equipment); Magnetoquakes (sudden magnetic field shifts; violent tremors; affect electronics; precede plasma storms)
- **Flora:** Lumiferns (bioluminescent ferns providing natural light in megacity shadows); Aether Orchids (float above ground on near-invisible roots; sensitive to psychic energy; found near poly-psyker testing areas); Ferravines (metallic vines winding through city structures; absorb iron; can weaken infrastructure)
- **Fauna:** Electrogriffons (feathered; generate electric charges; leave trails of sparks in flight; deadly); Crysliths (crystalline-sand burrowers; known to disrupt psychic abilities in poly-psykers); Phantom Mantas (semi-transparent air-gliding creatures; harmless; eerily beautiful); Cerebravores (insect-like creatures that feed on psychic mental emanations; significant pest in testing areas); Holo-Sprites (dragonfly-like beings that emit holographic illusions; mimic larger creatures to confuse and entertain)

## Hara

![[ahura_hara_landscape1.png]]

**Population:** 11 million (growing steadily)  
**Capital:** Namazhar  
**Setbek:** Mahya Vadat  
**Industry:** Theological seminary and academia; agriculture

The quiet and picturesque moon Hara houses the **largest known public university in the Sector** — the **Academy of Galactic Sciences, Exploration, and Arts** (commonly called **The Academy** or **GC/G-SEA**), also home to a prestigious Ul'Rekar theological seminary. Foreign students are **accepted**, reflecting the belief that through education, all of humanity can advance regardless of status.

Hara's landscape is uniquely striking: most plants lack chlorophyll and instead carry rare **anthocyanins**, making them deep red and purple. To absorb enough sunlight, they grow significantly larger than plants found elsewhere in the Sector.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Aurora Tempests (vibrant color storms from magnetic field and solar wind interaction; disorienting); Whispering Winds (gentle melodic breezes from unique topography and plant life; ethereal); Solar Veils (thin shimmering curtains of refracting light; calming)
- **Flora:** Crimson Blossoms (towering up to 30 feet; vibrant red; high anthocyanin content); Amethyst Vines (thick purple vines covering the landscape; integrated into Namazhar's architecture); Sunburst Trees (massive trees with orange and gold leaves; incredibly broad for maximum sunlight absorption)
- **Fauna:** Luminastra (graceful quadrupeds with bioluminescent antlers; nocturnal); Razorths (large winged insects with razor-sharp light-reflecting wings); Veraliths (slender serpentine creatures; iridescent scales change color with mood; nearly invisible among plants); Scarhawkks (apex avian predators with red and purple plumage; swift silent flight); Glimmerbeetles (tiny glowing insects; swarm in mesmerizing light displays)

## Angra

![[ahura_angra_main.png]]
![[ahura_angra_chinvat1.png]]
![[ahura_angra_chinvat2.png]]

**Population:** 23 million  
**Capital:** Chinvat Bridge  
**Setbek:** Tarmen Sala  
**Industry:** Military training grounds; weapons manufacturing; military contracting

Angra's economy revolves entirely around the military. The Rhodian Legion headquarters and training facilities are based here in **Chinvat Bridge**. Raw materials from Hara are manufactured into weapons and armor for the **Rhodian Legion** and ships for the **White Fleet**.

Angra also houses the **Rhodian Defense Institute (RDI)** — the military officer academy. Unlike G-SEA on Hara, the RDI **does not welcome foreigners under any circumstances**. Visits require **direct clearance from the Proxy** and are extremely rare. A beautiful moon with good weather, covered by interconnected archipelagos of active volcanoes — all serving as military bases.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Vortex Storms (electromagnetic whirlwinds; disable electronics; sweep unpredictably); Cryo-Mist (dense freezing ocean fog; flash-freezes exposed skin and machinery; lingers for days); Obsidian Rain (fine sharp volcanic glass particles; shreds unprotected surfaces without specialized gear)
- **Flora:** Ironspires (towering metallic plants with razor-sharp reflective leaves; used as natural military barriers); Blazeblooms (luminous bioluminescent flowers; mark pathways and training grounds at night); Gravivines (sticky-resin ground-hugging vines; immobilize small creatures; hazardous in training areas)
- **Fauna:** Quarosaurs (massive armored quadrupeds used in combat simulations); Vorlaks (stealthy nocturnal predators with bioluminescent eyes; used in night operation training); Skyrays (winged manta-like creatures on electromagnetic currents; deliver powerful electric shocks); Pyronids (heat-resistant insectoid swarmers; common field exercise threat)
