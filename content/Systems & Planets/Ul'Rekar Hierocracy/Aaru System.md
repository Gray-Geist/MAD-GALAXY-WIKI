# Aaru System

**Section:** Mid-Core  
**Location:** Eburnean Sea  
**Nation:** Ul'Rekar Hierocracy  
**Status:** Restricted — foreigners not welcome except on approved diplomatic or scientific missions

> *Aaru — the heavenly paradise of Ancient Egyptian mythology*

![[aaru_systems_intro.png]]

---

## Overview

Aaru is not the first Ul'Rek colony, nor the most resource-rich — but it contains **valuable research facilities** managed by a more militant wing of the Ul'Rekar Hierocracy: the **Kayan Knights**. An elite fighting force, the Kayan Knights govern Aaru's provinces with strict authoritarianism and rigid adherence to religious principles.

Unlike the more open [[Ahura System]], foreigners are **typically not welcome** in Aaru unless on specific diplomatic or scientific missions approved by the Proxy or his Setbeks. Although the Kayan Knights run the system with stifling oversight and frequently clash ideologically with the Proxy, they have not broken tradition by attacking foreign settlements, and they respect the commands of the [[Ul'Rekar]] Hierocracy.

**Central Star:** Merul *(Nubian/Egyptian deity symbolizing the sun)* — a large C-type red giant nearing the end of its lifecycle  
**Ethnicity & Culture:** Ul'Rek

---

## Government & Society

Unlike other Ul'Rek systems, Aaru is not run by Setbeks. Instead, it is managed by the **Ul'Nizam** — an elite military commander who rises through the ranks of the Kayan Knights — and **two appointed Viziers**. Ul'Nizam candidates often come from rich and powerful noble families who can afford to send their children to the prestigious **Rhodian Defense Institute** on [[Ahura System#Angra|Angra]].

What separates the Ul'Nizam from Setbeks and other RDI cadets: a **panache for violence** and a desire for control over others. Aaru is one of the **safest and most efficient systems** in the Sector — at an enormous cost to individual sovereignty.

## Karnax

![[aaru_karnax_main.png]]
![[aaru_karnax_obelisk.png]]

**Population:** Theorized ~10,000; could be as many as 100,000  
**Capital:** Obelisk Labs  
**Vizier:** Ra Hik-Ten  
**Industry:** Research facility — contents unknown

Built on what should be an uninhabitable planet filled with rivers of flowing lava and extreme heat. **Obelisk Labs** — a tall monolithic structure that towers into the sky — is the sole building on the planet. What is inside is unknown.

All attempts to infiltrate Obelisk Labs have failed. It appears to be guarded by **Mounted Jackals** — Kayan marines walking the perimeter in enormous mechanical armor, carrying heavy repeaters capable of destroying landing starships. Intelligence suggests Obelisk Labs is either a corporate shell front for a top-secret Ul'Rekar Hierocracy facility, or that it is solely under **Kayan Knight control** — possibly an attempt to steal power from the Proxy.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Lava Tsunamis (massive waves of molten rock triggered by seismic activity; capable of engulfing entire regions); Pyroclastic Blizzards (superheated ash and toxic gases; lethal blizzards of fire and smoke; advanced filtration suits required); Acid Rainfalls (corrosive rain that eats through metal and stone; constant threat requiring protective shelters and suits)
- **Flora:** Flameweeds (flame-resistant; absorb and store heat to power metabolism; glowing orange and red fronds); Thermo-Shrooms (gigantic mushrooms releasing spores that cause hallucinations and intense fever when inhaled; dangerous without protection); Molten Vines (grow in and around lava flows; extract minerals from molten rock; bioluminescent shifting spectrum; tendrils can ensnare anything that gets too close)
- **Fauna:** Inferno Wyrms (massive serpentine creatures that tunnel through the planet's crust causing earthquakes; expel jets of flame; nearly impervious to heat); Blazebacks (reptilian creatures with armor-like heat-radiating scales; generate localized heat waves that distort air and disorient predators); Scorchclaws (predatory beasts with metal-and-rock-cutting claws; hunt by heat and vibration; extremely difficult to evade); Ember Bats (winged creatures; adapted air filtration; sonar so powerful it can disrupt electronic equipment); Ash Stalkers (stealthy predators that blend into the ashen landscape; manipulate coloration and body temperature to become nearly invisible to thermal and visual sensors)

## Denderon

![[aaru_denderon_main.png]]
![[aaru_denderon_new_memphis.png]]

**Population:** 100 million (stable)  
**Capital:** New Memphis  
**Vizier:** Gharda Bak  
**Industry:** Scientific research; Eidolon core research; bio-engineering; technological advancement

The **largest settlement in Aaru**, Denderon is a planet devoted to mostly confidential and top-secret research, bio-engineering, and technological advancement. The Kayan Knights maintain their **largest troop deployment** here and keep its populace in line and focused on their objectives. Intelligence leaks from Aaru space are rare.

Known through intelligence leaks: the Ul'Rekar Hierocracy spends **several Gigacreds per solar year** researching Eidolon core technology and human genetic research. To outside observers, private companies appear to lead these endeavors. In reality, **the central Ul'Rek theocracy runs all of them**.

**Disciplinary response:** Rebellious behaviors on Denderon are immediately addressed through **forced labor camps and re-education centers**.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Vortex Storms (intense spiraling wind columns; uproot trees and structures; frequent and unpredictable; require advanced warning systems); Lumina Showers (rare bioluminescent particle release from upper atmosphere; breathtaking light display; particles believed to have harvestable technological properties)
- **Flora:** Xenoflora (bio-engineered plant; adapts to extreme conditions; iridescent leaves change color based on nutrient absorption; used for bio-engineering testing); Neura Vines (rapid growth; neural-like structure; can interface with electronic devices; key to organic technology development); Crystal Bloom (crystalline petals; refract light into dazzling colors; medicinal properties; highly sensitive to environmental changes; used as ecological indicator)
- **Fauna:** Syntheti-Drones (artificial creatures for research and surveillance; mimic natural wildlife behavior while equipped with advanced sensors); Drakonids (large reptilian apex predators with bioluminescent adaptive camouflage; subjects of genetic research for their unique DNA); Aetherwings (avian creatures with transparent wings; silent gliding flight; feathers have light-absorbing properties studied for stealth technology); Volt Arachnids (spider-like creatures generating electrical charges; conductive silk valuable for new materials)

## KCom Station — "The Jackal's Haunt"

![[aaru_kcom_main.png]]

**Population:** 1 million (stable)  
**Ul'Nizam:** Izad Keivan  
**Industry:** Kayan Knight military command space station

Orbiting Denderon, the **Kayan Knight command center** is known as "The Jackal's Haunt" for its ominous glow visible over the planet's sky. Foreign diplomacy and meetings typically take place on this station — outsiders are **rarely permitted to land on Denderon** without explicit permission.

**Ul'Nizam Keivan** is a ruthless autocrat who rules with an iron fist. Kayan forces deploy from The Jackal's Haunt regularly, conducting sweeps looking for unsavory or divergent behaviors — especially concerning **poly-psykers**. It is said that trained poly-psykers are used by the Kayan Knights to identify those going against the expected code.

### Special Features
- **Holo-Habitats:** Advanced holographic environments used for training and recreation; can simulate various planetary conditions; essential for preparing Kayan Knights for diverse missions
- **Psionic Dampeners:** Specialized areas equipped with psionic dampening technology to suppress poly-psyker abilities; ensure security and control of potentially dangerous individuals

### Environment
- **Weather:** Quantum Fluctuations (unpredictable effects from proximity to Denderon's magnetic field; objects can temporarily phase out of existence or appear distorted); Electroplasma Storms (charged particle storms causing power surges and temporary blackouts; visually spectacular arcs of plasma across the outer hull); Dark Matter Drifts (pockets of dark matter creating gravitational anomalies; objects feel heavier or lighter; disrupt delicate scientific instruments)
- **Flora:** Void Vines (bioengineered for low-gravity; enhance oxygen levels; rapid growth; cling to surfaces); Lumen Moss (bioluminescent moss providing natural corridor lighting; absorbs radiation; converts to soft greenish glow)
- **Fauna:** Kayan Vornyx (ferocious genetically enhanced guard creatures; reptile-feline hybrid; senses honed to detect intruders; capable of tearing through metal); Nebula Bats (small winged creatures; echolocation navigation; squeeze through tight spaces; useful for scouting); Star Leeches (parasitic organisms attached to the outer hull; feed on energy output; constant maintenance nuisance)
- **Environmental Hazards:** Gravity Flux (irregular gravitational forces in certain sections; disorientation and equipment malfunctions); Radiation Pockets (higher radiation zones requiring protective suits); Micro-Meteor Showers (necessitate advanced shielding and rapid repair protocols)
