# Kissinger System

**Section:** Edge  
**Location:** Ephialtes Nebula *(Greek: a demon incubus responsible for nightmares; also the name of the man who betrayed the Spartans at Thermopylae)*  
**Nation:** Former Savage territory  
**Status:** Abandoned ruins; archaeological and criminal use only

> *"Twisted and ruthless. No longer truly human and no longer wishing to be seen as such."*

![[kissinger_intro.png]]

## Overview

On very rare occasions, evidence of Savage landings and colonization attempts has been discovered. The largest such discovery is **Kissinger** — lying near the Sector's core, the closest humanity has ever traveled to the galactic center.

The system appears to have been used for **resources, prisoners of war, and extreme experimentation on captured humans** from Coalition space. The Savages did not invest in infrastructure for a true settlement here — instead developing a series of prisons and sadistic research facilities.

**Central Star:** Promethax *(a reference to Prometheus, Greek Titan and god of fire)* — a red giant star at the end of its life cycle

---

## The Savages: Background

The Savages were **post-human** entities — not colonizers. They remained in the void between stars aboard massive Lighthugger ships (megacity-sized juggernaut vessels not equipped with skip-drives and thus incapable of FTL). The cold black darkness of the void between worlds was their home — safe, empty, without threat.

But the void gave nothing. The Savages **needed resources, materials, people, and flesh**. So they became raiders. And eventually, they became something far worse.

> *"The void was safe for the Savages, with nothing but cold and black darkness that swallows everything and gives nothing back."*

Their dynasties, cut off from humanity for generations, evolved along entirely separate paths — developing **occult technology** that could not be fathomed or reverse-engineered even centuries later. Their internal alliances were tenuous; only one dynasty could ultimately survive.

## Pele

![[kissinger_pele.png]]
![[kissinger_diamond_head.png]]

**Population:** Zero official residents  
**Central Ruins:** Diamond Head *(Hawaiian legend: a crater Pele created to hide and heal after battle with her sister)*  
**Industry:** No official industry; archaeological digs; resource salvaging; criminal organizations hiding contraband  
*(Pele: Hawaiian goddess of fire, wind, dance, and volcanoes)*

Pele is a barren wasteland littered with derelict structures and abandoned Savage research facilities. **Approximately 300 million human remains** were discovered here — genetics indicating they were taken from Coalition space, most likely during the Savage Wars.

**Diamond Head**, the central ruin complex, contains the **highest concentration of human remains**, as well as facilities whose walls are stained with blood and other bodily humors.

Today, research teams visit Pele to study the Savages and recover artifacts or technology. Pirates and criminal organizations sometimes use it to lie low or temporarily store illicit cargo.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Volcanic planet with frequent eruptions and lava flows; atmosphere thick with ash and smoke; pyroclastic flows make travel treacherous
- **Flora:** Lava Ferns (grow near volcanic activity, absorb nutrients from volcanic soil); Ember Flowers (glow with inner light, bloom after eruptions)
- **Fauna:** Fire Drakes (large dragon-like creatures with heat-resistant scales, breathe fire); Ash Beetles (feed on volcanic ash); Magma Serpents (swim through lava streams, deadly venom, extreme heat resistance)
