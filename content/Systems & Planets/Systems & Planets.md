# Systems & Planets

The Antevic Sector contains roughly 2,000 physically discovered star systems — a fraction of what advanced observation has logged, and a vanishingly small slice of the galaxy itself. Each system below has been settled, contested, or scarred by centuries of human history. Some are the beating hearts of civilizations. Others are ruins, prisons, or pirate havens at the edge of the known.

## Republic of Man

| System | Summary |
|---|---|
| [[Alexandria]] | The Republic capital — a colossal space station of impossible scale, virtually impregnable, seat of the Senate and the Synarchs |
| [[Praetarus System]] | The Republic's industrial and population heart; Aquila (one trillion people), Aetna, Sabine, Aurelia |
| [[Primus System]] | The first human settlement; Pentarchy holy land; Pacem's Elysian Cathedral, Alpha Terra's decline, and the restless Coalition minority on Veles |
| [[Ilion Beta System]] | Republic Edge foothold; the Archipelago islands; CenCon's headquarters at FLS Voltaire |

| [[Ryūgū System]] | A recent Arienai-backed colonial project; the multi-ocean research world Suijin and the struggling colony Nagas |
