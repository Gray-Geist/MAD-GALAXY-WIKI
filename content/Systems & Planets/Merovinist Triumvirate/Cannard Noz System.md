# Cannard Noz System

**Full Name:** Cannard Noz *(Ancient Breton/Celtic: the "Midnight Washerwomen" — three mysterious figures who wash shrouds for men about to die; a three-part deity of death and slaughter)*  
**Section:** Mid-Core  
**Location:** Gaulian Expanse  
**Nation:** Merovinist Triumvirate (independent)  
**Status:** Sovereign trading federation; major Lothranium and drug hub

![[cannard_noz_intro.png]]

---

## Overview

**Cannard Noz** is the home system of the **Merovinist Triumvirate** — known throughout the Sector simply as **"The Family"**. Originally founded by the privately owned **Merovin-Lero Corporation** to escape government oversight and interference in their boundary-pushing commerce and trade.

The system's three habitable planets — Taranis, Cissonius, and Ankou — are each governed by one of three ruling families and are a metaphor for the Cannard Noz myth: the god of thunder, the gods of trade, and the guardian of the graveyard.

**Central Star:** Andrasta *(named after the Breton goddess of victory, following the Merovinists' victory in the civil war)* — an orange K-Type main sequence star, relatively young in its lifecycle  
**Ethnicity & Culture:** Gaulian

## History

Disagreements in colonial regulation led to **tribal infighting within the Merovin-Lero Corporation**, culminating in a brief civil war that ended with the assassination of key Lero family members and stakeholders. With the Merovins in control, the three planets were divided among siblings — each independently governing, with vastly different results. Centuries later, a tenuous but peaceful alliance remains, despite the envy directed at Cissonius' disproportionate success.

During the **Savage Wars**, the Triumvirate joined the Astral Alliance and provided vital fuel and munitions support. After the war, when the newly formed Republic demanded the Triumvirate disband and assimilate, it resulted in a short war. The Republic suffered mostly embarrassing defeats — the Merovins, by hoarding fuel and striking trade lanes with lightning speed, brought acute economic pressure to the nascent Republic despite being vastly outgunned. Both sides knew the Republic would eventually win through attrition.

The result: the **Ysian Accords** — sovereignty for the Merovinist Triumvirate within Cannard Noz, and an unwavering trade alliance between the two powers.

## Government: The Family

The **Merovinist Triumvirate** is a trading federation led by three related families, each governing one planet:
- **Merovins** — Cissonius/Cissonia (most powerful family)
- **Carols** — Taranis (envious of Cissonius' success)
- **Bosonids** — Ankou (heartless; engaged in the darkest enterprises)

**Key values:**
- Economic and social liberalism
- Strong opposition to trafficking and chattel slavery *(unlike their former Lero partners)*
- Contract servitude is permitted for debt clearance
- Illicit drug consumption is **legal** under personal responsibility
- Freedom from government oversight for corporations leasing space here

---

## Main Industries

- Drug and chemical production, trade, and smuggling

## Taranis

![[cannard_noz_brecilien3.png]]

**Population:** 200,000 (slowly declining)  
**Capital:** Brecilien *(Anglicized from Brocéliande — the fabled Arthurian forest, Vale of No Return)*  
**Ruling Family:** Carols  
**Industry:** Lothranium production and refining; hospitality; hydrogen and oxygen mining  
*(Taranis: the ancient Breton god of thunder)*

The entry point for all travelers to the Cannard Noz system. Taranis is a hot, cloudy planet with an atmosphere of mostly carbon dioxide, sulfuric acid, and nitrogen. Most inhabitants live in underground structures or massively fortified concrete buildings where oxygen synthesized from soil and nitrogen from the atmosphere is pumped through extensive pipe networks.

Potable water is synthesized from hydrogen miners who travel to the nearby gas giant **Sulis** *(Breton: goddess of healing hot springs)*, clearly visible in the sky. Sulis' gravitational pull creates massive storms at its closest point — Brecilien is located safely distant from this epicenter. Nearly **95% of the population lives in Brecilien**; the rest lead expeditions hunting new Lothranium deposits. Many locals call themselves people of the Sulis Moon.

Taranis is the **second-largest Lothranium manufacturing center** in the Triumvirate after Cissonia.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Violent electrical storms; hurricane-force winds; perpetually dark clouds illuminated by lightning
- **Flora:** Storm Reeds (channel electrical energy from lightning); Thunder Trees (conductive bark disperses lightning safely); Electric Blooms (emit small sparks)
- **Fauna:** Lightning Hawks (ride electrical currents, apex predators); Storm Wolves (electrically charged fur, shocking bites); Spark Beetles (store electrical energy in bodies)

**Population:** Cissonius: 200 million / Cissonia: 1 million (stable)  
**Capitals:** Ys (Cissonius) · Carnac Stone (Cissonia capitol spire)  
**Ruling Family:** Merovins  
**Industry:** Financial and commercial hypercenter (Cissonius); Lothranium production and refining (Cissonia)  
*(Cissonius and Cissonia: the married gods of trade and commerce)*

Cissonius is a small, mostly verdant planet with a mild climate, clean rivers, and resources necessary for human survival. Its location, weather, and loose economic supervision make it an **oasis for corporations** seeking shelter from restrictive governments. Scientific researchers also flock here knowing their work will go largely unnoticed and unregulated — as long as they pay their leasing fees to the Merovins.

**Ys** — the capital — houses 75% of the population, a concrete metropolitan wasteland fit for wealthy hedonists, corporate elites, and a dark underbelly. The countryside is largely untouched and picturesque, drawing researchers and those seeking isolation.

**Cissonia** (the moon) is the de facto administrative capital for the Triumvirate. Leaders convene at Carnac Stone. **VinCorp** — the largest, most profitable, and most powerful of the Merovist subsidiary corporations — is headquartered here as the financial arm of the Merovist government. Underground rivers of liquid Lothranium flow into surface craters, making Cissonia's operation **the largest Lothranium source among the three settlements**.

### Environment
- **Weather (Cissonius):** Temperate with frequent showers and occasional thunderstorms
- **Weather (Cissonia):** Cold and arid with frequent dust storms
- **Flora (Cissonius):** Rainforest Giants (massive interconnected canopies); Water Vines (draw moisture from air)
- **Flora (Cissonia):** Dust Cacti; Stone Flowers (drought survivors)
- **Fauna (Cissonius):** Aqua Drakes (amphibious reptiles); Sky Fish (bioluminescent flying fish)
- **Fauna (Cissonia):** Dust Serpents (burrow to escape storms); Rock Hoppers (small mammals leaping between boulders)

**Population:** 500,000 (rapidly growing at unstable rate)  
**Capital:** Bered Town *(Bered: Breton for "graveyard")*  
**Ruling Family:** Bosonids  
**Industry:** Production and sale of illicit chemicals and drugs; smuggling  
*(Ankou: the last to die in a village before midnight, doomed to walk the earth as guardian of the graveyard — source of inspiration for the Grim Reaper)*

On the final anchorage of Cannard Noz lies a barren meteor-like planet that is a **haven for smugglers, criminals, and some of the most nefarious humans in the Sector**. On all levels of Ankouzian society, malefactors do whatever they must to survive. Rule and order blur easily in this lawless settlement — anything goes, as long as the Bosonids are kept well paid and informed.

Those who don't follow these two rules face the **Tonton Macoute** *(Haitian: "Boogeymen" — a para-military secret police responsible for 60,000 deaths under Papa Doc)* — state-sponsored enforcers who use violence swiftly at the ruling family's behest.

Criminals learned early it is more advantageous to work with the Bosonids than against them. Despite being the smallest settlement by population, **Ankou generates more per-capita wealth than Taranis**. With barely any Lothranium, the Bosonids thrive as brokers in the inter-Sector black market, flooding it with illicit substances. Some family members engage in businesses so dark that the Merovins once led a civil war to stop them.
