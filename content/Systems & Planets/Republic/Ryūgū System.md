# Ryūgū System

**Section:** Edge  
**Location:** Styx Delta  
**Nation:** Republic of Man (recent acquisition)  
**Status:** Active colonial development; corporate-government hybrid governance

![[ryugu_intro.png]]

---

## Overview

One of the Republic's **more recent settlements**, the **Ryūgū Colonial Project** is a joint venture between the Republic government and a subsidiary of the [[Arienai Corporation]] — **Arienai Ventures**. Scientist Dr. **Rexan Corth** started the expedition while working for Arienai Ventures' wholly owned **Exo-Core Contractors**. Twenty-five years later, Corth converted his private sector role into an official governorship, providing the Arienai Corporation with yet another broker of power in government.

The planet Suijin officially entered the Republic **five years ago, in 1064 AE**, following the Republic's purchase of the territory from Exo-Core Contractors. As part of the deal, Dr. Corth was appointed Governor and Congress of the People delegate with a **20-year suspended election cycle** while the colony grows.

Two years ago, Exo-Core — led by Dr. Corth's brother Xan — used proceeds from the sale to begin colonizing the neighboring planet **Nagas**.

**Central Star:** Upsilon Cephei-35 — an orange M-Type main sequence star, near the end of its lifecycle  
**Ethnicity & Culture:** Primarily Praetarian settlers; emerging culture called **Corthan** (after the founder)

## Nagas

![[ryugu_nagas_main.png]]
![[ryugu_nagas_espera1.png]]
![[ryugu_nagas_espera2.png]]

**Population:** ~1,000 (nascent; growth unknown)  
**Main Settlement:** Espera  
**Organization:** Exo-Core Contractors, led by **Xan Corth** (Frontier Project Leader)  
**Industry:** Planet settlement; mineral surveying

Founded two years ago by Xan Corth — widely seen as **incompetent** and viewed as a nepotistic appointment by his brother Dr. Rexan Corth. Settlers condemn the decision openly. In two years, **Nagas Core One (NC1)** has lost nearly **one-third of its original team** and is behind schedule.

The conditions are brutal. Harsh living, dangerous wildlife, and constant environmental threats define life on Nagas.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Electro-Storms (violent lightning from high atmospheric pressure; unpredictable; lasting hours; severe infrastructure disruption; reinforced shelters required); Sulfurous Fog (volcanic activity generates dense toxic corrosive fog; requires protective suits; obscures visibility)
- **Flora:** Blight Ferns (thrive in acidic sulfur-laden soil; absorb sulfur and convert it to less harmful substances; tough waxy fronds; difficult to cultivate); Ironbark Trees (massive slow-growing trees with steel-hard bark; incorporate metals into structure; resist electro-storms; very difficult to harvest)
- **Fauna:** Volcanic Hounds (large quadrupedal predators; heat-resistant hides; survive sulfurous fog; highly territorial pack hunters that raid the colony's outskirts); Glow Lizards (small bioluminescent reptiles with faint greenish glow; non-threatening; feed on insects and plants; studied for their unique adaptations); Radmoths (large iridescent radioactive-glow moths; nocturnal; swarm artificial light; scales cause skin irritation; indicate high radiation levels)

## Suijin

![[ryugu_suijin_main.png]]
![[ryugu_suijin_kitezh_above.png]]
![[ryugu_suijin_kitezh_below.png]]
![[ryugu_suijin_protea.png]]

**Population:** 30,000 (slowly increasing)  
**Capital:** Kitezh  
**Governor:** Dr. Rexan Corth *(20-year suspended election cycle)*  
**Industry:** Planet settlement; scientific research facilities

A planet of **multiple liquid oceans**, each separated by underwater land formations and filled with liquids of different densities:

| Ocean | Composition |
|---|---|
| **Vitae** | Freshwater |
| **Salmar** | Saltwater (larger than Vitae) |
| Two equatorial oceans | Various radioactive elements and metals (near the equator's heat) |
| **The Corthian Sea** | Mysterious chemical unique to Suijin — named **Corthium** by Dr. Corth (the subject of decades of research) |

**Kitezh** — the capital and largest facility — has sections both above and below Vitae's freshwater surface. It sits miles from the Corthian Sea, making it the ideal central hub for nearby research. The growing research settlement of **Protea** is found just north of the Corthian Sea in Salmar.

Dr. Corth has been accused of **dismissing state anti-corruption laws** — unilaterally appointing his brother Xan to lead the Nagas team at Exo-Core's behest rather than the Republic's. The government turned a blind eye, hoping to replicate Suijin's success. This is far from the first time the Arienai Corporation has influenced the Republic to do its bidding.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Liquid Storms (currents from different-density oceans collide, creating violent turbulence in waters; makes navigation and underwater operations extremely dangerous; protective barriers and advanced forecasting are vital); Radiation Waves (equatorial radioactive oceans periodically surge surface radiation waves; severe electronic damage; significant health risks; specialized shielding required)
- **Flora:** Luminous Algae (bioluminescent blue-green algae in Vitae; illuminate underwater Kitezh sections; key ecosystem food source); Metalweave Kelp (Salmar; incorporates metallic elements into its structure; incredibly tough; harvested for underwater construction); Corthium Vines (grow near the Corthian Sea; absorb and metabolize Corthium; iridescent hues; subject of intense biotechnology and medicine research)
- **Fauna:** Vitae Drifters (enormous gentle jellyfish-like creatures in Vitae's freshwater; stunning bioluminescent displays; filter nutrients from water; ecosystem-critical); Salmar Predators (fearsome apex hunters in saltwater; metallic scales and razor-sharp teeth; pose threat to divers); Radiant Eels (radioactive element oceans; harness and emit radioactive energy; deliver lethal shocks; glowing warning patterns); Corthian Spinners (small agile fish in the Corthian Sea; feed on Corthium; rapid spinning motion evades predators; of significant research interest)
