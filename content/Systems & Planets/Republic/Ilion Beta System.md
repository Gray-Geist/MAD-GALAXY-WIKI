**Section:** Edge

## Overview

The **Ilion Beta** system sits at the heart of the **Spinward March** — a multi-sector region of space near the Edge known for sparse worlds and warring splinter governments. It is the Republic's primary edge world foothold and one of its earlier colonial systems, now economically self-sufficient with a trade surplus.

The system's strategic location on the border of Coalition space and the edge of known space gives it added importance. The Republic has stationed one of its **largest and most advanced fleets** here for decades — the **Sixth Armada** (aka the **Hyperion Vanguard**) — making Ilion Beta the only Edge system with a permanent Republic fleet presence.

A unique feature: the **Archipelago** — a massive network of interconnected space stations near the gas giant Dardanus, serving as a commercial and recreational hub for traders, gamblers, and furloughed military.

**Central Star:** Chrysomallos — a yellow H-Type main sequence star, midway through its lifecycle

## History

In **1726 AE**, Republic aid was requested against a marauding pirate king who had laid orbital siege to the capital planet. The Republic moved in force, drove the pirate king off, and began negotiating to make the Mycenax civilization a Protectorate. The fiercely independent Mycenax resisted diplomatically.

A revolution then occurred — **some called it a color revolution** — where rebels argued their heritage traced back to the Republic. Through moderate violent means, the rebels gained control of government and enacted the **Sensa Resolution**, officially making Ilion Beta a Republic Protectorate.

---

## Government & Society

Executive Protector **Vel Xebika** represents all of Ilion Beta from the planet Mycenae. The government is not officially represented at the Republic level and has enacted laws to operate as a **"gray zone" for Republic Lex** — granting some favorable self-governance.

However, like some Republic settlements, Ilion Beta is dependent on [[Cannard Noz System|Merovinist Triumvirate]] Lothranium, as well as Republic military support.

The Archipelago's commercial stations are rented to private companies; private vessels may join the gravitational link after an approval process and annual fee.

## The Archipelago

**Permanent Population:** 250,000 (varies seasonally; gaming/speculation tourists can quadruple this during peak times)  
**Main Settlement:** Argonautica  
**Industry:** Gaming and speculation; commercial trading; hospitality; import/export inspections

The **Archipelago** is a series of small, medium, and large space stations all linked together using an **intra-gravitational link (IGL)** near the gas giant Dardanus. Shuttles carry visitors throughout the network.

The Archipelago contains:
- Shopping and recreational centers
- Casinos offering gaming, speculation, excursions, tours, and unique experiences
- Government-run stations regulating commercial activity entering the Republic from Coalition space
- Private tours of the pinkish outer atmosphere of **Dardanus** on specially designed ships

It is a popular destination for gamblers, traders, and furloughed military — and regularly frequented by government regulators due to its Coalition border proximity.

**Note:** [[Centurion Contracted Corps (CenCon)|CenCon's]] headquarters — the FLS Voltaire — is docked in the Archipelago.

### Environment
- **Climate:** Artificial climate zones tailored per station — mild in shopping areas, simulated weather in recreational centers, lush casino environments with artificial sunrises and sunsets
- **Radiation:** Advanced radiation shielding against solar flares; panels adjust to diffuse harsh solar glare
- **Flora:** Extensive hydroponic gardens (fresh produce and relaxation spaces); bio-engineered decorative flora (luminescent vines, fragrant blossoms, air-purifying moss gardens)
- **Fauna:** Service drones (maintenance, cleaning, delivery — advanced AI navigation); recreational bots (lifelike robotic animals for entertainment); security AI drones (unobtrusive, non-lethal measures); bio-luminescent engineered insects in garden areas

## Mycenae

![[ilion_beta_eos.png]]

**Population:** 300 million (increasing)  
**Capital:** Agamenopol  
**Executive Protector:** Vel Xebika  
**Industry:** Financial, commercial, and trading sectors; manufacturing; academic

The resource-rich planet Mycenae is one of the Republic's earliest and greatest success stories — partly because the Republic historically took a **laissez-faire approach**, allowing colonists to develop economic sectors naturally matched to their environment rather than imposing top-down command systems.

The result: an **advanced, profitable, and proudly independent civilization** that has endured on the Edge of Republic space. Aspiring entrepreneurs flock here for favorable business laws and access to intra-Sector trading ports.

Mycenae is also home to the **University of Eos-Agamenopol (UEA)** — a large private academic center spread between the cities of Eos and Agamenopol.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Dry summer climate — hot dry summers, mild wet winters; occasional sandstorms from nearby deserts
- **Flora:** Olive Trees (arid-adapted, abundant); Desert Blooms (bloom spectacularly after rainfall); Stone Vines (grow on rocky surfaces)
- **Fauna:** Mountain Lions (apex predators, rocky terrain); Desert Eagles (cliff-nesting birds of prey); Sand Foxes (small mammals with sharp senses)
