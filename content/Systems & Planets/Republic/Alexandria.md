## Overview

**Alexandria** is housed on a colossal fortified space station found on the edge of the **Higgs Nexus** — directly accessible by all primary anchorages in each Republic settlement, serving as the central hub of their entire territorial expanse. All trade, commerce, travel, and communication relays through this technological marvel.

Despite its physical isolation, the station boasts the **most advanced weapons systems known to humankind** and is rumored to house an experimental shield system. It is virtually impregnable.

Regardless of one's allegiance, Alexandria remains a symbol of humanity's potential and achievement.

> *"Only the truly elite can go within Alexandria."* — RIA intelligence note on Sage's Row Orbital approach

---

## History

Humanity started this project in **secret before the Savage Wars**, stripping several planets entirely of their resources to build the first iteration. The project took nearly a century to complete — the Astral Alliance razed entire planets and drained their molten cores for parts and energy. Destroyed starships from the Savage Wars were recycled, broken down, and repurposed to facilitate its completion.

## Neighborhoods & Districts

| District | Description |
|---|---|
| **Victoria Centrum** | The main political area — all Republic central political and governmental offices, including the Executive Tower, Senate, House of Reason, and agency headquarters |
| **Shibuya Town** | Young, stylish, and cosmopolitan — known for culture, nightlife, and hospitality |
| **Sapien City** | Poor by station standards; houses political and professional pariahs forcefully kept from upscale neighborhoods; located near the station's plasma core; the blue collar workforce running the core lives here; periodic protests turning violent over lack of housing and forced segregation; extremely rare for anyone here to ever move out |
| **Nova Angelus** | The largest region — financial and commercial sectors; residential for elites and the well-to-do |
| **Bessemer** | Industrial and factory center; station infrastructure — water manufacturing, waste management, food production |
| **Port Constance** | The bustling spaceport — commercial hub for all imports and exports; the doorway into and out of the seat of Republic power |
