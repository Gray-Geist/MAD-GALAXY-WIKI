# Praetarus System

**Section:** Core  
**Location:** Higgs Nexus, Capitoline Sector  
**Nation:** Republic of Man  
**Status:** The Republic's beating heart; most populated system in the Sector

---

## Overview

The **Appian Anchor** — the route between capital [[Alexandria]] and the Republic's homeland Aquila — is the most traveled and arguably the most important anchorage within the Sector, crossed by billions every solar cycle.

Praetarus experienced a tumultuous and bloody history, reaching a violent apex during the Savage Wars when Savage Marines turned the system into a years-long stalemate. Through the concerted effort of the Astral Alliance, the Savages were pushed out following a **17-year offensive called "Operation Carthage"** — widely considered the turning point of the Savage Wars.

Today, Praetarus contains the majority of the Republic's population and is the economic and industrial crossroads of the Sector.

**Central Star:** Mithra — a yellow K-Type main sequence star, midway through its lifecycle  
**Ethnicity & Culture:** Alexandrian/Praetarian

## Government & Society

Although [[Alexandria]] is the official capital of the Republic, **Aquila** is considered the Republic's homeland, cultural epicenter, and most important planet. Unofficially, Aquila's High Governor is the second most influential political figure in the Republic. All other governors in the system report to the High Governor, who in turn reports directly to Victoria Centrum in Alexandria.

**Population:** 1 Trillion (stable/slowly growing)  
**Capital:** Coryth  
**High Governor:** Caius Imperati  
**Industry:** Export of raw and manufactured materials; financial services; private enterprise

An **ecumenopolis** — a city-planet. The majority of habitable land is entirely devoted to housing one trillion people. The Savage Wars damaged most of Aquila's interconnected megacities; during reconstruction, the Republic urbanized at an even deeper level to incentivize rapid population rebound and project strength.

Today, Aquila is an **economic hypercenter** for the Republic and arguably the most diverse human planet. People from all corners of the Sector travel here for its boundless financial opportunities and technological innovations, including planetary cooling mechanisms that mitigate the warming effects of rapid industrialism.

Aquila is the headquarters of **[[Atheon Heavy Industries]]** — the Sector's largest and most successful corporation focused on AI, robotics, cybernetics, and non-human engineering. The Republic gives Atheon virtual carte blanche; controlling and monitoring Aquila's one trillion person population is effectively impossible without Atheon's technical support. It is more likely that a citizen is fined or arrested by a **weaponized bot** than by an actual human.

**Fibona (Sub District 2257):** A thriving megacity district where an unusually high percentage of inhabitants have an affinity for numbers. Many of the Sector's well-known mathematicians and numerologists come from here. Most children from Fibona grow up on a path that yields near-guaranteed wealth and success through mathematical careers in pharmaceuticals, communications, defense, or education. However, in recent decades approximately **1.8% of teens** born in Fibona have begun using their innate skills for immediate illicit gain, a percentage trending upward.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Controlled climate zones across the planet; massive Cloudscapers create localized microclimates including sudden temperature drops, rain showers, and gusty winds; perpetual hazy cloud cover from industrial emissions visible from space; dramatic smog-enhanced sunrises and sunsets
- **Flora:** Sky gardens on skyscrapers (vertical hydroponic systems, exotic bio-engineered plants); bioluminescent flora (glows at night, reduces artificial lighting needs); aerial mosses (cling to buildings, filter pollutants)
- **Fauna:** Urban avian species adapted to navigate building gaps; subterranean bio-luminescent fungi and small creatures in forgotten underground tunnels; cybernetically enhanced creatures (robotic cats for pest control; cybernetic birds for surveillance)

**Population:** 50 million (stable)  
**Capital:** Matterhorn  
**Governor:** Ilia Nixus  
**Industry:** Mineral and resource mining, extraction, and shipping (primarily to Aquila)

The sole purpose of Aetna is to supply the system with raw materials. It is by far the **Sector's largest known mining operation** — virtually all inhabitants work for mining corporations or adjacent professions.

> *"Not even the rocks on Aetna were born here, but they bleed credits when you break 'em so who cares."* — Aetnan saying

Most inhabitants are men, either from generational mining families or arriving from other systems seeking income. Partners and families typically live on other systems; miners earn and send credits home. Life is brutal — many young people leave each year to join the Republic Guard or relocate to Aquila, but the population remains stable because of how lucrative mining can be.

### Key Resources
| Resource | Properties |
|---|---|
| **Forcyron** | Immensely durable metal; processed with carbon into Proto-Steel; resists high temperatures, physical damage; ideal for space travel |
| **Potensium** | Rare radioactive and unstable element from deep in Aetna's mantle; used as energy source for space station and military spacecraft reactors |
| **Psyllicate Dust & Crystals** | Delicate crystal mined and manufactured into cybernetic semiconductors |

### Environment
- **Weather:** Volcanic planet with frequent eruptions and lava flows; atmosphere filled with ash and smoke; dark and ominous
- **Flora:** Ash Trees (survive volcanic soil); Lava Flowers (bloom in heat, release seeds during eruptions); Fire Ferns (grow near lava flows)
- **Fauna:** Magma Beasts (withstand intense heat); Ash Vultures (scavenge near volcanic craters); Lava Worms (burrow through molten rock)

## Sabine

![[praetarus_sabine_main.png]]
![[praetarus_sabine_tarpeian.png]]
![[praetarus_sabine_biotech.png]]

**Population:** 5 million (growing steadily)  
**Capital:** Tarpeian Rock  
**Governor:** Marquella Sade  
**Industry:** Human detainment and prisoner rehabilitation; human biological research

With billions of people in this system and the Republic as a whole, **Sabine** — run by the ruthless Governor Marquella Sade — is essentially a **prison colony** built into a cold and barren planet. There are no worthwhile resources here, but enough vegetation and water to sustain an oxygen atmosphere. All food and water must be imported, and if shipments are ever delayed, chaos ensues in the open air prisons.

Governor Sade has **expertly and cruelly** navigated this variable for decades — using the food supply itself as leverage to keep prison factions in check.

Individuals sent to **Tarpeian Rock** are typically the worst of the worst, but Aquila's overpopulation has increased the flow of minor offenders who stand little chance against the brutish population. The advertised slogan is "rehabilitation and chance for all." It is rare that anyone ever leaves Tarpeian Rock alive. Those who do are never the same.

**Biotech research facilities** operate on the outskirts of Tarpeian Rock — their research is largely unknown. Governor Sade frequently dines with their directors, and together they **hand-select prisoners** who enter the facilities and never return.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Frigid winds sweeping across barren landscape; temperature rarely rises above freezing; perpetual overcast with thick gray clouds; no sunlight
- **Flora:** Frostweed (hardy, dense clusters, thick waxy thorned leaves; not edible; causes severe skin irritation); Bitterbark Trees (gnarled, dark, twisted; bark mildly antiseptic but otherwise toxic; hard inedible fruits); Snowspike Moss (gray-green, grows on rocks; absorbs moisture from air; crucial to the limited ecosystem)
- **Fauna:** Ice Fang Serpents (large, venomous; paralytic and lethal venom; highly aggressive); Frostclaw Drakons (massive predators; thick fur and sharp claws; highly territorial; encounters often fatal); Snow Wraiths (packs of nocturnal pale hunters; nearly invisible against snow; coordinated pack attacks; eerie howling communication); Steelbeak Birds (metallic beaks that tear through flesh and some armor; highly territorial)

**Capital:** Mara  
**Star Marshall:** Faustus Kern, Republic Guard  
**Commandant:** Hector Indares, RTAC  
**Industry:** Military training grounds; weapons manufacturing; military contracting

Technically a moon orbiting the gas giant **Palos**, Aurelia is the **central hub and training ground** for Republic forces and the headquarters for their special black operations. All facilities are built into Aurelia's mantle and toward its core — providing natural protection, heat, and energy for stationed troops. Large above-ground training sectors simulate various combat styles, including a nearby space station serving the space fleet.

Third-party contractors maintain offices recruiting soldiers whose government contracts have expired. Arms manufacturers work hand-in-hand with soldiers who test the latest weapons and gear.

Aurelia is **the pride and joy of the Republic** and a large reason for their survival during the Savage Wars. Its hard-to-find location made it the perfect place to plan counter-attacks against the Savage Marines. For the entirety of the war, Aurelia remained unscathed and **Mara** was the epicenter of the Astral Alliance's resistance.

Today a monument stands at this location symbolizing Antevic unity and Republic power.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Eternal twilight (thin atmosphere); perpetual dusk-like conditions; deep indigo sky with gas giant Palos dominating the horizon; rare but fierce Lunar Sky Quakes (high-pressure wind storms damaging exposed equipment); minerals on the surface fluoresce from Palos radiation creating an ethereal nocturnal glow
- **Flora:** Moon Lichen (silvery sheen; withstands extreme temperatures and radiation; emits faint blue light); Radiant Cacti (thick segmented bodies; bioluminescent; edible fruit; water storage); Crag Moss (grows in rock crevices; absorbs moisture; harvested for medicinal treatment of radiation burns)
- **Fauna:** Lunar Arachnids (large six-legged; low-gravity adapted; bioluminescent markings to lure prey; nocturnal hunters); Void Rats (oversized radiation-adapted scavengers; raid supply caches; used in experiments and training); Spectral Hawks (partially transparent wings; silent flight; solitary predators); Aurora Beetles (bioluminescent swarming insects; attracted to artificial light; interfere with sensitive equipment; pollinate plant life)
