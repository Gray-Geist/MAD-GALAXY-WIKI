# Olodumare System

**Section:** Core  
**Location:** The Shadow Pall  
**Nation:** Republic of Man  
**Status:** Vital food supply system for the Republic; heavily militarized

---

## Overview

One of the earliest settlements in the Republic of Man, the **Olodumare** system acts as one of the Republic's "Gardens" — supplying a steady and reliable food source to the entire nation. The system's powerful star **Olorun** makes the inner planets uninhabitable, but the two planets farthest from it sit perfectly in the habitable zone, ideal for year-round farming.

Because of its strategic importance, garrisons of Republic Guard are stationed at the **Ogun Beltline** ready to protect the system and its food transports at all costs.

**Central Star:** Olorun — a blue B-Type dwarf main sequence star, at the beginning of its lifecycle  
**Ethnicity & Culture:** Orishan

## Government & Society

Both planets send representatives to [[Alexandria]]. The **Ogun Beltline** serves as the system's military backbone and travel checkpoint. **Solarus Station** serves as the corporate center for both farming planets to maximize their respective land use.

Strategically built in an asteroid belt, the **Beltline Defense Network (BDN)** comprises:
- Five military bases (Republic Guard Sectors: Utah, Omaha, Gold, Juno, Sword)

## Solarus Station

![[olodumare_solarus_station.png]]

**Population:** 100,000 (stable)  
**Administrator:** Ravi Atreya  
**Industry:** Agricultural corporate headquarters; trading and exchange floors

Created to maximize efficiency across both farming planets. The **trading floor on Market Square** is one of the busiest in the Sector, running at all hours. Because of its financial and agricultural importance, it is guarded by some of the Republic's most sophisticated juggernauts and destroyers.

**[[Nutrionix]]** corporate headquarters is located in Mercury Ward.

| District | Function |
|---|---|
| Market Square | Trading and exchange center for agricultural goods from Orisha Oko and Erinle |

## Orisha Oko

![[olodumare_orisha_oko.png]]
![[olodumare_aja.png]]

**Population:** 1 million (stable and controlled)  
**Capital:** Aja  
**Planetary Governor:** Alabi Adebayo  
**Industry:** Agriculture and farming; water transportation

Far from Olorun, Orisha Oko and sibling Erinle orbit in what would typically result in icy barren planets — but Olorun's enormous size pushes the habitable zone far outward. The result: **two verdant and fertile worlds**.

Orisha Oko was the first of "**the Twins**" — boasting a warm tropical climate ideal for year-round farming and large sugary citrus fruits. Despite being older, it is **less developed and less populated**. The government deliberately restricts development and travel to protect its vital industry.

However, the **Placid Isles** off the coast of Aja feature premier resorts and romantic coastlines that attract wealthy travelers.

Orisha Oko is also the **Republic's largest producer and exporter of sugar- and fruit-distilled spirits** — including *saccharum* and *groja*.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Tropical; frequent rain showers; high humidity; warm and stable; occasional thunderstorms
- **Flora:** Banana Trees; Spice Bushes (fragrant and flavorful); Rain Ferns
- **Fauna:** Jungle Monkeys; Parrot Snakes (colorful, mimic bird calls); Water Buffalo

## Erinle

![[olodumare_erinle1.png]]
![[olodumare_erinle2.png]]
![[olodumare_cornucopia.png]]

**Population:** 50 million (steadily growing)  
**Capital:** Cornucopia  
**Planetary Governor:** Elia Tilo  
**Industry:** Agriculture; farming; livestock domestication and slaughter; agricultural technology and engineering

The younger, more populous, and more developed of the Twins. Regulations are more lax than Orisha Oko and the manufacturing sector significantly larger. In addition to farming and livestock, Erinle has an **industrial center focused on agricultural technology and engineering**, including genetic research companies that modify food sources.

[[GaiaTech]] modified a strain of hucklewheat here to resist sub-zero temperatures — now sold to the Republic's most remote colonies.

The capital **Cornucopia** sits in a temperate zone with deep water ports and fertile grain land. Like its sister, Erinle is a significant alcohol producer — known for **hearty ales** and **plant-based intoxicants**.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Mild; moderate rainfall; seasonal changes; gentle breezes; occasional thunderstorms
- **Flora:** Willow Trees (along riverbanks); Meadow Flowers (vibrant seasonal blooms); Herb Gardens (medicinal plants)
- **Fauna:** River Otters; Field Mice; Heron Birds (wade in shallow waters)
