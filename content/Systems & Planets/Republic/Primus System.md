# Primus System

**Section:** Core  
**Location:** Gagarin Nebula  
**Nation:** Republic of Man (formerly Coalition)  
**Status:** Republic-controlled reparations territory; Pentarchy holy land; social unrest on Veles

![[primus_intro.png]]

---

## Overview

**Primus** is widely considered to hold the **first major human settlement** — Alpha Terra. Despite its enormous historical importance, Primus is not resource-wealthy and life across its settlements is arduous.

Once the epicenter of Eidolon Core Drive production, Primus suffered an economic depression as manufacturing shifted to better-situated systems. The Coalition never adopted Primus as its homeland but held it for its symbolism — and because the Sector's largest religion, the [[Pentarchy]], considers **Pacem** to be the holy land.

In the aftermath of the Savage Wars, under the **Orders of Man**, the Coalition was forced to pay reparations for their non-aggression pact with the Savages (the Alatian Accords). The Republic took Primus — the first settled human system — as a form of reparation, humiliating the Coalition in a way they have never forgotten.

The planet **Veles** continues to be a persistent thorn in the Republic's side, with a large Coalition minority growing increasingly violent.

**Central Star:** Dazhbog — a yellow H-Type main sequence star, midway through its lifecycle  
**Ethnicity & Culture:** Alpha-Republic, Pentarchian, and Coalition (mixed)

| Pentarchian: Naive · Idealistic · Progressive · Kind |

## Government & Society

Primus provides three representatives to the Republic's House of Reason. The space station Rho has **no representation**. The Governor of each settlement takes orders from [[Alexandria]]. On Pacem, a planetary bureaucrat handles daily administration — but in practice, residents turn to the **High Presbyter at the Elysian Cathedral** for true leadership.

## Alpha Terra

![[primus_alpha_terra_main.png]]
![[primus_eidolon_city1.png]]
![[primus_eidolon_city2.png]]

**Population:** 200 million (declining)  
**Capital:** Eidolon City (nickname: *"E-Troit"* or *"E-Town"*)  
**Governor:** Drexus Kalder  
**Industry:** Eidolon core manufacturing; mining and manufacturing of raw materials

Once one of the wealthiest centers of production in the known galaxy — now a shadow of its former self. For humans originally leaving Old Earth, Alpha Terra was a paradise: similar size, climate, and the resources needed for Eidolon travel. Decades of mismanagement, territorial disputes, and the discovery of raw materials in other systems left this settlement struggling to stay relevant.

Today, Alpha Terra doesn't crack the **top five regions** for Eidolon drive production. **[[E-Con]]** — the oldest known megacorp in the Sector and the major producer of Chained Eidolon Reaction Drives — maintains its headquarters in Eidolon City even though the bulk of their profit comes from other Republic systems.

As industry left, **organized crime rushed in**. Alpha Terra is a major hub for illicit smuggling within the Republic. Gangs from [[Cannard Noz System|Ankou]] in the Merovinist Triumvirate often maintain satellite operations in E-Troit as an entryway into Republic trade routes.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Industrial smog (decades of unregulated manufacturing have choked the atmosphere; perpetual haze; air quality ranges from poor to hazardous; filtration systems required); acid rain (corrodes buildings and infrastructure; accelerating the city's decline); temperate climate (mild winters, warm summers; increasingly erratic with sudden storms and temperature fluctuations)
- **Flora:** Rust Vines (metallic-tinted leaves; grow on any surface; draw nutrients from air and rain; cover abandoned buildings); Soot Moss (black spongy moss; absorbs pollutants; harvested for potential medicinal detoxifying properties); Ironbark Trees (thick iron-like bark; resist acid rain; valued for durable wood used in construction)
- **Fauna:** Scrap Hounds (canine-rodent hybrids with metallic fur; scavenge urban wastelands for metal scraps; aggressive when competing for resources); Pollution Wasps (large, highly toxic sting; nest in ruined buildings; feed on Rust Vines and Soot Moss); Ghost Rats (albino nocturnal rodents in dark polluted underbelly; hunted for food by the poorest residents); Neon Spiders (bioluminescent webs creating eerie glowing patterns; feed on Pollution Wasps; generally harmless to humans)

## Rho-324-b ("Rho")

![[primus_rho_main.png]]
![[primus_rho_indoors.png]]

**Population:** 200,000  
**Chief Administrator:** Ariana Kennet  
**Industry:** Hospitality and transportation toward Pacem; trading outpost

Originally established as a corporate center and trading post for the once-flourishing Eidolon drive industry on Alpha Terra. Over the past century, its focus pivoted to sustaining the influx of **Pentarchian tourism toward Pacem**.

Rho is generally peaceful and quiet — but its **Lower Ward** attracts nefarious smugglers seeking access to the Republic's port system. Many of Alpha Terra's wealthier residents choose to live on the Rho station in its exclusive Caelum Lofts.

### Districts
| District | Description |
|---|---|
| **Center Mass** | Commercial and private transportation hub; Pacem tour arrangements; mid-tier lodging; Eidolon-related corporate center |
| **Lower Ward** | Trading markets and old warehouses, many taken over by smugglers for laundering into Republic trade networks |
| **Caelum Lofts** | Luxury apartments and shopping centers exclusively for Alpha Terra's rich and elite |

### Environment
- **Climate:** Advanced artificial climate control; simulates Earth-like weather patterns; periodic malfunctions cause abrupt temperature and humidity shifts; solar flare alerts with protective shields
- **Flora:** Lumina Vines (bioluminescent, natural light source, decorative in luxury areas); Oxygen Ferns (engineered for space; convert CO2 to oxygen; minimal maintenance); Plasteel Orchids (genetically engineered metallic petals; symbol of wealth; found in elite private gardens)
- **Fauna:** Spacer Rats (navigate ventilation systems; feed on waste in Lower Ward); Microgravity Geckos (specialized adhesive feet; cling to any surface; popular pets); Void Hawks (surveillance/maintenance drones gone rogue; nest in obscure station areas); Neon Moths (genetic experiment result; attracted to Lumina Vines; create mesmerizing light displays)

## Pacem

![[primus_pacem_main.png]]
![[primus_pacem_planetside.png]]
![[primus_pacem_sanctus.png]]
![[primus_pacem_cathedral1.png]]
![[primus_pacem_cathedral2.png]]
![[primus_pacem_cathedral3.png]]
![[primus_pacem_cathedral4.png]]

**Population:** 200,000 permanent residents (excluding pilgrims)  
**Capital:** Sanctus  
**Governor:** Ariana Kennet  
**Industry:** Pentarchy tourism and hospitality

**Pacem** is the historical holy land of the [[Pentarchy]] religion and home of the **Elysian Cathedral** in the capital city Sanctus. Billions of pilgrims travel each year from across the Sector to visit this sacred site and pay tribute to the Chained Eidolon Reaction Drive.

According to Pentarchic scripture: the founder of the Chained Eidolon Reaction Drive traveled **without a Navigator** to the same location where the cathedral now stands. Surviving the round trip, he claimed divine intervention had saved his life while binding the Sector together through faster-than-light travel. Following his death, his adherents spread his teachings — and the religion became the fastest-growing in the Sector.

The Pentarchy is now the **official state religion of the Republic** and the largest following in the known galaxy. It is widely viewed as a tolerant and accepting religion compared to most others in history.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Serene climate; mild temperatures; gentle breezes year-round; atmospherically managed; the rare "Celestial Rain" (bioluminescent sky glow) is considered a divine sign by followers; infrequent Sacred Storms are holy events — lightning creates spectacular light shows that believers gather to witness and pray through
- **Flora:** Elysian Lilies (iridescent petals changing color through the day; mild calming effect on those who meditate nearby; grown around the Cathedral); Radiant Foliage (bioluminescent forests; guided night walks are a standard part of spiritual pilgrimage); Verdant Spires (towering trees growing in natural spire shapes; patterns in branches interpreted as holy symbols by Pentarchic scholars)
- **Fauna:** Seraphic Doves (white, sacred birds; considered divine messengers; found around the Cathedral; protected by law; their cooing brings peace); Pilgrim Beetles (iridescent insects that form intricate sand patterns interpreted as divine messages); Guardian Drakes (majestic dragon-like creatures; revered as protectors of the sacred lands; depicted throughout Pentarchic art; gentle and docile despite fearsome appearance)

## Veles

![[primus_veles_main1.png]]
![[primus_veles_main2.png]]
![[primus_nav1.png]]
![[primus_nav2.png]]

**Population:** 1 million (rapidly decreasing)  
**Capital:** Nav  
**Governor:** Evgeni Karvan  
**Industry:** Rare element and mineral mining (Velysium, Potensium, Uranium, Curium)

A tiny **purple planet** — its electric atmosphere is laden with argon gas, creating a purple sky that sits above soil stained with the violet-colored element **velysium**. Rare and often radioactive element mining makes Veles the most **profitable planet per square acre** in Primus.

The majority of inhabitants mine rare metals, but frequently suffer severe or fatal health consequences from radioactivity. Companies take basic precautions but no person is ever fully secure. Competitive salaries draw workers — but automated workers are increasingly replacing them. Laid-off miners lose their health protections and can no longer afford to leave to start over. The death rate rises. Social unrest is growing. Republic Guard soldiers are deployed to stabilize the population.

A large minority of **destitute Coalition ethnic groups** also live on Veles — unable to leave Coalition space when the Republic took over (neither their own money nor their government's debt-laden finances covered the journey). They stayed and were pushed into the worst, most dangerous jobs. Derogatorily called **"Vell Heads"** — though they are recognized as Republic citizens and have planetary representation. Surprisingly, they have a **lower mortality rate** and significantly **higher radiation tolerance** than their Republic counterparts.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Argon Storms (electrically charged; intense purple lightning; structural damage; mining shutdowns; surreal violet and magenta sky); Radiant Fog (velysium-laced glowing purple fog; hazardous to breathe without protection; most common in mornings and evenings)
- **Flora:** Velysium Vines (creeping plants absorbing velysium from soil; faint purple glow; highly radiation resistant; harvested for velysium content despite danger); Radflora (bioluminescent neon-colored flowers; thrive in radioactive soil; absorb small amounts of radiation; highly toxic to humans; require careful handling)
- **Fauna:** Glofishers (subterranean insect-like creatures with bioluminescent exoskeletons; burrow through velysium-rich soil; can destabilize ground and cause cave-ins); Aether Serpents (large; radiation-resistant; hunt via bioelectric signals; scales infused with velysium giving a shimmering purple appearance; occasionally prey on straying miners); Spectral Arachnids (large cave-dwelling spiders with bioluminescent purple webs; highly venomous; venom studied for medicinal properties despite harvesting danger)
