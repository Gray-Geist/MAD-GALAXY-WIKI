# Asphodel System

**Section:** Edge (Grave Region)  
**Location:** Siren Cluster  
**Status:** Pirate colony; one of the most dangerous areas of known space

![[asphodel_intro.png]]

---

## Overview

**Asphodel** is a pirate colony in the Grave Region — founded on idealism, quickly consumed by brutality, and now ruled by violent warlords. Its coordinates are not widely known. The journey there is exceedingly dangerous.

**Central Star:** Octaris — an orange L-Type main sequence star, midway through its lifecycle  
**Ethnicity & Culture:** Libertan

## History

Forty years ago, a young pirate group called the **Starry Corsairs** formed in Bered Town on [[Cannard Noz System|Ankou]], deep in Merovinist space. Their leader, **Renan Mezec** — a disgruntled cousin to the Bosonid ruling family — attempted a coup that lasted nearly a year. Mezec and his crew of anarchists and class discontents waged a guerrilla campaign on Bered Town. In response, the Bosonids recruited the Tonton Macoute to spread terror. Mezec was eventually forced out — but before he fled, he **stole Eidolon coordinates** leading through the Cosmic Strands to a newly discovered nebula called the **Siren Cluster**.

There, the Starry Corsairs founded **Asphodel** — a pirate colony predicated on complete freedom, where the common man holds power from tyrannical elites.

Renan Mezec was killed several years after establishing the system. Dangerous gangs overran Libertatia, murdered him, and seized the territory.

---

## Government

Libertatia began as a **direct democracy** — leadership, rules, resources, plunder, and bounties all voted on by constituents. Zero government interference. Zero regulations. Everything permitted.

Now, a group of rival warlords called the **Forsaken Moons** controls Asphodel.

What worries Sector-political theorists: the reorganization under **Queen Marth** could enable long clandestine guerrilla campaigns destroying settlements from within. What truly scares the star nations is the possibility they might try to **legitimize themselves on the intra-Sector stage**.

## Libertatia

![[asphodel_libertatia.png]]
![[asphodel_lafitteville.png]]

**Population:** ~3,000,000 (quickly rising)  
**Capital:** Lafitteville  
**Industry:** Illicit drug and chemical production and trade; human trafficking; chattel slavery; smuggling; racketeering; murder for hire  
**Chieftainess:** Xyla Marth — "Queen Marth"

In Lafitteville, anything goes and the strongest survive. What was imagined as a pirate utopia devolved into tyranny, brutality, and debauchery.

**Xyla Marth** — clever and elusive; chess-like strategies; unconventional methods of execution; general lack of conscience. Like a cat, Marth prefers to play with her food — setting elaborate traps because she enjoys the hunt. The act of killing alone is too easy for her. Under her notoriously violent leadership, the rival warlords have reached a period of mutual cooperation.

### Why Libertatia Is Nearly Impossible to Destroy

- Coordinates are not widely known; journey there is exceedingly dangerous
- Access requires passing through a **dense asteroid belt** — nearly impossible for capital ships
- **"The Teeth"** — heavily militarized planetary defenses; entire legions would fall before Libertatia felt a dent
- Classic porcupine defense that gives any attacker severe hesitancy

Despite a small population, the gangs generate extraordinary black market wealth — one of the **wealthiest settlements per capita in the Sector**.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Mild climate; frequent gentle rains; perpetual twilight due to slow rotation; dense mist and fog; occasional auroras
- **Flora:** Dream Willows (silvery leaves, calming fragrance); Mist Lilies (bioluminescent); Elysium Moss (soft carpet-like ground cover)
- **Fauna:** Phantom Stags (deer-like, glowing antlers); Twilight Owls (shimmering feathers, silent hunters); Mist Serpents (translucent, blend into fog, hypnotic gaze)
