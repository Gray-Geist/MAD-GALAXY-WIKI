# Corot Proximity

**Section:** Edge  
**Location:** Eriksen Verge  
**Nation:** Coalition (loosely)  
**Status:** Frontier system — semi-autonomous, Gostic-settled

---

## Overview

The **Corot Proximity** embodies the character of the Edge: disjointed law, opportunity for those seeking fresh starts or riches, and minimal governmental control. Generations ago it was colonized by settlers and advocates from [[Baadensenar System|Helgoth]] in the Baadensenar system — people looking to escape, expand, or exploit untapped resources.

The Coalition's influence is minimal but expanding, and is the strongest presence compared to other governments. This limited control has made it a haven for adventurers, outlaws, and entrepreneurs.

**Central Star:** Corot — a medium-sized white dwarf providing stable energy; will gradually cool over billions of years and eventually form a "black dwarf"  
**Ethnicity & Culture:** Gostic

| Common Gostic Stereotypes (Frontier) |
|---|
| Rugged/Hardy · Brusque · Resourceful · Pale · Independent |

---

## Government & Society

Several colony planets, each with its own local governance, loosely tied back to Helgoth. The Coalition is primarily represented by trade representatives and military outposts. Each planet operates semi-autonomously under appointed **Stewards**. No representation in the Coalition Chamber.

Society is a mix of rigid Helgothian traditions and the liberal attitudes common to frontier systems. The inhabitants are primarily Gostic — with a cultural belief in their superiority over baseline humans — and adhere to a strict code of honor that values physical and mental prowess.

---

## Main Industries
- Mining: Rich in rare minerals and ores
- Agriculture: Hardy crops and livestock adapted to harsh frontier conditions
- Trade: Hub between the Edge and Mid-Core worlds
- Manufacturing: Machinery and tools for local use and export

## Vinnusemi

![[corot_vinnusemi.png]]

**Population:** 760,000 (steadily growing)  
**Capital:** New Valeria  
**Steward:** Aric Veran  
**Industry:** Agriculture *(from Icelandic: "Hard Work")*

The first planet colonized in the system and its most developed — the administrative center and breadbasket. Rich mineral resources in fertile lands support the largest population and pump the blood of life through the Corot Proximity.

### Environment
- **Fauna:** The *Terraquill* — docile, enormous bird-like creature with iridescent feathers; used by settlers as pack animals. The *Viperflora* — carnivorous plant species with venomous tendrils lurking in dense forests
- **Weather:** Seasonal *Thunderquakes* bring torrential rain and fierce lightning; widespread crop and settlement damage

## Montalba

![[corot_montalba1.png]]
![[corot_montalba2.png]]
![[corot_castellan.png]]

**Population:** 150,000 (very slowly growing)  
**Capital:** Castellan  
**Steward:** Elara Vin  
**Industry:** Manufacturing *(Catalan: "White Mountain")*

Known for rugged, mountainous terrain. Descended from a guild of crafters, Montalba has a strong tradition of manufacturing. Castellan is a fortified mountain city renowned for skilled artisans and engineers. The harsh environment has fostered deep self-reliance and innovation.

### Environment
- **Fauna:** The *Gravihorn* — massive, bull-like creatures with rock-like skin that scale vertical cliffs; peaceful except during mating season. The *Shadeshroom* — bioluminescent fungi providing light in deep forest twilight, but emitting hallucinogenic spores
- **Weather:** *Cryostorms* — blizzards with ice-shard winds that can strip flesh from bone

## Járnbraut

![[corot_jarnbraut1.png]]
![[corot_ferrum.png]]

**Population:** 75,000 (slowly declining)  
**Capital:** Ferrum  
**Chamber Regent:** Brynn Serran  
**Industry:** Resource extraction; high-tech manufacturing *(Old Norse: "Iron Path")*

Extreme conditions: vast iron-rich deserts and volatile weather. Rich in rare metals and high-tech materials. Ferrum is a heavily industrialized city specializing in extraction and processing. The population consists of resilient individuals adapted to harsh conditions — a critical supplier of high-tech components.

### Environment
- **Fauna:** The *Metallicus* — reptilian creatures with metallic scales that conduct electricity; deadly encounters common. *Ironroots* — plants with metal-infused bark; release toxic fumes when cut or burned
- **Weather:** Sandstorms with electrically charged winds that short out unshielded electronics and cause severe burns
