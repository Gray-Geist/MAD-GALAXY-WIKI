# Alatyr System

**Section:** Core  
**Location:** Rose Sea  
**Status:** Devastated — Sot Prime irradiated, Silo Station a ruin, two surviving settlements on moons

![[alatyr_sot_prime_today.png]]

---

## Overview

In its former glory, the **Alatyr System** was the keystone of the Coalition — a paragon of economic excellence, progressive intellectualism, and cultural richness. **Sot Prime**, resource-rich and temperate, was the Coalition's first megacity and the financial and cultural center of the known galaxy. The **Epsilon-739-D / Silo Station** was the largest human space station ever built.

Today, Alatyr is a husk. Sot Prime is an irradiated wasteland. Silo Station is a floating ruin. Two small and declining settlements struggle on moons of a ringed gas giant.

**Central Star:** Xihe — a yellow F-Type main sequence star in the beginning of its lifecycle

---

## The Hounding

A Coalition cruiser unknowingly made contact with a Savage recon force. The Savages secretly followed it home. Over the next year they infiltrated population centers, inserted doppelganger agents into vital positions, and released a debilitating virus — all while the agents struck key nodes of security and power. **399 days** after first contact, the Savages emerged from the dark:

- **Silo Station** was destroyed — all 100,000 aboard killed within an hour
- **Sot Prime** went dark

The Combined Forces advanced to retake the system and swept aside Savage forces who seemed to melt away — until they struck in force at Sot Prime, annihilating the Combined Forces in what became known as the **Disaster at Sot Prime**.

Faced with annihilation, the Coalition signed the **Alatian Accords** — a non-aggression ultimatum that gave them safe retreat in exchange for permanent, forced neutrality. Alatyr became Savage territory.

---

## The Requite Dilemma

For nearly a century the Savages razed Alatyr. Then the **Astral Alliance** arrived — but could not break the stalemate on Sot Prime. Commander **Tyrus Martell** of the VTAP made an impossible decision: he unleashed a **Chained Nuclear Trigger Ordinance (CNTO)** on the planet, dealing a devastating blow to the Savage Armada and **glassing Sot Prime entirely** — poisoned with radiation in perpetuity.

> *"There was no humanity left on that planet, just the illusion of it. If liberated, no human slave there would have made it off that planet alive."* — Tyrus Martell

A special forces element had attempted a humanitarian rescue before the CNTO. It failed.

## Sot Prime

![[alatyr_sot_prime_then.png]]
![[alatyr_sot_prime_today.png]]
![[alatyr_mons_principa.png]]

**Population:** 0 (formerly **10 billion**)  
**Former Capital:** Mons Principa  
**Status:** Irradiated wasteland

Once the wealthiest planet in the known galaxy and the Coalition's cultural and financial heart. Now a radioactive hellscape poisoned by ionized radioactive isotopes from the CNTO. No group has successfully returned — though certain corporations remain obsessed with trying to access its buried riches.

### Weather
Severe radiation storms blanket the landscape with deadly energy. Toxic gases and radioactive dust erode all exposed materials. Acid rain is common.

### Flora
- **Irradiated Fungus** — bioluminescent; absorbs radioactive isotopes as energy; spreads rapidly, consuming structures
- **Blight Moss** — radiation-resistant, fast-growing; emits toxic gas when disturbed
- **Crystal Spikes** — crystalline formations growing from irradiated soil; razor-sharp; grow rapidly

### Fauna
- **Rad-Beasts** — insect-reptile hybrids; multi-limbed; radiation-resistant glowing scales; aggressive; reproduce by laying eggs in prey carcasses
- **Glarehounds** — bioluminescent-eyed pack predators; disorient prey with blinding glare
- **Screecher Bats** — nocturnal; emit high-pitched screeches causing disorientation; feed on glowing fungi
- **Radiant Eels** — aquatic; generate electricity; emit sickly green glow; can briefly emerge onto land to hunt

## Epsilon-739-D (Silo Station)

![[alatyr_silo_station_then.png]]
![[alatyr_silo_station_now.png]]

**Population:** 0 (formerly **100,000**)  
**Status:** Floating ruin — attracts scavengers and treasure hunters

Before Alexandria, the largest human space station. A commercial trading post of extraordinary scale. Today it is devoid of life, its remaining structure bombarded by debris storms, flooded with cosmic radiation, and prowled by adapted creatures.

Legends persist that vaults deep within hold valuables beyond imagining. Most dismiss this as dangerous myth.

### Environment
- **Cosmic Debris Storms** — constant micro-meteoroid bombardment, tearing through remaining structure
- **Radiation Surges** — periodic deadly hotspots sweep corridors

### Flora
- **Vacuum Vines** — bioluminescent; roots penetrate metal; photosynthesize scant sunlight; absorb radiation
- **Frost Lichen** — pale blue; draws moisture from cracked pipes; glows faintly in the dark

### Fauna
- **Sentinel Drones** — repurposed security bots with rudimentary AI; maintain fortified zones; patrol and defend territory
- **Star Wasps** — insectoid; hardened exoskeletons; build nests from debris; hunt in aggressive swarms; paralyzing venom
- **Metal Mites** — tiny spider-bots; rogue AI-directed; scavenge and repair; dangerous in swarms when threatened
- **Specter Fish** — eel-like bio-experiments; inhabit remaining water tanks; blind; rely on electroreception
- **Plasma Leeches** — feed on residual power sources; bioluminescent; deliver painful shock if handled

## Solus

![[alatyr_solus.png]]
![[alatyr_derelicta.png]]

**Population:** 50,000 (slowly declining)  
**Capital:** Derelicta  
**Major Industry:** Port for criminals, smugglers, and Sot Prime research charters  
**Chamber Regent:** Aran Vorr

Governor **Aran Vorr** — whose great-grandfather governed Sot Prime during the Disaster — is possessed by thoughts of vengeance. Young, intelligent, and dangerously bitter, Vorr despises High Regent Pieters of Lux Aureum as an apologist and sympathizer. He declared support for **the Autarch** alongside High Regent Atix.

Vorr has little time for actual administration. Solus survives on smuggler bribes and the docking fees of private research charters who want access to Sot Prime — and appreciate that Vorr asks no questions.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Mild climate with occasional solar flares creating auroras; flares disrupt electronics and cause temporary blackouts
- **Flora:** Solar Blossoms — open during flares to absorb energy; bioluminescent at night
- **Fauna:** Solar Drakes — large reptilian creatures with light-reflecting scales; emit blinding flash to deter predators; Sun Beetles — pollinators feeding on Solar Blossom nectar

## Denfert

![[alatyr_denfert.png]]
![[alatyr_infernal_ward.png]]

**Population:** 200,000 (slowly declining)  
**Capital:** Infernal Ward  
**Major Industry:** Gas mining (Guixu) · Debris recycling from Silo Station  
**High Regent:** Kaori Atix

High Regent **Kaori Atix** — historically stoic, immensely popular, and revered throughout Coalition society as a noble patriot — shocked the Sector when he declared his support for **the Autarch** against his longtime friend High Regent Pieters. Denfert is a functional port settlement built around gas refining stations and repurposing plants.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Frequent thunderstorms with intense, unpredictable lightning
- **Flora:** Thunder Trees — metallic bark conducts electricity; absorb energy from lightning strikes
- **Fauna:** Storm Eagles — large avian predators generating electrical discharges; Spark Lizards — channel electricity through their bodies as defense

## Guixu

![[alatyr_guixu.png]]

Ringed gas giant. Primary fuel source for Alatyr's surviving settlements. Gas mining operations run out of Denfert's orbital platforms. The rings are navigational hazards but also cover for smugglers and scavengers moving between Solus and the ruins of Silo Station.
