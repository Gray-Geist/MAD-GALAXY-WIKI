# Paradis System

**Section:** Edge  
**Location:** Eriksen Verge  
**Nation:** Coalition (Autarch-controlled; not yet officially recognized)  
**Status:** Secret — restricted to Autarch loyalists; rapidly developing

---

## Overview

**Paradis** is one of the newest colonies in Coalition space — and not yet officially recognized by the Coalition confederacy. It is instead a **personal project** led and financed by the [[Baadensenar System|Autarch]], who sees this system as the Coalition's biggest chance to reclaim its former glory.

What separates Paradis from every other system: it boasts **two perfectly habitable planets** containing more resources and Lothranium than both Sot Prime and Gaesmere **combined**.

Settlement is currently restricted to those loyal to the Autarch. He is attempting to keep it off [[Arkadien System|High Regent Pieters']] radar while rapidly colonizing it. Eidolon coordinates are provided only through his office. Several Autarch-aligned systems have received clearance to begin settling here.

**Rumored:** Black operations from major megacorps have been tied to Paradis, and some of these clandestine operations have resulted in proxy battles.

**Central Star:** Cetan *(Lakota: the hawk spirit — regarded for speed, perseverance, and vision)* — a medium-sized orange G-Type main sequence star, halfway through its lifecycle  
**Ethnicity & Culture:** New Gostic

## Government & Society

Each planet is governed by an **Elitekommandant** — hand-selected by the Autarch — tasked with managing, growing, sustaining, and researching the colony. Elitekommandants are officers trained at a prestigious military academy on Helgoth in a specialized program covering not only military strategy but environmental biology, engineering, agriculture, and colonial sciences.

Despite restrictions on settlement, populations are **rapidly booming** due to a massive rise in families having young children. Ethnic Helgoths have experienced a notable rise in fertility rates upon arriving on Paradis.

## Niagara

![[paradis_niagara.png]]

**Population:** 60,000 (growing rapidly)  
**Capital:** Promontory Hill  
**Elitekommandant:** Kasper Thorne  
**Industry:** Planetary settlement and research

A verdant and rainy planet filled with large trees, fresh water, and mineral-rich mountains. Settlers arriving from harsh or dangerous worlds are often overwhelmed by its simple and pure natural beauty. It is a peaceful land with a wide range of indigenous animals and unique flora never before seen.

In many ways, Niagara is **too pure to industrialize** — but the lakes of liquid Lothranium buried in its mantle are too valuable to ignore.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Temperate with distinct seasons; frequent rain and snow; stunning foliage transitions in spring and autumn
- **Flora:** Cascade Vines (colorful flowers, sweet nectar); Whispering Willows (long drooping branches, graceful in the wind)
- **Fauna:** River Otters (playful aquatic mammals); Snow Foxes (thick fur, agile predators in cold regions); Aether Eagles (large birds with iridescent feathers, soar high above)

## Winthrop

![[paradis_winthrop1.png]]
![[paradis_winthrop2.png]]
![[paradis_providentia.png]]

**Population:** 150,000 (growing rapidly)  
**Capital:** Providentia  
**Elitekommandant:** Gunter Vormelk  
**Industry:** Planetary settlement and research *(named after John Winthrop, colonial governor who called his colony "a city on a hill")*

Similar to Niagara in beauty but less rainy — a warm, semi-tropical paradise. Palm-fringed trees line tranquil coastlines. It is almost so peaceful that it's a surprise work gets done at all.

The Autarch's pride and joy. He believes Winthrop will be the planet that rescues the Coalition and enables its rise. **Winthrop is larger and more resource-filled than any other planet in Coalition space.** A massive rise in industrialism looms — the Autarch has promised to strike a balance, developing highly precise mining tools that extract resources without razing the landscape. He ultimately wants Winthrop to also generate income through **tourism**, and is actively working to hold back megacorporation lobbyists.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Warm tropical climate; high humidity; frequent rainfall; monsoon seasons with significant flooding potential
- **Flora:** Rain Orchids (vibrant colors, attract pollinators); Canopy Ferns (large, umbrella-like, upper rainforest layer)
- **Fauna:** Jungle Cats (large solitary feline predators); Parrot Lizards (small colorful reptiles that mimic bird calls); Glow Frogs (bioluminescent, light up the rainforest at night)
