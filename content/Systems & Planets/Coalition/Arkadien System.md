# Arkadien System

**Section:** Core  
**Location:** Rose Sea  
**Status:** Coalition core system — struggling, declining, but fighting for survival

![[arkadien_lux_aureum.png]]

---

## Overview

During the **Exploration period**, the Coalition pioneered humanity's journey into the cosmos — and Arkadien was one of the three original systems colonized alongside Primus and [[Alatyr System|Alatyr]]. Arkadien was meant to be the Coalition's industrial backbone. The Savage Wars stripped it of resources and people. The Republic took Primus as reparations. What remains fights for its existential survival.

**Central Star:** Volksonne — a small red M-Type main sequence star, halfway through its lifecycle  
**Ethnicity & Culture:** Arkadian

| Common Arkadian Stereotypes |
|---|
| Jealous · Bitter · Proud · Downtrodden |

## Government & Society

The Coalition has been a **confederacy** since its inception — a governing body called the **Coalition Chamber** made up of principal representatives (called **High Regents**) from each sector of space. The original founders agreed the Coalition would have no permanent capital, instead relocating the Chamber to a different settlement every five years.

Today this tradition persists in Arkadien — but faces significant pushback from Confederate systems increasingly centralizing under a leader called **the Autarch**, despite the governor of Lux Aureum's efforts to resist this trend.

---

## Main Industries

Arkadien devotes itself to industry, material recycling, energy production, and protogenetic agriculture. With most resources stripped by the Savages and the land poisoned by manufacturing, Arkadien had to entirely reposition itself to survive.

## Lux Aureum

![[arkadien_lux_aureum.png]]
![[arkadien_sieghall.png]]

**Population:** 400 billion (steadily declining)  
**Capital:** Sieghall  
**High Regent:** Maximillien Pieters  
**Major Industries:** Material recycling & repurposing · Metal refining · Protogenetic agriculture

Despite its name — "golden light" — Lux Aureum was never the shining beacon its founders hoped for. It was the Coalition's industrial backbone before the Savage Wars transformed it into something far lesser. Now it is a **shadow of itself**, fighting for existential survival while remaining the de facto cultural epicenter of the Coalition.

**High Regent Pieters** — honorable, proud, popular, and related to the planet's original founders — leads the struggle to hold the Coalition together against the centrifugal pull of the Autarch. His opposition demands fresh ideas and a new order.

With infertile soils and a massive population to feed, Arkadien has become dependent on **protogenetic agriculture** — food artificially created in subterranean facilities (colloquially: **"scop"**).

### Environment
- **Weather:** Frequent solar flares and dust storms; dry and dusty atmosphere; occasional rainstorms; intense heat waves
- **Flora:** Dustbloom plants (drought-resistant, bloom after rain); Solar Lotus (highly efficient solar energy harvesting)
- **Fauna:** Flare Lizards (solar energy storage); Sandstalkers (desert predator insects); Mirage Beasts (large gargoyle-like creatures using mirages to confuse predators and prey)

## Umbreich

![[arkadien_umbreich1.png]]
![[arkadien_umbreich2.png]]

**Population:** 20 million (slowly declining)  
**Capital:** Myrkur Tor  
**Chamber Regent:** Ludo Gresh  
**Major Industries:** Military training grounds · Naval ship manufacturing · Former Savage base (Death's Doorstep)

Lux Aureum's moon. Umbreich maintains Arkadien's defenses and hosts the Coalition's **largest military force**. Governor Gresh has converted the entire economy to war production — all resources find a purpose, and though the engineering may not look pretty, it can still get the job done.

### Death's Doorstep

![[arkadien_myrkur_tor.png]]
![[arkadien_deaths_doorstep.png]]

The former Savage base and command center — located just south of Myrkur Tor, **impossible to miss**. From here, the Savages ruled Arkadien and broke the will of millions during the occupation. The fortress looms over the capital as a permanent reminder of Savage dominance.

- People have tried destroying or demolishing it. It never takes damage.
- Many believe it can **repair itself**.
- Most citizens distrust it and stay away.
- The Coalition **secretly attempts to harness** the occult Savage technology within.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Harsh, cold climate; frequent blizzards; significant temperature swings; periods of "polar night" (no sunrise for weeks)
- **Flora:** Ice Ferns (antifreeze sap); Glacier Moss (grows on ice, absorbs scarce sunlight)
- **Fauna:** Frost Wolves (pack hunters); Glacial Hares (burrowing mammals); Snow Phantoms (translucent jellyfish-like air drifters, harmless); **Bleak Phantoms** (similar but will attack and feed on humans — dangerous)

## Gaesmere

![[arkadien_gaesmere.png]]

**Population:** 30 million (slowly declining)  
**Capital:** Donarbor  
**Chamber Regent:** Xion Holst  
**Major Industries:** Mineral mining · Lothranium mining & refining · Planet core extraction

At the end of the Savage Wars, the Coalition Chamber agreed to **sacrifice Gaesmere** for the greater good. What followed was a 200-year campaign to completely strip the planet of all valuable resources — including its planetary core.

Lothranium deep beneath its mantle has proven invaluable. But demand for energy forced extraction deeper and deeper. In a few more decades, Gaesmere will have nothing left — collapsing into a large barren rock. The Coalition has bet that by then they will have acquired enough fuel to launch one more extensive search for a resource-rich system to save them from existential collapse.

Until then, Gaesmere works itself to death.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Dusty, windy, with frequent dust storms; geological instability from core extraction; sudden earthquakes, fissures, and sinkholes
- **Flora:** Core Vines (grow in deep crevices, tap into geothermal energy); Ash Lichens (absorb minerals from air, cover rocks)
- **Fauna:** Rock Burrowers (mineral-eating, resilient); Dust Bats (echolocating, dust-adapted); Cave Leviathans (enormous, deep underground, feed on geothermal energy)

## Petran

![[arkadien_petran.png]]
![[arkadien_petran2.png]]
![[arkadien_gelidarcis.png]]

**Population:** 50 million  
**Capital:** Gelidarcis  
**Chamber Regent:** Olivanna Pieters  
**Major Industries:** Ice mining & exportation · Hydrogen & carbon refining · Biochemical research

The outermost planet of the Arkadien system — a frozen world orbiting far from the dim red star Volksonne. Ice cutters harvest the massive polar freshwater oceans and transport water throughout the system.

During the Savage Wars, a contingent of Coalition intellectuals relocated to Gelidarcis and built a resistance through technological subterfuge and sabotage. That intellectual tradition persists today.

**Regent Olivanna Pieters** — cousin of High Regent Maximillien Pieters — governs this small planet and has fostered a community of researchers serving the Coalition in ways beyond war.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Extreme cold; constant snowfall; severe blizzards; powerful ice storms lasting days; spectacular aurora displays
- **Flora:** Icicle Trees (crystalline, centuries-slow growth); Frost Flowers (bioluminescent, survive beneath snow); Glacier Moss (on glacier surfaces, rare food source)
- **Fauna:** Ice Mantises (razor-limbed, camouflaged predatory insects); Cryo-Beasts (massive, slow-moving, heat-generating fur); Frostbiters (burrowing small mammals); Aurora Foxes (nocturnal; color-shifting fur matches auroras)
