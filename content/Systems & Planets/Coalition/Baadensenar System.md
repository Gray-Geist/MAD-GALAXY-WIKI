# Baadensenar System

**Section:** Mid-Core  
**Location:** Copernican Maze  
**Nation:** Coalition  
**Status:** Rising — home of the Autarch; militarized and expansionist

---

## Overview

One of the Coalition's newer developed systems, Baadensenar was a desperate gamble by the Coalition government and [[Neus Kapitel Group (NKG)]] to find valuable resources that could restart the economy after the Requite Dilemma. Hundreds of millions of refugees were siphoned from Sot Prime to a planet previously deemed uninhabitable — a world of hazardous dust storms, lightning, and brutal conditions. They named it **Helgoth**, after a terrifying underworld of torture and death.

The gamble paid off — not as planned, but in a way no one anticipated.

**Central Star:** Fenix Omega — a medium-sized orange G-Type main sequence star, halfway through its lifecycle

## The Helgost Mutation

Over approximately three generations, humans on Helgoth began to change. The planet's gravity, radiation, and microbiology altered them at a fundamental level:

- **Lost hair and skin pigmentation** (pale appearance)
- **Increased radiation resistance**
- **Stronger musculo-skeletal connective tissue**
- **Highly oxygenated blood** flowing more efficiently throughout the body
- **Physical performance** exceeding baseline humans in speed, jump height, and endurance
- **Significant loss of muscle tone** (a major drawback)

To compensate, genetic research firms and pharmaceutical companies — especially [[BioMega]] — developed steroids and high-protein supplements. These chemicals had notable side effects: **increased aggression, insomnia, and in some cases infertility**. This has given pharmaceutical megacorps an outsized role in Helgost society.

The Autarch considers the Helgosts to be a new race of humans — superior, distinct, and destined for a different future.

## Government & Society

**High Regent / The Autarch:** Arden Laurent — *"the Autarch"*

Officially the High Regent of Baadensenar in the Coalition Chamber, Laurent has built a political alliance of High Regents and governors around a shared vision: catapult the Coalition back to Sector-wide prominence, disrupt the Republic's hegemony, and break free from the old-guard traditions. Laurent argues that the genetic mutations on Helgoth have created a new human race that needs a new order — one he intends to build.

His transformation of Baadensenar's economy has made him one of the most popular Coalition leaders — second only to High Regent Pieters on Lux Aureum.

## Main Industries

Baadensenar's economy is entirely oriented toward **military industrialism** — creating a large, powerful force for territorial expansion. [[Neus Kapitel Group (NKG)]] and [[BioMega]] have major presences here, with top-secret projects in genetic engineering, extreme resource extraction, and survival on harsh climates.

Rumored Autarch goal: successful pioneer mining expeditions onto irradiated **Sot Prime**.

## Altair

![[baadensenar_altair.png]]

**Population:** [Redacted] (steadily growing)  
**Capital:** Falcon's Nest  
**Chamber Regent:** Laera Ilys  
**Industry:** Military outpost; Sector travel screening and checkpoint

A small hot planet near Fenix Omega. Altair houses the majority of Helgost forces and serves as a mandatory processing depot for all persons traveling in or out of the system. **All ships that do not register at Falcon's Nest are immediately marked for destruction by Falkcast cruisers.**

### Environment
- **Weather:** Frequent thunderstorms and heavy rainfall; humid and tropical
- **Flora:** Dense jungle; Storm Trees (withstand strong winds, extensive root systems)
- **Fauna:** Thunder Birds (large avian, powerful wings and sharp talons); Rain Frogs (breed in abundant water sources)

## Helgoth

![[baadensenar_helgoth1.png]]
![[baadensenar_helgoth2.png]]
![[baadensenar_nagrindr.png]]
![[baadensenar_nagrindr2.png]]

**Population:** 3 billion (steadily growing)  
**Capital:** Nagrindr *(from Norse mythology: a great fence of corpses surrounding hell)*  
**High Regent:** Arden Laurent (the Autarch)  
**Industry:** Military industrialism; metal mining, refining, & manufacturing; genetic research

Home of the Autarch. Depending on who you ask, Helgoth is either the Coalition's last hope and center of progress, or home to a domestic pariah who should be arrested and removed. Through economic results alone, Laurent has won over a majority — he is now one of the most popular leaders in Coalition space.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Brutal radiation storms with high winds that can strip flesh from bone; radiation sickness risk; unpredictable; dry atmosphere filled with fine abrasive dust
- **Flora:** Radiant Cacti (absorb radiation, bioluminescent deterrent, deep roots); Graveyard Vines (rapid growth, absorbs nutrients from soil and air); Stone Flowers (tough stone-like petals, rare bloom, dormant spores)
- **Fauna:** Rad-Scorpions (large, tough exoskeleton, burrow to escape storms); Sand Vipers (venomous, heat-sensing, camouflaged); Dust Rats (radiation-adapted, nocturnal, ubiquitous in ruins and metroplexes); Storm Hawks (large predatory birds, ride storm winds, nests on inaccessible peaks)

## Noirhaven

![[baadensenar_noirhaven1.png]]
![[baadensenar_noirhaven2.png]]

**Population:** 6 million (steadily growing)  
**Capital:** Meili Harbor *(Norse: Meili is the goddess of travel)*  
**Chamber Regent:** Drycus Larsen — also **Prime Admiral of the Falkcast**  
**Industry:** Shipbuilding; Lothranium refining

Noirhaven acts as the Helgost Force's primary starship manufacturer and energy refiner. **Meili Harbor** houses one of the largest, if not the largest, shipbuilding centers in the known galaxy. The Autarch has devoted enormous resources here, knowing that the Coalition's future depends on the ability to rapidly travel and settle foreign planets.

[[Neus Kapitel Group (NKG)]] is the primary corporate partner, with several major competitors.

Regent Larsen is the Autarch's right hand and confidant, as well as Prime Admiral of **the Falkcast** — the Autarch's military fleet *(from German "falke" = eagle, combined with "cast" = a group of predatory birds)*.

### Environment
- **Weather:** Perpetual twilight due to thick atmosphere; cool and damp; frequent fog
- **Flora:** Shadow Moss (thrives in low light, spreads rapidly)
- **Fauna:** Night Stalkers (dark fur, excellent night vision, apex predators); Glow Beetles (bioluminescent, attract mates and prey)
